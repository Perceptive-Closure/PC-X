# T13 assumption ablations and counterexamples

Pointwise computing f_M(n) for finitely simulated machine steps does not decide whether some n yields 1. Unbounded evidence and pair quantification can encode halting. In a finite malformed source instance or a time-capped solver branch, an unverified no-conflict response can never certify observed agreement.

The shared [proof packet](../../WORKSTREAMS/exact/CONSTRUCTIVE-PROOFS.md) gives the full dependencies and derivations. The counterexamples record the stated scope and do not change the accepted experimental protocol.
