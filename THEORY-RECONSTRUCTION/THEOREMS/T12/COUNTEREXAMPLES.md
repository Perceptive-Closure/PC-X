# T12 assumption ablations and counterexamples

A single action can have a closing and a stranded successor. Deleting just the stranded outcome creates a closer despite no baseline proper policy. That is support pruning rather than whole-action deletion. A length-m chain of mandatory stages with two alternative actions per stage has 2^m minimal supports.

The shared [proof packet](../../WORKSTREAMS/exact/FINITE-PROOFS.md) gives the full dependencies and derivations. The counterexamples record the stated scope and do not change the accepted experimental protocol.
