# T09 assumption ablations and counterexamples

A constant route needs no address probe. A direct multi-valued probe returns a selected site at its declared price, which changes the quantitative cost model. These cases refute any unqualified exponential claim over all interfaces. At the fully independent parity realization, changing any omitted address bit alone reverses the target.

The shared [proof packet](../../WORKSTREAMS/exact/FINITE-PROOFS.md) gives the full dependencies and derivations. The counterexamples record the stated scope and do not change the accepted experimental protocol.
