# T04 assumption ablations and counterexamples

A two-row domain has answers 00 and 01, distinct target labels, and a single edge {second}. Reading only the first bit certifies neither label. An empty cell has no representative, even though it has no opposite-target pairs. Two duplicate informative probes can both appear in an edge without changing or causally contributing to the target.

The shared [proof packet](../../WORKSTREAMS/exact/FINITE-PROOFS.md) gives the full dependencies and derivations. The counterexamples record the stated scope and do not change the accepted experimental protocol.
