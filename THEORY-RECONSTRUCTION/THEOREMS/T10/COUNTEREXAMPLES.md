# T10 assumption ablations and counterexamples

A serial fragment with just one residual two-mode stage still hides the question whether other fixed-equal modes sum exactly to the residual budget. Its two costs straddle the budget iff a positive SUBSET SUM instance has a witness. Small local width alone gives no polynomial bound in the bit lengths.

The shared [proof packet](../../WORKSTREAMS/exact/FINITE-PROOFS.md) gives the full dependencies and derivations. The counterexamples record the stated scope and do not change the accepted experimental protocol.
