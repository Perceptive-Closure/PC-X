
# T25 · Boundary witnesses

1. **Same complete transcript law, different misses.** Let $Z$ have a warning class of mass $1/4$ and a silent class of mass $3/4$, with no audited labels. Assign all $V=0$ in one completion, and assign $V=M^c$ in another. Both preserve all visible outcomes, and their miss risks are 0 and $3/4$.
2. **Positive alerts are not all true positives.** Keep the same $Z$. One completion labels every alert as a false positive and leaves half of the silent mass positive. Here $a_1=1/4$ but the actual hit mass is zero and violation risk is $3/8$. Both this and any all-negative completion give the same monitor observations.
3. **Zero denominator.** Take $V=0$ surely. The ratio $P(V=1,M=0)/P(V=1)$ has no numerical value. The joint cap inequality is $0\le0$. Forcing the ratio to some observed value would change the target.
4. **Correlated contexts.** Suppose exactly one of two contexts has violation rate one and the other has rate zero. Both contextwise lower bounds are zero, while the worst-context risk is one in every completion. Taking each context's lower envelope at once requires compatibility of the chosen labels.
5. **Unlabelled mass versus fixed support.** An unscored deterministic atom with mass $1/2$ may permit only $V=0$ or $V=1$ if physical immutability forbids splitting it. Claiming that $1/4$ additional risk is then attainable is false. Use an explicit candidate catalogue or declare latent splitting before using the rectangle's attained interior.
