# T06 assumption ablations and counterexamples

With two duplicate sufficient readouts of price 1 and 3, the expensive singleton is inclusion-minimal but not minimum. In three one-hot rows with distinct labels and unit costs, each true row has a realized one-probe certificate while the static optimum is two. An adaptive first negative answer still leaves two rows.

The shared [proof packet](../../WORKSTREAMS/exact/FINITE-PROOFS.md) gives the full dependencies and derivations. The counterexamples record the stated scope and do not change the accepted experimental protocol.
