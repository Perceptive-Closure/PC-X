# T22 · Boundary cases

An initial flag with positive Bernoulli KL followed by immediate stopping gives no probe acquisitions and positive transcript KL. Counting only queries misses the initial information. In a separate example the first bit is fair, a second bit agrees with it with probabilities 3/4 and 1/4 under the competing laws, and only a first 1 triggers sampling the second. The unconditional bit marginals agree. The stopped KL is exactly (1/4) log 3, verified numerically.

The case limits the affected generalization to its stated premises. Full-width experimental applicability follows the independently typed interface. [Proof and model](PROOF.md)
