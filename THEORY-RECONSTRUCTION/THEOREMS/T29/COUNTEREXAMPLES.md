
# T29 · Falsifying examples and algorithm boundaries

1. **Arbitrary finite cost of uniformity.** Two concealed types share a public starting signal. Each has its own licensed zero-cost lane for one completed job, and both have a charged lane costing $K>0$ for the same work. The common optimum is $K$ and each known-type optimum is zero. Deleting the shared route leaves both singleton witnesses and no common policy. A safe refusal never processes the job.
2. **Information consumes time in the plant.** Start with the same two types and offer a licensed audit move at cost $r>0$ that reveals the true lane but completes no work. One tick permits no uniformly winning plan. With two ticks the lawful read and a zero-cost type-matched lane cost exactly $r$. Treating this move as an external read with no time effects erases the one-tick impossibility.
3. **All pairs can win and their union can lose.** Three states admit action pairs $\{b,c\}$, $\{a,c\}$ and $\{a,b\}$, each usable for the same completed move. Any pair has a common action. All three share none. With no readbacks the only minimal obstruction is this triple and its disagreement edge is empty. Pair-only reasoning gives a false readiness verdict. The construction scales by omitting a different action per state.
4. **Chance success is a different objective.** If one available move has an arbitrarily rare positive-support unsafe outcome and the other move guarantees safety with higher cost, the sure-supported optimum cannot use the first move. A small tolerated-risk specification may allow it. Discarding the bad branch changes the objective.
5. **Forged disproof through a collapsed belief.** After a coarse observation two hidden states may both remain compatible, though one leads to a different safe completion. A verifier must reconstruct the entire union of equal-observation successors. Removing a feasible branch can manufacture a winning claim, while inventing an impossible observation can manufacture a losing one.
6. **Pure external queries do not create physical capability.** If all possible completely classified installations have no usable route within the original bound, pure readbacks can justify the optional negative verdict. They cannot produce a successful policy. A grant or change to the available route is a plant intervention under different semantics.
