# T29 · Alignment realizability under semantic uncertainty

**Charter.** Charter §33, pp. 30–31.

**Research question.** Can one observable policy do safe useful work in every compatible completion, and when is further lawful acquisition sufficient to construct it?

**Candidate statement.** T29.a gives the exact finite-horizon least worst-supported-path cost and constructive winning or losing witnesses. T29.b identifies minimum lawful fixed acquisition through unready-cell obstructions and an exact adaptive worst-cost recursion. It allows a separate legitimate uniform intrinsic-failure outcome. **Assumptions.** Finite completion-tagged state and support, finite nonnegative cost common across compatible states per modeled action, finite horizon, truthful observations and exact legality, safety and progress checks. For T29.b, external queries are fixed, pure, legally available and positive-cost. Their answer cells only restrict the initial alternatives, with operational horizon and budget held fixed. Rational encoding supports explicit algorithm analysis.

**Mathematical scope.** The information-state engine proves a sufficient and necessary bounded deployment condition. The higher-order acquisition formulation directly links overlapping success regions to a lawful acquisition oracle. It supplies no polynomial time guarantee for general state, action or query universes.

Its actual external target is profile-vector identity, whereas the new readiness target requires a common successful plant policy for each surviving cell or a uniform infeasibility certificate.
