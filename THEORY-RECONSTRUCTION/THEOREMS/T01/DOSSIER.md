The full labeled ordinary behavior identifies available actions and where they lead. Same observable trajectories can come from different extensional effects on a source atom, so a resource freeze can remove different menus. A missing backup exhibits structural infeasibility. The target-specific freeze makes the generic obstruction concrete. The proof is constructive in $g$ and uses a constant-size graph. The cost gap is controlled by an input cost, and no system-wide frequency or computational hardness follows.
