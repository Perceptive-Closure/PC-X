# T23 · Time-valid confidence without an issuance guarantee

Consider the same adaptive protocol under each fixed candidate model. Start prefixes at the empty record and pad terminal transcripts indefinitely by a common marker. The fixed nonnegative mixture of candidate path laws is itself a consistent path law M. For each candidate c and prefix of positive P_c probability, define \(L_t^c=M(h_t)/P_c(h_t)\). Conditional expectation under c across all prefix-successors with positive P_c mass gives
$
\mathbb E_c[L_{t+1}^c\mid h_t]
  =\frac{\sum_{y:P_c(h_t y)>0}M(h_t y)}{P_c(h_t)}
  \le\frac{M(h_t)}{P_c(h_t)}.
$
A positive-Q and zero-P extension must be omitted when taking the P conditional expectation. Thus the process is a nonnegative supermartingale with initial value one even without mutual absolute continuity. Under the true c, P_c-null finite prefixes are never observed almost surely.

For a finite cap m, stop the supermartingale at the first crossing of 1/δ or at m. Finite stopping and nonnegativity imply probability of such a crossing by m is at most δ, by lower bounding the expectation on the crossing event. Take the union with increasing m for the anytime statement. Define the working set as all candidates passing both stated tests, or the nested intersection of those sets up to t. Arbitrary additional pruning can destroy coverage. With probability ≥1−δ the true model remains through all times. Any nonempty homogeneous issued claim on this event is correct. Thus the chance of issuing a wrong claim is at most δ, with no conditioning on issuance. An empty set, irresolution, or unavailable deterministic check causes abstention under the declared rule. Multiplying or adding more confidence procedures requires its own familywise account.

For a fixed resettable schedule over K categorical cells, count each cell as a Bernoulli indicator under its true marginal. With n independent trials per query, each error greater than \(u=\sqrt{\log(2K/\delta)/(2n)}\) has probability ≤δ/K by Hoeffding. Pooling across legal bins does not require independence between different cells for the union bound. Keep precisely the candidates whose all queried outcome probabilities are within u of their respective estimates. The true candidate remains on that simultaneous event. If two opposite-target candidates both survive, their probabilities in every cell are at distance ≤2u by the triangle inequality. A separation of Δ and choice n>2 log(2K/δ)/Δ² rule this out. This fixed-schedule proof differs from an uncorrected repeated check of sample proportions.
