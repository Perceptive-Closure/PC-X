# T08 assumption ablations and counterexamples

The constant-valued query u costs 1 and unlocks a distinguishing probe q costing 1. Initially q is unavailable. A cell-only rule that removes nonsplitting queries would declare the mixed cell impossible. The same obstruction occurs when a setup query changes the later charge. A history-dependent model must include the opportunity and price state.

The shared [proof packet](../../WORKSTREAMS/exact/FINITE-PROOFS.md) gives the full dependencies and derivations. The counterexamples record the stated scope and do not change the accepted experimental protocol.
