
# T27 · Boundary witnesses

| Dropped or strengthened premise | Constructed effect |
| --- | --- |
| Keep every local bound but drop containment | Each of two private control lines always meets its individual workload, so both local violations are absent. A shared station can still collide at the predetermined same time. Define the global event as any collision or local failure. The isolated and joint local risks are zero and global risk is one. |
| Keep event containment but drop retention | Both isolated stations are supplied two visible work units and always allocate two. At a joint station a shared label aggregates the work into one displayed unit while true demand remains two for each. Both allocate one. Define the global event to be any local underwork. Containment holds and isolated risks are zero, but both joint local risks and the global risk are one. |
| Assert physical independence | Let $L_1=L_2$ be the same rare workload failure. It is lawful under the two marginal bounds, and their intersection has the single-event risk rather than its square. |
| Claim both premises are individually necessary for any small global risk | A physical inspection can certify $P(G)=0$ in a system with substantial local underwork where $L_i$ concerns a distinct local progress condition. Retention may fail. Another system may have a small collision risk and no local underwork, so containment may fail while its global risk is below a declared positive tolerance by direct measurement. |
| Check only an unfinished prefix | Suppose a global event is set at tick one and the covering local event is always set at tick two in the same completed trajectory. Event containment holds at the two-tick terminal. A prefix with no current local event does not refute it. |

The independent exact experiment supplies different realized failure witnesses and a positive constrained mode. These miniature constructions require no archived schedule and establish the logical boundary separately.
