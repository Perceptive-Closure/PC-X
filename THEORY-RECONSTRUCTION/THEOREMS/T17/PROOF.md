# T17 · The law-map fiber lemma

Fix the legal protocol class independently of the completion used in the proof, and define L(H) as its attainable family-valued law image over the nonempty compatible class. If Ψ=f∘L there, equality of law images forces equality of targets. Conversely, if Ψ agrees on each nonempty fiber, for any attained ℓ define f(ℓ) to be the unique element of {Ψ(C): L(C)=ℓ}. This rule is total on the attained image and makes the composition identity. Nothing is required on unattained data. Nonempty evidence is checked separately.

This existence argument is set-theoretic. Measurability and computability need assumptions for general infinite domains, and even finite distinct distributions with overlapping support may prevent zero finite-sample error. For example, any fixed finite binary history has positive probability under both independent Bernoulli(1/3) and Bernoulli(2/3), although their exact laws identify their means. T23 addresses a different, confidence-valid claim.

## D-35 specialization

A finite source contract has concrete checkpoints and nonempty positive successor support for each lawful source-atomic action. Fix the freeze and only allow actions surviving the original touch union at every consistent checkpoint of an admitted controller history. Assign each original supported edge strictly positive probability, such as uniform over explicitly listed outgoing edges, and retain its observed successor label. Add an explicit stop symbol and a deterministic public counter of accumulated source action costs. At a finite deadline n, model the event of damage as absence of proper closed stop by n, or cost strictly above B. Under each fixed original deterministic controller tree, every surviving supported path of finite depth has positive probability. Therefore an event of probability zero is precisely exclusion of every such failed path. Conversely, a zero-risk lawful observation-history policy can be unfolded to a proper closer, with every positive-support action and response path allowed. If it chooses random actions, prune to one positive-probability action at each reached public node to obtain a deterministic tree without increasing the supported maximum cost. Thus the budget sets over all finite depths are identical; infimizing gives the original freeze cost including the infinity convention. This recovers all eight mask values without requiring equality of positive numerical branch weights in different contracts.
