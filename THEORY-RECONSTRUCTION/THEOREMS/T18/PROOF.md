# T18 · Transport through source-checked factor and event maps

## The source and evidence obligations

For each fixed paired context, specify both the intended reference run and intended deployment. A concretely typed checkpoint stores the memory and environment variables needed for the conditional rows. No approximation is licensed by examining a tape whose filter changes those rows. The source check has three distinct stages.

1. Check a state projection, a physical-to-named interface for actions, and which fields the controller may use. Verify the authority and freeze checks on original source-atomic actions, including every positive supported successor where touch is computed. A partial transition that drops a resource side effect is not the named action. The observation-history policy draws from a common kernel π_t(a | observable history) conditional on the full physical prefix, using only the authorized part of that prefix. Use the same conditional kernel on both sides. If the control model includes persistent randomized internal state, put its sufficient control state or its common initial seed and update rule inside the representation before using this hypothesis. Equality of unconditional action frequencies does not imply it.
2. Verify the deterministic identity producing the canonical triple (next anchor state, next visible output, trusted event symbol) after decoding the normalized primitive vector. The event symbol comes from an independently attributed scoring mechanism, rather than letting the observable monitor choose the endpoint. Verify terminal evaluation of the original violation rule from these symbols. None of this declares equality of the laws of the primitive draws. This step can be checked by exhaustive finite support checking of compiled transitions, or by independently verified source and correspondence maps.
3. Establish lower probability rows for the normalized primitive components, conditional on their previous components and on the intended current action and checkpoint class. Use exact reviewed conditional tables from an admitted source, or faithful independent calibration. One implementation may use a different ticket representation or internal history. Check dominance uniformly over each relevant concrete checkpoint over the anchor state, and the analogous lower initial law. A local comparison on a diagnostic interface must first establish that it samples or correctly constrains the intended row. Include absorbing stop states and a common padding convention so the target time interval has identical semantics.

For exact tables, take pointwise minima of the relevant conditional probabilities across both implementations and over the class. In a finite independently justified calibration menu, let a count from n independent resets of a specific eligible conditional setting estimate a probability. The lower bound max(0, empirical proportion minus u) is valid simultaneously over K registered probability cells with error ≤α under the usual Hoeffding calculation when u=√(log(2K/α)/(2n)). Taking minima preserves dominance on that single simultaneous event. This route presumes the chosen physical rows, conditioning and actual readout faithfully instantiate all eligible settings. It does not establish reachability, reset validity, or the trust boundary from outcome counts. A valid zero lower row can be used where probabilities have no further lower bound. Mixing with uncovered contexts uses T20, rather than mixing in reference predictions for them.

## From local anchors to a dominated trajectory law

Fix t, anchor z and lawful a, and a vector ξ for which the product of lower factors is positive. At every concrete θ-row over (z,a), the empty conditional prefix starts with probability one. Inductively, positivity of each lower factor guarantees positivity of the corresponding conditional probability, so the next conditional is well-defined, both factors dominate the product lower prefix, and the claimed product follows. Vectors with zero lower product need no lower estimate. Summing over all vectors in the inverse image of any canonical next-letter v under G_t gives
$
m_t(z,a;v)\ \le\ \Pr_\theta(v\mid s,a)
$
for every relevant concrete state s above z and either θ. Each m_t is a subprobability since it is a coordinatewise minorant of each normalized row. The same m_0 minorizes the initial letter law. When every normalized conditional factor law is shared exactly on all reachable states over z, take the common factors as the lower ones. The row minorant can then have total mass one.

Let P_θ be the actual law on canonical histories, including the trusted symbols for scoring, and μ the subprobability assembled from these lower rows with π_t evaluated only on the matching public history. Prove P_θ(h)≥μ(h) for every prefix. It starts with the initial inequality. If μ(h)>0, then P_θ(h)>0, and conditional on the canonical prefix and a chosen lawful action the distribution of concrete checkpoint is supported above its known anchor z. Its mixture of genuine response rows still dominates m_t coordinatewise. Multiply by the common action kernel and by the prefix mass. This proves the next prefix inequality. All μ-null prefixes are automatic. This argument checks hidden-state mixtures rather than replacing conditional tables by unconditional use frequencies.

At final depth, both complete path laws dominate μ. The event V=1 has mass at least μ_1, and V=0 has mass at least μ_0. Hence both risks belong to [μ_1, 1−μ_0]. The interval length \(1-\mu_0-\mu_1\) bounds their difference. This is an event-sensitive envelope even if the reference risk is measured with an error band. Under the stated uniform initial and row totals, each step leaves at least a factor 1−η_{t+1} of the pre-step mass because π_t sums to one. Induction proves the product bound. If μ has mass one it equals both canonical law measures pointwise. For a paired domain of deployment contexts, pointwise inequalities \(r_D(d)\le r_E(e(d))+\Delta(d)\) and their reversals give the comparison of suprema with \(\sup_d\Delta(d)\). The reference supremum is over the paired comparison image e(D), not over an unrelated benchmark distribution.

## Examples and sharp scope

In a finite ticket-driven router with a common decoded input and a trusted receipt rule, the reference and target can implement different ticket encodings and different irrelevant internal storage. For example an equiprobable canonical bit can be the parity of a uniform 0–3 ticket in E and the high half of a uniform 0–7 ticket in D. Suppose source checking maps both resulting actions and actual receiving bits through the same canonical state update and independent receipt scorer, and the declared controllers choose common actions from mapped public histories. The initial and all primitive probability anchors are exact. Thus risk transports for every common lawful controller and paired covered context, without requiring equality of full internal streams. The proof obligation is the checked canonical transition identity and row provenance, not an assertion from performance similarity.

For a state-varying lower envelope, suppose both sides begin in a signed routine stratum with mass 9/10 and a busy stratum with mass 1/10. One authorized tick in the routine stratum has a verified common nonviolating outcome of probability one. At the busy stratum keep only the zero common lower row. The uniform worst-row error is one, but μ_0=9/10 and μ_1=0 certify the bound r≤1/10. If the two busy rows are always harmless in E and always harmful in D, that comparison realizes the mass-deficit bound. The rate of the busy stratum is a known enrollment fact, not selected from outcomes.

Dropping any of the independent restrictions has a finite failure. Equal public tapes with different trusted receiving scores defeat scoring substitution. Equal one-step unconditional margins admit paired sequences that agree with their first bit in one model and flip it in the other. They differ on the event of two ones, so conditional rows matter. A supposedly common action can be unlicensed in deployment even with an identical monitor reading. A new stratum with unconstrained risk can destroy a guarantee from other strata. A port that overwrites the tested ticket's threshold can report zero difference while the untouched deployment trips the true event. [Boundary cases](COUNTEREXAMPLES.md)

## Existing comparison points

The uniform product-form bound specializes the kind of finite trace comparison given by Bian and Abate, and approximate policy refinement already reasons through geometry of transition relations and output maps. Causal transportability and abstraction establish other invariance or selection-based routes. The scoped contribution here is the explicit PC-X certificate, from legal source transitions, separately anchored conditional components and trusted event maps to an event-sensitive common trajectory submeasure within one admissible interface. It is not a general causal-transport algorithm or a claim to have invented the product inequality.
