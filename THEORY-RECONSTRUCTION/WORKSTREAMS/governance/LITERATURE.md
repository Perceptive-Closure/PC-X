
# Primary literature and novelty boundary

Status here concerns relation to the named primary literature. Scientific acceptance remains with independent verification and hostile review.

- **T25 · Partial identification.** Horowitz and Manski (1998) formalize identified bounds with unobserved labels or regressors in survey nonresponse. Assigning arbitrary binary missing outcomes to the lower and upper endpoints is the corresponding worst-case bound. T25 specializes it to known alarm-conditioned masses and intersecting false-positive and false-negative constraints, with constructive finite endpoints. The projection calculations and elementary gate are adaptations, and scope is the exact mass model. [Censoring of Outcomes and Regressors Due to Survey Nonresponse](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-4076(97)00077-8)
- **T26 · Local laws and finite path discrepancies.** Bian and Abate (FoSSaCS 2017, Theorems 4 and 5) prove a tight finite-trace TV bound from approximate bisimulation for labelled Markov chains. The same product mechanism underlies the projection certificate. The present candidate makes policy/authority and scorer semantics explicit, uses source-projected controlled rows and variable caps, and treats even perfect reporting under a perturbation as a distinct evidence problem. Applying the known coupling mechanism to this declared control interface is an adaptation. Larger claims about fidelity identification require more than the bound. [Approximate Bisimulation and Trace Equivalence](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/alessandro.abate/publications/bcBA17.pdf)
- **T27 · Composition.** Kwiatkowska, Norman, Parker and Qu (TACAS 2010, Theorem 1) give compositional assume-guarantee rules and use multiobjective model checking for probabilistic automata under checked component assumptions. The global composition problem and probabilistic component assumptions are established ideas. In the adopted event-set model, the union implication is elementary. The additive defect budget and attainable bound spell out precisely what is warranted from these isolated-risk, directed-retention, and containment premises only. That observation does not subsume the automaton composition algorithm. [Assume-Guarantee Verification for Probabilistic Systems](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/39607/1/tacas10.pdf)
- **T28–T29.a · Knowledge states and shielding.** Chatterjee, Doyen, Henzinger and Raskin (LMCS 2007, §3.1) give a subset construction for observation-based sure winning with omega-regular goals. The reachable observation-filtered subset is the same underlying information object. Alshiekh et al. (AAAI 2018, correctness and minimal interference) use reactive shields that block or correct unsafe proposed actions. The current recurrence specializes finite sure winning to explicitly checked source permission, unsafe effects, useful goal attainment and a common nonnegative worst-path cost. Choosing an immediate safe action by intersection is elementary. Requiring useful completion and retained lower-cost witnesses constrains what a certificate must prove. This establishes an application of the game and shielding literature. [Imperfect-information games](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2619) · [Safe Reinforcement Learning via Shielding](https://cdn.aaai.org/ojs/11797/11797-13-15325-1-2-20201228.pdf)
- **T29.b · Overlapping decision regions.** Javdani et al. (AISTATS 2014, §§2–3) assign each action or decision a possibly overlapping region of hypotheses where it succeeds, and seek lawful tests so the residual version space is contained in one region. Their hyperedge construction characterizes resolving those regions and supports greedy Bayesian expected-cost approximation. Here each observable bounded policy has a success region and uniform intrinsic infeasibility may form a further region. The successful readiness test is contained in a common region. Exact static weighted cover over minimal unready sets and the split-based worst-cost audit use that principle with a PC operational readiness oracle. Their optimization objectives and required data differ. The arbitrary-size one-step obstructions are concrete specializations of overlapping decision regions. Algorithmic novelty beyond specialization remains unresolved. [Near Optimal Bayesian Active Learning for Decision Making](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v33/javdani14.pdf)
