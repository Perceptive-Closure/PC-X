# Primary related work for the foundation

The novelty categories below are constructor judgments pending independent hostile review. Mathematical novelty differs from experimental correspondence.

| Primary source | Related result and assumptions | Boundary for PC-X |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Blackwell, *Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments* (1953)](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032) | Compares statistical experiments by attainable decision performance and informativeness under specified parameter and decision models. | The general observation-insufficiency idea is established. T01 varies hidden extensional resource effects while all ordinary support trees agree, so the target itself concerns an added resource intervention. The typed finite witness is an adaptation. |
| [Chatterjee, Doyen, Henzinger and Raskin, *Algorithms for Omega-Regular Games with Imperfect Information* (2007)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2619) | The knowledge-based subset construction and fixed-point algorithms handle observation-based winning strategies on graphs. | Belief filtering and the Boolean attractor are established. The finite additive worst-branch cost recursion and explicit removal of full source atoms instantiate them in the PC setting. T03 lifts a matched support relation to beliefs and depends on common observation-based label maps. It makes no general new claim about incomplete-information games. |
| [Fisler, Krishnamurthi, Meyerovich and Tschantz, *Verification and Change-Impact Analysis of Access-Control Policies* (2005)](https://web.cs.wpi.edu/~kfisler/Pubs/icse05.pdf) | Margrave verifies decisions and compares supplied XACML policy variants using decision-diagram representations. | T02 varies the source-consistent admission and certificate intervention while preserving the same declared raw program. It separates evaluating a supplied variant from inferring which resource intervention is supported by independently governing evidence. Margrave's conclusions do not supply this omitted semantics. |
| [Javdani et al., *Near Optimal Bayesian Active Learning for Decision Making* (2014)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v33/javdani14.html) | Acquires information until remaining hypotheses occupy one overlapping decision region and studies efficient acquisition under Bayesian assumptions. | The idea that enough evidence to settle a decision can be weaker than full latent-state recovery is established. D-35 uses a nonprobabilistic represented fiber relative to the certificate and separates it from the controller's observable histories and the investigator's contract class. |
| [Beckers and Halpern, *Abstracting Causal Models* (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.03789) | Studies precise transformations between models relative to domains of interventions and comparison maps. | T03 uses explicit matching of a source-backed support relation and lawfully visible transition labels to preserve the feasible cost of a contingent action tree. T02 uses omissions of those fixed intervention semantics to build two systems with differing freeze values. The generic idea of preserving interventions under abstraction is prior. |
| [OASIS, *XACML 3.0 Core Specification* (2013)](https://docs.oasis-open.org/xacml/3.0/xacml-3.0-core-spec-os-en.html) | Specifies logical request contexts, designated attribute lookup and condition-dependent missing-attribute semantics. | It is authoritative for the native fixture's interface semantics. A fixed partition, authority operator and intervention checkpoint additionally require declared fixture and adapter facts. |

**Verdict.** Treat T01–T03 and P1–P7 as original PC foundation, with known or adapted proof machinery and explicit scope. The strongest supported distinction here is source-relative identification of authorization-resource intervention effects after separating the three information roles. The current review does not establish research priority for that narrower formulation. Broader priority claims across stateful authorization, semantic change analysis and observational abstractions remain unresolved.
