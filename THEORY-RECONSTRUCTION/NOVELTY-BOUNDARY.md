A valid theorem that instantiates one of these mechanisms is useful to this proof architecture regardless of originality. [Primary-source inventory](LITERATURE/PRIMARY-RESULTS.md)

The work supports a scoped positive transport/synthesis direction. It leaves the proposed general scalability/novelty result open. This is a research conclusion for future consideration, with no publication composed here.
