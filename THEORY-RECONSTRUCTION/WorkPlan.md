## Execution and role separation

Up to three research workstreams are active simultaneously, including parent work whenever it performs independent mathematics. The default deployment is two subagents plus the parent. Each research worker receives a dated WorkPlan before dispatch and writes a scoped Path. A constructor seals a statement-only file and the definitions/dependencies, separate from the proof. A distinct verifier gets that file and records a fresh derivation or objection before viewing any proof. A hostile reviewer probes claims, novelty, assumptions and evidence separately. The parent records acceptance and reconciles substantive objections. A founding result can be used conditionally before acceptance when its premises are explicit. Extension claims depend on accepting the precise foundation they use.
