# P3 · Rectangular partial identification

D-35 p. 6 and Appendix F p. 14. **Constructor verdict:** monotone bounds hold over any nonempty set of R-touch masks in the common kernel. Endpoint realizability supplies sharp bounds. Rectangularity is a sufficient way to ensure endpoint realizability.

Let $M_C$ be the blocked state/action locations in completion $C$ under an R freeze. Let $D=\bigcap_C M_C$ and $U=\bigcup_C M_C$. The common kernel fixes the source locations, all other transitions and their costs, observations and outcomes, closure status and ordinary availability. If $B_1\subseteq B_2$ are removal masks, every legal proper policy after deleting $B_2$ is also one after deleting $B_1$, with identical costs and physical support. Infima in the extended reals are therefore nondecreasing under enlarging the removal mask. Thus
$
k_{\neg D}\le k_{\neg M_C}\le k_{\neg U}.
$
Full per-location independence supplies one actual completion for $D$ and one for $U$. More generally the conclusion only needs those two masks represented. They attain the coordinate extrema. Subtracting a common finite unrestricted cost preserves their sharpness for the R penalty. The proof says which bounds are attained, without filling any gaps between discrete marginal values or manufacturing feasible cross-coordinate combinations.

**Correlated mask counterexample.** There are two unit-cost route atoms, and each can close on every supported outcome. Admitting the full identifying history is part of each atom. R changes only at its positive successors if one auxiliary partition block is changed, independently of the terminal fibers. Exactly one route has this R side effect in each of two compatible completions. Hence $D=\varnothing$ and $U$ contains both routes. Every actual frozen-R optimum is $1$, while deleting the possible mask would give $+\infty$. All signatures remain evaluated under the fixed source locations and their full supported successors. X02 measured a strict product overapproximation under these types of correlated anchors. [Evidence](../WORKSTREAMS/foundation/EVIDENCE.md)

If costs, authority-driven ordinary menus, physical support, observation or closure differ across completions, a mask containment by itself no longer embeds the corresponding policy sets into one fixed kernel. The result remains explicitly conditional on that common comparison graph.
