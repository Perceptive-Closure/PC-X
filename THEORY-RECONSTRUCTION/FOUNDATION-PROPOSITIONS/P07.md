# P7 · Target refinement and exact transfer

D-35 p. 7 and Appendix G p. 15. **Constructor verdict:** the proof transfers all policies under the same atomic interfaces and observables, with the stated fiberwise target condition.

Use a fixed complete contract apart from its target map. At every reachable represented fiber $C$ assume
$
\forall x,x'\in C,\quad a_2(x)=a_2(x')\Longrightarrow a_1(x)=a_1(x').
$
If a nonempty fiber is homogeneous for $a_2$, the implication makes it homogeneous for $a_1$. For any freeze, the same policy is therefore a proper $a_1$ closer whenever it is a proper $a_2$ closer. Retain the same stopping decisions and supported physical branches. Every underlying feasibility constraint and cost is unchanged, because the only modified component is the sealed target. Taking infima gives $k_F(a_1)\le k_F(a_2)$ in extended order. No attainment or finite baseline is needed. Bidirectional equivalence of the two fiberwise equality partitions yields equality of the proper-policy sets and of all eight costs.

The premise is local to the reachable represented fibers. It does not require a global refinement between the target label sets. Equal status is enough for the checkpoint-based closure objective and need not preserve the names of closed labels. If target designation also changes what the policy sees, the declared available actions or its intervention surface, the policy-class inclusion must be checked again.
