## Finite proof attacks and surviving assumptions

### T03 and the original D-35 appendix

A zero-cost setup reaches hidden states $u,v$ with the same outcome and next observation. Give their respective physical repair menus $\{L\},\{R\}$ in the first contract and $\{F\},\{F\}$ in the second. Pair their corresponding states, costs, effects and closing successors. The single observation-indexed mapping $L,R\mapsto F$ gives a bijection separately at both paired menus. Its restrictions preserve every physical repair. The first contract's actual observation has no common continuation, while $F$ closes both branches of the second at cost 1. A common injection on the union rules this out. A hidden-state-dependent outcome renaming has the analogous failure when a visible bit distinguishes repair branches in only one side.

D-35 Appendix D uses the existence of matching successors as though all cross-pairs were related, and recursively indexed continuations can depend on hidden branch identity. The constructor's induction instead relates whole information sets at matching public histories. For each observation/action replay the visible action and outcome maps, then use both support directions to cover the successors contributing to that same history. Under union injectivity, legality over a belief is preserved in both directions. Matching leaf status, and closed labels when they enter the stop predicate, preserves stopping. Common prices and whole-atom touch preserve the value under each of the eight deletions. This repairs an argument in the manuscript under the explicit interface reading. The support-to-policy mechanism already has precedents in knowledge-state games and controller refinement. ### T12 support, deletion and attainment

At a compatible belief a policy chooses one common action. The support contains its occurrence at each possible physical state and then the union of the single continuations for each attainable outcome and next-observation pair. A whole-action mask leaves the entire tree available exactly when it is disjoint from that union. A retained action brings all its original successor edges. This is the operational premise connecting executable support to the classical blocker duality. The empty family means every mask destroys feasibility. The singleton family containing the empty edge means immediate permissible stopping survives every mask. [Edmonds and Fulkerson, blocker involution](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0021-9800%2870%2980083-7)

Every action choice pays from the incoming budget and every continuation receives the same remaining budget because the action cost is public. A family computed at a looser budget can otherwise supply a minimal support belonging to an infeasible path. Fixed-kernel belief repetition permits substituting a strictly smaller suffix, preserving costs by nonnegativity and avoiding new checkpoint-action occurrences. This relies on a Markov checkpoint that carries all information affecting legality, cost and the stop test. The finite antichain may still contain $2^m$ sets in a chain of $m$ two-route stages, each route costing one and each complete choice vector minimal. If a port removes the stranded outcome from a sole mixed atom while retaining the operation, the baseline has no proper tree and the modified system does. That defeats extrapolation from whole-action masks to edge pruning. It must certify a no-conflict result and the weighted optimum over the current constraints if making a minimum-cost claim. **T14.b.** Actual prefix-compatible witnesses matter twice. Second, independence and query locality preserve compatibility and equal acquired answers along every one-coordinate hybrid. Restricting the full pair to $\{00,11\}$ can remove all hybrid witnesses. Rational acquisition arithmetic adds its actual encoding costs. The capped pair oracle handles nonnegative integer scores and correctly records an overflow for huge values or infinity. It keeps real mode witnesses in each pair of equal-answer cells. Surviving left-smaller/right-larger dominance is safe because future local options are independent of previous pair choices. **T24.** Under the rectangular Bernoulli richness premise, choose one shared conditional mean for each query independently at both installations. For a countermodel to marginal agreement, use a fair bit $Z$ and a fair auxiliary bit $A$, with $A=Z$ for one installation and $A=1-Z$ for the other. For sufficiency, center each sampled score at its true mean conditional on the actual pre-response history and query. The bounded-score supermartingale skips non-selected samples. For a countermodel to using marginal calibration as conditional calibration, draw a fair bit once and return it forever. ### T27 and T29 joint scope

Two independent checks are needed for the T27 bound. If $G$ occurs surely and all $L_i$ are empty, every local law could retain risk zero while containment fails. If $G=L_1$ occurs surely but the isolated law for that event gives zero, containment holds while retention fails. X13 preserves each kind of failure separately. The composed theorem is the union bound with a directed retention bridge and uncovered global mass. It does not infer the bridge from the isolated system or a scalar marginal equality on separate testers. This conditional structure has an established analogue in assume–guarantee verification, where the environment assumption is independently discharged. [Cobleigh et al., learning assumptions for compositional verification](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20030017771)

In T29, a winning witness chooses a common authorized action and uses every supported observation child induced by that very action. A losing verifier must rebuild these child beliefs from the whole support and demand a rejected first action or one actually attainable losing child for every available first action. Omitting a supported successor can fabricate a winning tree, and citing an invented observation can fabricate a refutation. Zero-cost actions preserve the horizon proof because horizon still decreases. With uniform nonnegative finite costs an optimum at a target belief stops immediately; otherwise the finitely many feasible first moves attain the recursion. The refutation needs the same residual budget for the child, with all conditionally hidden states included. A refutation at $h\max_a c(a)$ proves infeasibility at that horizon.

For each allowed policy $\pi$, its success region is the set of installations on which it satisfies the common fixed horizon, safety, work and budget. Deployable readiness is containment in at least one such region. The optional intrinsic-failure set is a further region disjoint from all success regions.
