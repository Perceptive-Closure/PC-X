## Auxiliary finite closer evaluation

**Accept** the truncated min–max algorithm, belief attractor, and finite attainment. The observable prefix determines a nonempty set of physical states. The constructor valuation and the blind recurrence both induct on allowable depth. A later occurrence of the same belief has the same available actions, observation updates and stop test. Copying its suffix to an ancestor eliminates a nonnegative prefix and strictly shrinks the tree, giving finite height $2^{|S|}-1$ as a safe bound. The value descends from zero at legal stops and infinity elsewhere, and is the greatest nonnegative extended-real Bellman fixed point. An open zero-cost self-loop permits lower, spurious fixed points. Every finite proper optimum is attained even with real finite action costs. A computational implementation needs exact cost comparison and addition.

I also checked the extra T11 label-setting variant. A provisional label is built from finalized child values and the action cost, so it has a proper-tree witness. If an unfinished belief has a better actual optimum than the next provisional label, descend its optimum tree to a deepest unfinished node. Its children are finalized, so it provides a provisional label at most its own continuation cost and hence at most the better root cost. A legal stop is seeded at zero. This gives the contradiction needed at each finalized belief, including ties and zero-cost transitions. Deletion cannot create a new reachable belief under any legal survivor policy, since replay before deletion preserves every successor of the selected actions.
