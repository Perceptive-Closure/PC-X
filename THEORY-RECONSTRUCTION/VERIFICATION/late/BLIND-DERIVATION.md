## PC embedding

For each eligible action use a normalized distribution with full positive support on the original joint successor and visible-label alternatives. Apply authority and whole-action freezing before assigning rows. The initial observation fiber keeps its original alternatives and has full initial support wherever the contract requires a guarantee. Add public cost and tick accumulation and score the declared terminal predicate at a lawful stop. A finite supported closing tree with depth at most n and maximum cost at most B gives zero risk. Conversely, every finite path made of positive initial, action and row probabilities has positive path probability. A positive-mass illegal stop, failure to stop, incorrect closed predicate or overspend therefore contradicts zero risk. If the policy randomizes, fix one positive-probability action at each reached history to obtain a supported deterministic tree. Erasing one successor of a frozen atomic action would change this correspondence.

For fixed B permit any finite n. A proper tree has finite depth directly if proper already means a finite tree, or by finite branching and the absence of an infinite branch. This yields the same budget set as the original worst-branch formulation and hence the same infimum, including infinity for an empty set. Repeating the construction for each mask gives all eight coordinates. The result uses positivity only. Two different probability assignments on the same support can give different positive risks.

## T19 · Risk endpoints and the exact direct comparator

Uniform passing is equivalent to every s∈S satisfying s≤ε, or sup S≤ε. Uniform failing means every s>ε. This holds for inf S>ε and for inf S=ε with ε absent from S, and fails in the other cases. If both uniform statements fail, their negations supply actual opposite witnesses. A finite nonempty S attains both endpoints. Risks {0,1} and [0,1] have the same outer interval, while one contains every interior risk and the other contains none.

The combined deterministic audit can use the faithful root direct measurement for d or an optimal semantic-only policy for W. Any exact combined strategy using the direct measurement on a reachable path pays at least d on that path since all preceding acquisitions cost nonnegative amounts. Any such strategy that never uses it on reachable paths is a semantic-only strategy and costs at least W in the worst case. The two inequalities give min(d,W), including W=∞ or a homogeneous root of cost zero. This worst-path argument provides no guarantee for an incomplete noisy readout or an expected-cost criterion.

## T21 · TV testing bound

Let g(h)∈[0,1] be the decision kernel's probability of issuing the P target. With abstention charged as failure, α=1−E_P g and β≥E_Q g. By layer cake or the mass definition, E_P g−E_Q g≤TV(P,Q). Consequently α+β≥1−TV(P,Q). Equal laws imply max(α,β)≥1/2. If abstention carried no charge, always abstaining would escape this decision guarantee.

## T22 · Stopped KL and reset counts

For each realized finite stopping length, factor the record's likelihood into its initial record, predictable common query and stopping kernels, response rows and common decision law. Absolute continuity at P-reached rows gives a likelihood ratio on P records. With common initial law and all model-dependent recorded innovations in the response rows, the log ratio is the sum of the response log ratios up to the stop. The separate support slices for different lengths cover the P measure and do not introduce a further sampling factor. Absolute integrability justifies exchanging expectation and the stopped sum. The indicator that a round has been initiated and its query are known before seeing its response. Hence iterating conditional expectation gives
$$
\mathrm{KL}(P_\tau\|Q_\tau)=\mathbb E_P\sum_{j\le\tau}
\mathrm{KL}\big(P_j(\cdot|h_j,q_j)\|Q_j(\cdot|h_j,q_j)\big).
$$
If the initial record is model-dependent, its own KL adds to this identity subject to its absolute continuity and integrability. This is necessary literally. Initial Bernoulli observations of probabilities 1/4 versus 3/4 followed by stopping with no queries yield positive stopped KL and an empty row sum under the stated sampled-round conditions. The same issue would arise from an uncounted model-controlled authorization signal or terminal observation.

Apply data processing to the event that the certificate gives P's target. It has P-probability at least 1−δ and Q-probability at most δ. Binary KL is increasing in its first argument and decreasing in the second on that ordered region, by its derivatives and continuity at the boundary. This gives the required kl(1−δ,δ), with abstention counted as failure.

With genuinely reset rows the divergence for q is fixed, so grouping by q yields the expected count times its divergence. A uniformly correct policy's expected count vector is feasible against every opposite rival and its cost is the weighted sum. The lower bound uses ≥, since a single Bernoulli draw with success probabilities 1−δ and δ attains equality. Interpret the optimization as an infimum if a minimum is unavailable. Finite expected cost with positive costs gives finite individual counts. Zero-cost queries with infinite mean count can be discarded from the allocation if they have zero divergence against every compared rival, as the absolute integrability premise requires for such infinite counts in the reset case. If a model draws one Bernoulli bit and returns it on every query, the whole stopped tape has one draw's information when the number of observations is fixed and positive. Multiplying one marginal KL by that number would be wrong.

## T29 · Governed synthesis and audit

For a budget refutation, record an unsafe state, a non-goal exhausted horizon, or the failure of each possible first action. ## T30.S · One serial governed fragment

### Operational value and threshold

Let U=∏ U_i be a nonempty actual residual answer cell, A=Σ a_i and f_i(m_i)=w_i x_i(m_i). At each mandatory stage completion requires one of the two designated processing modes. Each x_i=1 removes the whole fast action because a single positively supported relation-changing successor makes that atomic action touch R. Each x_i=0 permits fast. Every slow preserves R and completes the job. Extra waiting or refusal cannot improve a passing job schedule. Thus every supported all-jobs path in completion m costs at least A+Σ_i f_i(m_i), and choosing the cheaper available class at each stage attains that value. The argument holds for the concrete deployment when the checked public interface implements these class choices lawfully at the actual checkpoints.

Across a cell, choose slow when any residual mode in U_i has x_i=1 and choose fast otherwise. This rule depends only on stage observation and audited answers. The cost is A+Σ_i max_{U_i} f_i=max_U v, where independent residual coordinates allow simultaneous maximizers. Every common policy must cost at least this much at a maximizing completion. The analogous simultaneous minima give L and H. Empty local mode sets are inconsistent. At a nonempty cell all pass when H≤B, all fail when L>B, and otherwise a minimizing and a maximizing tuple give opposite verdicts. Equality at the minimum counts as a passing witness. A positive cell returns the common schedule, which completes each named mandatory job including the last. A stochastic realization giving full positive probability to the declared finite support has the same zero-risk guarantee under this schedule. Positive support is the basis for a qualitative guarantee.

The deployed lower bound only needs projection of concrete paths and tariff and job-event checks. The upper bound needs an observable lawful implementation of each chosen class. If “required actions exist at every checkpoint” asserts class-level existence alone, the literal transport conclusion can fail. Use one stage, a single mode of bit 1, two possible initial hidden checkpoints y_0,y_1 with observation 1, and B=a_1+w_1. At each checkpoint the fast class is frozen. The only slow request enabled at y_0 is s_0 and the only slow request enabled at y_1 is s_1. Both requests map to slow, cost a_1+w_1, preserve the relation and reach done with the job completed. Before the freeze both checkpoints also have a baseline fast request with a relation-changing outcome. Every projected enabled set is as declared. No common concrete request can serve the initial belief. An independently checked same-name public request or an authorized dispatcher lifting each class to executable operation throughout each compatible belief repairs this issue. If the statement's “declared public interface” already means such a common invoker, make that meaning explicit. The audit needs no knowledge of the actual hidden checkpoint.

The Cartesian restriction is essential to the common-value proof. With two stages of base and extra tariff 1 and residual mode pairs only (1,0) and (0,1), each fixed completion passes B=3. Identical pre-stage observations and identical slow-path logs force the common controller to use both slow actions, costing 4. Choosing a per-completion policy is insufficient even with individually aligned models.

### Exact static audit

The fixed query output cells are again Cartesian. A local crossing pair (u,v) at i is exactly a pair with f_i(u)<f_i(v) and an actual attainable outside-stage excess s satisfying
$$
f_i(u)+s\le B-A < f_i(v)+s.
$$
Such a pair plus the same outside tuple gives two actual opposite completions. Queries at other stages agree. At least one selected query at i must give unequal answers on u and v. For sufficiency, suppose a product answer cell contains two opposite verdicts. Replace the coordinates of one tuple one at a time by those of the other. All intermediate tuples remain compatible and give the same selected answers. The first verdict flip is a crossing pair left unseparated at its stage, a contradiction.
