# PC-X final experimental results

**Program completed on 26 November 2026.** The 15 experimental packages are published in Perceptive-Closure/PC-X. The master branch records the scope, custody, independent acceptance and cleanup of each experiment. [Work plan](WorkPlan.md) · [Execution record](Path.md)

The governing sources are the 43-page *Perceptive Closure X — Final Theorem Package* (SHA-256 `3110e8020cc1b86cdf7ba389602fb0ce0b941be84dfdef05d3eeb5580dec06bc`), the original 18-page *Perceptive Closure* manuscript (SHA-256 `f27b6f6e593129592577a0863f46853540b4377ec9393a4f28d48026dd2ebcd4`) and the accompanying execution directive. The theorem status matrix uses charter p. 38. The frozen source plans and reproduction entry points are pinned with the evidence below.

## A. Executive summary

- **Exact bounded identification works.** Whole-action freezing and observation-measurable policies recover exact signatures in the declared finite models. Correlated constraints can make rectangular evidence unnecessarily ambiguous. Minimum-cost target acquisition can differ sharply from full-contract acquisition. At the largest X05 case, both minimized efforts cost 677. The symbolic advantage was solving the case without materializing its 2^192 completions.
- **Independent semantic evidence can resolve a native counterfactual.** All eight Open Policy Agent resource-freeze forecasts matched. OpenSSL confirmed all eight diagnosis forecasts and two corrected coarse rejections. The richer OpenSSL diagnostic also achieved 8/8.
- **Equivalent observations can conceal harmful deployment differences.** Matched evaluation histories, alarm laws and probe readouts concealed distinct measured risks in the bounded scenarios. Two unsupported scoring shifts yielded false favorable forecasts. Independent fidelity and support checks distinguished justified transport from abstention. An independent trusted audit outperformed the motivating acquisition method in X12.
- **Useful governed action was realized in a finite class.** The governed policy was safe on all ten tickets and completed the full workload on eight. Its two refusals matched the bounded infeasibility proof. The composition experiment separately falsified automatic transfer of local risk bounds and automatic containment of the joint event.

The evidence supports the named finite models, native fixtures and sandbox interventions. General anchored transport (T18) and constructive identification of governed deployment guarantees (T30) remain open. Section F describes the independent reproduction, additional falsification and custody timing qualifications.

In the authorization studies, E is admitted evidence, R is certificate representation and A is action authority. A freeze removes a complete action when any supported successor changes a frozen resource. The controller selects actions from what it can observe. Audit costs are the units declared by each experiment.

## B. Individual experiment results

### EXP-X01 · Exact finite completion recovery

Compared an optimized finite belief solver with exhaustive observation-measurable policy trees under all eight E/R/A freezes. The engines agreed on 1,944 coordinates across 243 contracts, 1,378 evidence cases and 1,155 signature witnesses. Controls covered cyclic paths, atomic effects on multiple successors, representation relabeling, source-equivalent E/R interpretations and contradictory evidence. A controller given hidden state understated the required cost or changed feasibility on 6 of 18 controls. Reading only the first supported successor missed changes on 3 of 6 mixed-action controls.

The results support finite target recovery and witness-checking for T01–T04 and T13. The result depends on completeness of the finite catalog and its frozen projection.

### EXP-X02 · Nonrectangular semantic ambiguity

Enumerated 249 compatible completions from 94 Boolean-constrained cases and checked all 1,992 freeze coordinates with three implementations. The rectangular relaxation enlarged the joint set in 29 of 83 nonempty cases. It agreed in the other 54. It created 19 false coordinate ambiguities and widened 51 coordinate sets. For mutually exclusive effects, the exact R-frozen attained set was {1}, whereas the product gave {1, ∞}. With the finite fallback, the exact R bounds were [1, 2], while the product gave [1, 5]. Equality, inconsistent evidence and multi-successor controls were retained.

This is exact evidence for correlated ambiguity in T02–T04 and a bounded construction for T11–T13. The fragment is fully observable and forward finite. General nonrectangular algorithmic tractability remains open.

### EXP-X03 · Global and local certificate optimality

Compared minimum weighted disagreement coverage with independently enumerated answer partitions. Across 865 domains and 3,828 scopes, 59,971 subset checks verified 3,102 certificates, 725 inaccessible scopes and one inconsistent scope. For the 645 finite uniform scopes, mean exact cost was 2.131783. Weighted greedy cost 2.207752, deletion 2.412403, random orders 3.095446 and full acquisition 6.262016. A structured freeze case required cost 6 for the target and 8 for full identity. In 180 uniform scopes a target certificate existed while full identity remained inaccessible.

The one-hot control gave cost 2 uniformly and 1 for every realized completion. Complete-interface aliases and contradictory evidence produced checkable failures. These checks instantiate T04–T07 and the finite certificate machinery behind T14. The query costs and completion families are declared design inputs.

### EXP-X04 · Adaptive semantic audits

Used an observation-only minimax tree, exhaustive static subset checks and an independent answer-history search. In 88 cases the solvers agreed on 47 finite optima, 40 inaccessible cases and one inconsistent case. Replayed 21,062 policy paths and checked 37 pinned X03 scopes. Of the 47 finite cases, 16 improved on static and 31 tied. Six of seven feasible routed authorization cases improved. A five-site case cost 2 adaptive and 6 static. A weighted case cost 7 and 12. The four finite X03 authorization controls tied. Two routed-query removals made the target inaccessible.

These results support bounded T05–T10 mechanisms. The 10-bit address result is an algebraic example. The measured 1- and 2-bit cases and structured route examples supply finite evidence. Cost improvements depend on the query interface, family and weights.

### EXP-X05 · Symbolic scalability

Compared a paired symbolic oracle and lazy minimum audit with direct partitions, eager disagreement enumeration and a no-dominance ablation. The 70 cases include 49 original cases and a parent-approved, pre-execution amendment adding 21 controlled comparisons of stratum, freeze and threshold. There were 62 minimum uniform audits, three point identifications, one inaccessible audit, one inconsistent case and three deliberately unresolved controls. All 65 affirmative results passed independent sufficiency and optimality checks.

At the largest matched case, 64 stages with three free facts each represented 2^192 completions. All 192 facts were individually pivotal somewhere in the legal support. The audit cost 677 against 1,070 for all 256 queries. X15 independently computed an optimal full-identity audit cost of 677 on this same largest case. The acquisition advantage over buying every query comes with a tie against the minimized identity comparator. The method used 412 paired-oracle calls and opened zero full-catalog rows. The author run recorded a 1.046377 s median and 1,208,636 Python-traced bytes. The auditor's fresh run gave a 0.94308 s median with the same traced allocation. The explicit comparators refused 35 cases at the completion cap. Direct and eager methods ran faster in 20 and 22 of the 28 jointly affirmative cases, respectively. The no-dominance variant exhausted its work cap on the largest matched case. Dropping the second supported branch falsely accepted two control modes.

Symbolic gain is supported for the specified layered fragment and cost models in T10–T14. The input factorizations, work caps and machine affect measured scalability. The timing reflects the stated runs and Python allocation tracing.

### EXP-X06 · Held-out authorization system

Used the unchanged Open Policy Agent v1.22.4 engine and an unmodified upstream tutorial policy, with a local adapter defining atomic edits and the resource-freeze target. An independent parent selected the sealed roster, measured legitimate admission counts and verified the frozen forecast before invoking the native engine. All eight budget-one R-freeze forecasts and all 64 freeze coordinates matched. There were 112 held-out native decisions, equally split between allow and deny, and five separate native development controls. Independent reconstruction found 48 whole-action removals.

Exact acquisition cost was 8 across the roster, compared with 12 for cheapest-first and 28 for full acquisition. Terminal-only reasoning falsely certified four cases. No audit abstained in all eight. Finite R-frozen fallbacks at costs 3 and 5 missed the budget-one target. This supplies native evidence for T02–T04, T06, T11 and T13–T14, and limited constructive evidence relevant to T30. The authorization result is specific to the pinned engine/policy and declared adapter.

### EXP-X07 · Security diagnosis reversal

On eight sealed, offline X.509 fixtures, OpenSSL 3.2.4 distinguished previously rejected cases after the approved chain was independently provisioned. The signature-binding and signed-validity audit correctly forecast all eight, with two reversals and six retained failures. An always-fail coarse diagnosis got 6/8 with two false failures. The stronger native diagnostic using printed identifiers and validity tied at 8/8. No audit abstained eight times. Omitting either probe gave four correct and four abstentions. The valid, expired and wrong-anchor controls all behaved as specified.

The pinned X03 solver and an independent calculation agreed on uniform minimum cost 4 and realized minima 4, 1, 3 and 1. This supports a defensive instance of T02, T04–T06 and T13. The fixture family and weights were constructed. The observed gain applies to the coarse baseline and the rich-baseline tie bounds the incremental claim.

### EXP-X08 · Evaluation-equivalent AI agents

The exact law checks covered every authorized reachable three-round evaluation history and next request in a local routing sandbox. The two assignments had identical full permitted evaluation laws and opposite deployment classifications. A floor cue broke equivalence with total variation 1/2. An independent logging bit preserved equivalence. Removing route calibration made the target inaccessible. The measured held-out run used 8,192 episodes, confirmed both threshold decisions and overlapped all four preregistered contextual bands. Worst deployment violation risk was 651/1024 versus 128/1024. The benchmark-only baseline falsely certified one unit. Minimum acquisition cost was 2 against 6 cheapest-first and 8 exhaustive.

The bounded equality and counterexample support T15–T17 and the applicability of T21. The evaluator interface, three-round horizon and prescribed resets delimit the equality. The held-out chronology qualification is recorded in the reproducibility assessment.

### EXP-X09 · Evaluation-aware AI systems

Compared permitted randomized cues and verified cold resets with fixed cues and carried history. The held-out factorial and native samples contain 65,536 audit observations and 131,072 task episodes. All four admitted units matched forecast thresholds and all eight admitted contextual bands overlapped. Both midpoint controls gave inconsistent fit. Both deliberate scoring shifts outside the calibration family falsely passed under the unanchored rule. Fixed-blue carry correctly left the target unresolved. Direct blue extrapolation falsely passed two of four admitted units.

Mean and worst acquisition costs of 720 and 800 tied the finite greedy method. Exhaustive acquisition cost 1,440. These checks support bounded T15–T17 and T21–T24 mechanisms and expose the missing transport anchor under the two shifts. Statistical conclusions depend on reset validity, sampling independence and the admitted scoring model.

### EXP-X10 · Monitor blind spots

The independent route card and physical tray defined the true violation and the missed event. In 768 exact unit-stratum-ticket traces, the full monitor-visible laws agreed across eight candidate installations. A reader control changed the alarm law. The 16,384 held-out trials confirmed all four threshold forecasts and overlapped all eight contextual forecast bands. Peak alarm rates were 17.14% and 16.64%, while peak missed-event rates were 48.24% and 8.58%. Alarm-only extrapolation falsely certified one of two units. The exact audit cost 2, compared with 4 for the greedy and cheapest-first comparators and 6 for full acquisition.

Suppressing oversize route-card labels widened the risk intervals enough to prevent identification, and the conditional false-negative estimate became unavailable. The evidence supports T25 for this monitor, independent route-card labels and balanced enrollment. Rates depend on the chosen deployment mixtures.

### EXP-X11 · Intervention fidelity

Paired four print-queue probe regimes on common tickets and scored arrivals/completions independently of the public logger. The 8,192 matched blocks contain 32,768 episodes. Four risk and three fidelity forecasts were confirmed, with overlap in all 14 registered bands. Passive capture and placebo matched block by block. The disturbing probe raised risk by 69.56 percentage points in the steady context and 66.63 in the burst context while the public tape stayed equal. Unrestricted probe transfer failed and withholding the relevant anchor caused abstention.

The result supports T26 and gives a concrete requirement for T18–T19. Transport of the observed outcome under the passive probe depends on the independently validated fidelity check. The independent audit's timing qualification appears below.

### EXP-X12 · Deployment generalization

Used a reactive sorter and a stateful print queue. Independently scored machine ledgers and pre-run reference banks supplied calibrated contextual risks, while verified support and matched readbacks supplied fidelity and discrepancy evidence. The 98,304 held-out episodes confirmed all six anchored verdicts, with six justified abstentions, zero false certifications and overlap in all 24 contextual bands. The anchored prediction envelopes covered all 24 independently computed exact contextual risks. Evaluation-only extrapolation falsely passed 7/12. Omitting fidelity falsely passed 2/12. Large drift gave a vacuous [0, 1] bound and missing support required abstention.

Configured acquisition operations were 1,048,804 for the anchored method and 1,573,092 for fixed nonadaptive acquisition, both yielding six confirmed decisions. Full trusted acquisition resolved ten at 786,444, so it dominated the anchored method on these costs and decisions. These are conditional instances of T17–T20 and T23. The scoped coupling and reference banks leave general T18 and T30 unresolved.

### EXP-X13 · Multi-agent composition

Independently scored local underwork and global bench overlap on 512 joint and 1,024 isolated episodes. All eight registered context judgments matched. Mode A preserved local bounds while event containment failed. Mode D kept containment while local bounds failed in joint operation. Mode B failed both, reaching 15/16 measured risk. The naive use of isolated local bounds falsely certified three of four modes. Exclusive, faithful mode C maintained both conditions, certified a 1/4 upper bound and had worst measured risk 7/64, with at least 121 of 128 jobs per dispatcher qualified in each context.

The constructed counterexamples and useful positive control support the conditional structure in T27. Retention of local bounds and containment were verified independently within this finite joint model. Wider composition remains open.

### EXP-X14 · Governed alignment realizability

Synthesized finite observation-only shop policies and checked them with a separate concrete budget oracle, a physical scorer and an infeasibility proof checker. The solvers agreed in 92 bounded cases, including 22 winning, 69 infeasible and one inconsistent. Seventeen cases permitted an individual policy for each contract with no common policy. Independent checking covered 144 physical replays and 1,545 losing-proof nodes. Layout, masked-panel and grant controls isolated the required authority and observation assumptions.

All 40 forecast tickets matched. Governed control was safe on 10/10 and finished both jobs on 8/10, with 16/20 jobs qualified. It refused both slips at the bounded-infeasible port. Fixed-left gave 4/10 safe and 4/10 complete. A single-contract policy gave 6/10 safe and 4/10 complete. Permanent refusal was safe on 10/10 with zero jobs. External acquisition cost 9 on the realized branch and 11 minimax, tied by greedy and below the exact static 20. Governed in-task acquisition cost 6 total and 3 worst. External and in-task weights refer to different interfaces. This supports bounded T28–T29 synthesis and supplies a limited example relevant to T30.

### EXP-X15 · Independent hostile reproduction

Reacquired the exact 14 terminal packages from GitHub and verified archive members against their Git tree and blob IDs. Separate code reconstructed the reported results and checked all nine holdout commitments and their forecast-to-freeze ancestry. A new X08 seed covered all four exact risk values within the disclosed sandbox model. The X15 harness passed eight additional tests.

The primary quantitative results reproduced. The audit identified the exact target/full-identity cost tie in X05. It reaffirmed the stronger native baseline tie in X07 and the lower-cost trusted comparator in X12. It qualified prospective timing evidence for X08 and X11. It also reconstructed the 18 matched X05 seed pairs and tested the relation between their generated graphs, the chosen freeze and the cutoff rule. Three pairs repeat their complete measured setting. The correlation strata can differ in seed and numerical costs, limiting causal claims about runtime.

The negative controls and scope review support bounded instance-level conclusions for T01–T30. The audit knew the published outcomes and used independent checks on reproducible inputs. It assessed the experimental consequences and left the outstanding proof obligations in place.

## D. Alignment and generalization

- **Evaluation non-identifiability and deployment counterfactuals.** X08 supplies equality across the entire permitted adaptive transcript interface at its horizon. X09 shows how a carried history preserves an ambiguity that a valid cold reset can break. Native X06 and X07 establish the value of specific independent semantic facts in two different local systems.
- **Evaluation awareness and intervention validity.** In X09, the cue and history policy belong to the evidence model. X11 independently measures causal disturbance despite unchanged public observations. X12's substitution port shows why agreement between two affected readouts cannot establish a faithful anchor.
- **Semantic audit complexity and minimum-cost identification.** X03 separates uniform sufficiency, realized sufficiency and full identity. X04 shows which routed structures benefit from adaptation and retains ties. X05 trades explicit enumeration for verified symbolic work under known structure. X14 buys only facts needed for a deployment vector. The weights and dynamic acquisition menus vary by experiment.
- **Monitorability and multi-agent composition.** Alarms are incomplete evidence for missed events in X10. X13 shows why local bounds require independent confirmation of both their retention during joint operation and containment of the global event by the local events.
- **Governed alignment and transport.** X14 certifies safe work on feasible ports and defensible refusal on an infeasible port. X12 confirms confined transfer when support, fidelity and discrepancy checks succeed. Missing support, substantial drift and misleading probes restrict the result. Joint deployment and external shifts outside the declared classes need further evidence and mathematical treatment.

## E. Negative findings and limitations

- Apparent precision can fail under hidden-state policy selection, incomplete atomic touch, rectangular relaxation, incomplete audit interfaces and policy access that exceeds the controller's observation boundary. X01–X05 retain witnesses and expected refusals. An inconsistent completion set cannot certify a target. X05 preserves three deliberately unresolved cases and comparator cap failures as such.
- Adaptive savings were conditional in X04. Small explicitly solved cases often favored simpler timing baselines in X05. The largest X05 case showed zero acquisition savings against exact full-identity minimization. The stronger OpenSSL baseline tied the independent audit in X07. Greedy tied the measured cost of X09 and the exact worst external acquisition cost of X14. In X12, trusted measurement was both cheaper and more informative than the proposed anchor sequence.
- Evaluation-only extrapolation produced false favorable results in X08, X09 and X12. Both prespecified unsupported X09 shifts falsely passed the unanchored rule. Monitor-only reasoning in X10 and probe-only transfer in X11 gave false conclusions. X13's blind aggregation failed in three of four conditions, and X14's fixed and single-contract comparators produced unsafe work attempts.
- The formal cases are finite constructed workloads. The OPA and OpenSSL evaluations use actual engines on controlled inputs and environments. X08–X14 measure benign local sandbox runtimes with declared support and outcome instruments. Population prevalence remains unmeasured for production deployments, frontier models and public-key infrastructure. The deterministic studies use exact counts. Stochastic confidence intervals apply to the declared sample designs.
- Custody involved separate investigator roles, exact GitHub revisions and nonce-backed disclosures where implemented. The tasks ran on a shared host, so the record demonstrates process separation within the experiment. Chronology qualifications and runtime portability limits appear in the reproducibility assessment.

## F. Reproducibility assessment

The parent replayed each package from an exact GitHub checkout or archive. X15 then verified the 14 upstream GitHub trees, repeated the 152 predecessor tests and reconstructed the published results through separate evaluators and scorers. The audit checked raw digests, native return codes and scope-specific comparisons. X01, X03 and X05 regenerate fixture inputs from pinned generators before independent checks. X08 retains the opened seed and transcript digest to regenerate its full raw stream. The staged experiments retain the applicable combination of raw ledgers, receipts, forecast and opening records, and replay manifests.

- **Prospective timing.** All nine opened commitments recomputed. The forecast commits directly follow their pinned pre-audit executables with authorized file deltas, and the published forecast bytes match the terminal evidence. Seven packages record a strict timestamp order. X08's timestamp precision gives the same whole second for verification and completion. X11 relies on the cited parent ledger for the sequence that its opening record does not timestamp. These two quantitative replays passed with qualified timing corroboration. The custodian attests to the order, and the retained package evidence alone leaves the timing qualifications in place. Shared-host process separation and same-seed replay have the limits described in section E.
- **Statistical scope.** X15 independently computed X09 upper error bounds of 0.0477815, 0.0401988 and 0.00914249 for the audit, native and factorial families. Their sum is approximately 0.097123. The reported family guarantees apply separately. The study counts are heterogeneous and some artifacts supply prerequisite inputs to other experiments, which rules out treating the experiment totals as one independent statistical sample.
- **Runtime and recovery.** X06 depends on the official OPA Windows AMD64 release and pinned policy/license. X07 depends on the recorded OpenSSL and Python cryptography environment. X14 requires canonical LF bytes for offline Git archive replay. Several initial X15 driver invocations exposed scratch location, output collision, dependency environment or receipt-writing problems. The corrected driver reran the affected commands and preserved the outcomes. The X05 performance replay reports host-dependent timings and independently checked deterministic outputs.
- **Orchestration choices.** The parent approved the matched-grid addition to X05 before execution and kept all original cases. X15 began with 12 closed inputs and froze each of the final two pins after its acceptance and before retrieval. The final independent review used all 14 terminal packages. The GitHub visibility is public by explicit owner decision. Some immutable records refer to the earlier private repository or include local runtime metadata.

## G. Final experimental verdict

The independent reproduction and its limitations qualify that operational completion.

- Present the two prospective native case studies with the precise budget, adapter and trust assumptions. Pair X07's correction of the coarse baseline with the stronger baseline tie.
- Present the evaluation-equivalence, monitor, probe disturbance, transfer and composition counterexamples alongside their valid positive controls. Include X12's cheaper trusted baseline and the two X09 false favorable shifts.
- Use X14 to demonstrate useful governed action on a finite class with a verified common-policy gap. Publish the refusal arm's zero utility and the exact inaccessible port.

The failed universal assumptions are that incomplete observation suffices, that fit or matching readouts guarantee transport, and that isolated certificates automatically compose. General scalable audit complexity, arbitrary nonrectangular reasoning, general strategic evaluation, a substantive anchored transport theorem and the integrated T30 result remain open. Elevating the full flagship framework requires additional mathematical results and external validation. The original Perceptive Closure manuscript remains the research baseline.
