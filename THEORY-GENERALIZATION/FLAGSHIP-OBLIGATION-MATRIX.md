## Timing and meaningful comparators

T30.CG generalizes the task to correlated installations, actual informative work and dynamic prices. Its acquisition is stage-local. A predeployment read of a later installed bit can distinguish paths with the same first edge. Emulating all later read answers at the first stage of a deterministic stage-local model can require a different first edge per distinct read vector. This precludes a width-preserving claim that the original T30.S read timing is a special case.

Within the joint online model, a Cartesian supporting instance has \(n\) ancillary jobs of base tariff 1 and extra slow tariff 1, then one gateway job of base tariff 1 and extra slow tariff \(n+1\). At \(D=2n+1\) and \(H=2n+2\), the target depends only on the gateway. Doing the common slow ancillary jobs, then reading the gateway at cost 1 and conditionally serving it gives the optimal target policy. Full identity accompanies that same verdict/work specification by reading all \(n+1\) bits at their stages. Each omitted bit could flip with no received record under lawful common moves, so the identity minimum is \(n+1\). This supporting check stays within the same online timing.
