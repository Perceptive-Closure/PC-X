**Residual-grid qualification and independent depth bound.** Two consecutive safe useful-work moves of tariff \(1/2\) can use work budget 1 exactly, but the first successor has residual \(1/2\) and is absent from an unscaled integer residual grid. A rational input admits a common-denominator scale, or the displayed grid may be read with integral charges from the start. The constructor proof states the arithmetic restriction. It belongs with any standalone statement of that grid bound.

The existence and witness-depth assertion also follows without discretizing nonnegative charges. Suppose a proper policy exists for the stated time-homogeneous \((B,p)\) rules with exact rows and no hard depth cap. Choose a successful deterministic tree of smallest total size. Its finite existence follows from supported derandomization and finite branching with proper termination. If an ancestor node \(u\) and proper descendant \(v\) on one branch have the same \((B,p)\), transplant the entire subtree from \(v\) to \(u\), selecting its actions by the future public suffix. The two histories have exactly the same current hidden possibilities, legal rows, next-record meter updates and stop predicates. All suffix executions remain supported in precisely the same way. Both remaining budgets at \(u\) are at least their values at \(v\), since the skipped charges are nonnegative. Thus the replacement is safe, affordable and proper at \(u\), and the whole tree has fewer nodes, a contradiction. Each branch therefore visits at most \(|P|(2^{|S|}-1)\) distinct pairs, and takes at most one fewer move. Bounded search over that depth decides feasibility when charges admit exact comparisons. The proof requires every history effect on the rows to be in \((B,p)\); the residuals only constrain affordability.

## Eight obligations within the online support model

| Accepted original obligation | T30.CG disposition on the same class and objective |
| --- | --- |
| Soundness | Nonempty genuine state summaries certify precisely the intrinsic threshold, and physical YES/NO obey the common-history rule. |
| Constructiveness | Produce attained extremal or opposed paths, a minimizing implementable policy, or an exact losing certificate. Reconstruct concrete witnesses from the chosen history. |
| Completeness | Exhaust all callable common first moves and every genuine branch over the finite horizon. The finite recurrence returns the value or infeasibility; empty evidence is handled separately. |
| Exactness | Intrinsic tariffs, verdict, legality, work and acquisition price match the independently specified supported physical protocol. |
| Audit optimality | The minimum worst supported acquisition price is attained among permitted joint adaptive policies. T30.C gives the separate minimum for a pure static menu. |
| Transport validity | Conditional primitive rows preserve the exact concrete outcomes and progress in both directions, with no unpaid disclosure of the installed path. Sure-to-zero-risk transport also fixes the stated initial and conditional supports. |
| Non-vacuity | Every successful positive branch performs every required job within the work and move budgets. The receipt and meter-gated examples exhibit informative real work and a nonzero paid optimum respectively. |
| Computational advantage | Edge-indexed attained extrema, cap arithmetic and finite meters avoid full path and full score-set materialization at the stated width and numeric bounds. |

This is the conjunction of the eight scoped obligations for the declared online model. T30.C independently supplies optimal static pure identification. General positive-risk, adaptive deployment and strategic guarantees require their own assumptions and proofs.
