# M02 · sealed approximation corollaries

These statements refer only to the exact M02.1 and M02.4 definitions sealed separately in [STATEMENTS.md](STATEMENTS.md). They add no prerequisite from another workstream.

## M02.5 · certified bounds and an approximation boundary

**Checked interval claim.** For an inhabited M02.1 input, expand any prefix of the acyclic exact information-state graph so that each expanded decision retains all lawful query choices and feasible reply branches. Frontier states may be assigned cost interval \([0,+\infty]\), or narrower independently certified intervals for their optimal remaining cost. Replaying the same exact cost-to-go expression with the respective lower and upper endpoint at each frontier yields certified lower and upper bounds for the root. For a fixed ordering of expansions and successively tightened frontier intervals, expanding by the exact recurrence with still-open child intervals beginning at \([0,+\infty]\) need not preserve prior tighter independent bounds unless those bounds are also retained by intersection. With intersections, the root lower bound weakly increases and its upper bound weakly decreases. A finite upper bound certified by proper policies at the frontier has a replayable realizing proper audit. In particular positive lower bound \(L\) and finite such upper bound \(U\) give a certificate that this audit costs at most \(U/L\) times optimum. No uniform ratio is claimed for a partial expansion.

**Succinct approximation boundary.** In the succinct deterministic M02.4 representation, even restricting to instances with positive finite optimum and a lawful direct fallback, there is no deterministic polynomial-time estimator returning a number between optimum and \(p(L)\) times optimum for any fixed polynomial \(p\) in the input bit length \(L\), unless \(\mathrm P=\mathrm{PSPACE}\). The claim permits nonnegative binary integer prices and does not apply to the explicit bounded-width representation of M02.1. A polynomial-time optimizer that also outputs such a certified cost would be subject to the same bound.
