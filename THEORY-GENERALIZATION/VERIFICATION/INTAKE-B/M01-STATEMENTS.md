## M01.1 · finite typed source extraction and exact certificates

**Fragment.** Fix finite explicitly represented types \(X,U,Z,O,V\), a finite registry \(A\) of distinct whole source actions and an extensional action permission type \(2^A\). Fix a sealed world target \(A_\Pi:X\to D\) and a fixed entry locator. A checkpoint is a vector \((z,h,p,\lambda)\in S=Z\times U\times \operatorname{Eq}(U)\times 2^A\), with equality on the last two coordinates given by their extensional tables. The expansion of these types is a permitted computation cost. A finite tuple \(\theta\) in a product of finite interpreted slot domains is fixed for the entire contract. It can fill declared source field slots and environment parameters; its correlations are expressed by governing constraints. Typed, total straight-line circuits over these finite types give the entry \(s_0(\theta)\), compatibility \(x\sim_\theta h\), public controller observation \(\omega_\theta(s)\), and enabled-action and postcondition semantics as follows.

- A single registered action \(a\) is admitted exactly when its source guard (a total circuit with no permission-register argument) holds and \(a\in\lambda\). A declared finite collection of guarded environment alternatives gives whole postcheckpoint vectors. Exactly alternatives with true guard and strictly positive weight contribute to support. Weights are exact declared nonnegative rationals. A total outcome circuit \(Y_\theta(s,a,u)\in V\) gives the unique visible edge label for an ordered triple of checkpoints and action. Alternatively a source that retains an outcome in its raw state can use that component to evaluate this circuit. A finite nonnegative rational \(c(a)\) is fixed per source action. Controller choices use only the full history of observations, chosen action identities and visible outcomes. Controller observation and certificate equality are separate types.
- A permitted machine has nonempty support at each admitted occurrence reachable from the entry and has a nonempty represented fiber at each reachable checkpoint. Reachability uses the unfrozen full positive support. The fiber at \(s\) is \(\{x:\exists h'\in [h]_p,\ x\sim_\theta h'\}\), and closure requires constancy of the sealed \(A_\Pi\) there. From the source support, the mask of an admitted occurrence is the union of extensional changes in \(h,p,\lambda\) over its supported successors. Freezing \(F\subseteq\{E,R,A\}\) removes a whole occurrence when its mask intersects \(F\). Kept occurrences retain every supported successor. The target \(\Phi\) is a specified total circuit over complete canonical kernel fields and, if requested, the exact eight-coordinate optimal proper closure signature, with \(+\infty\) represented by a separate flag.
- Governing evidence \(\Omega\) is a Boolean circuit over the slots and the canonical fields, with allowed atoms including existential support of a particular finite trusted trace (truncated or complete as declared) and exact finite readbacks from named licensed source instruments. Its reachability or trace atoms inspect only source-generated entries and edges. A specified instrument returns a source-grounded finite value by a total circuit and does not change the plant, slots or target. Instrument outputs do not become controller observations unless explicitly in the controller interface. Requirements outside these finite typed schemas are outside the fragment. The actual system inclusion claim is conditional on the actual governing machine having this source semantics for some permitted \(\theta\) and satisfying \(\Omega\).

**Claim.** For every such source, there is a terminating typed-to-Boolean compilation of a reusable canonical kernel together with witness and refutation checking for the machine-validity and evidence predicate. For every assignment to its input slots, the kernel fields are uniquely determined by the source equations. Satisfying assignments to validity plus evidence project exactly to compatible interpretations, allowing duplicate representations that induce the same contract. Kernel evaluation preserves full support, availability, the observation boundary, the extensional resource masks and the eight finite-tree optima including \(+\infty\). Consequently the following tasks have total certificate-producing procedures for the declared encoding.

1. Find a compatible slot witness or certify inconsistency. Certify the possible values (jointly, for a tuple) of the finite target \(\Phi\), and certify singleton identification only with a compatible witness and a checked no-opposite-value certificate. The same applies to a consistent observed readback cell. Finite minima/maxima here are attained.
2. Replay a paired ambiguity witness including the actual source assignments, exact targets and any readback equalities. Given a specified complete list of deterministic pure readbacks, either certify that their joint answers separate every opposite-target pair or exhibit two compatible opposite-target assignments with equal answers to the entire list. In the latter case no policy using solely these readbacks can identify uniformly with certainty, including adaptive selection.
3. For a fixed compatible kernel, a nonnegative integer budget \(b\), and any subset of the fixed-kernel admitted occurrences marked kept, construct a finite monotone Boolean circuit in the keep bits for proper closure by budget \(b\). In this clause costs are nonnegative integers. If the number of physical checkpoints is \(n\), using \(2^n-1\) as a depth bound suffices. An occurrence that is unavailable at any possible current checkpoint cannot serve as a common action. The inclusion-minimal satisfying keep sets are exactly the inclusion-minimal proper policy supports at budget \(b\). Replacing keep bits by the corresponding whole-atom resource-freeze bits specializes the circuit to each fixed \(F\).
4. All the preceding certificate tasks may use explicit finite expansion with local equations and a finite Boolean proof of absence. There is no bound polynomial in succinct source length in this claim. A solver timeout or a checkable satisfying relaxation without a compatible concrete source assignment is insufficient for exact identification, refutation or a concrete disagreement witness.

## M01.2 · directed observable belief cover

Fix two D-35 finite typed kernels with supported successors, nonnegative finite action costs, and nonempty observable entry beliefs. At each nonempty belief \(B\), define \(L(B)\) to be its whole source actions legal at every \(s\in B\). For \(a\in L(B)\), let \(U(B,a)\) be the union of canonical occurrence masks across \(B\). A child is the full nonempty successor belief \(B_{a,\ell}\) indexed by a feasible visible pair \(\ell=(Y,\omega_{\mathrm{next}})\). Write \(J(B,a)\) for the set of these labels. Declare \(D(B)\) to be its stopping menu: a single dummy label iff every state is closed for plain closure, or the set of permitted common report labels under an explicitly declared common-label stopping rule. In the second system use primes. The entry beliefs are reached by controllers that know their own histories, with no hidden state access.

A directed relation \(R\) on pairs of reachable unfrozen beliefs, with source first and receiver second, is a cover if for all \(B\,R\,B'\):

- \(D(B)\subseteq D'(B')\).
- For every \(a\in L(B)\), there exists a **single** \(a'\in L'(B')\), with \(c'(a')\le c(a)\) and \(U'(B',a')\subseteq U(B,a)\), and there exists a function \(\sigma:J'(B',a')\to J(B,a)\) such that \(B_{a,\sigma(\ell')}\,R\,B'_{a',\ell'}\) for every \(\ell'\) in the domain.

The action and function may depend on the pair of belief nodes and the chosen source action. They do not depend on an unobserved physical state or successor. Commands remain actual source atoms in each system. No injectivity or global relabeling of menus or outcomes is required.

**Claim.** If the entry pair belongs to a cover, then for every \(F\subseteq\{E,R,A\}\) and every finite proper source policy after freeze \(F\), there is a finite proper receiving policy after the same freeze, with no greater depth or worst-support cost. The receiving controller can implement it from its own observable history. Thus \(\kappa_{\rm receiver}^{\neg F}\le\kappa_{\rm source}^{\neg F}\) in the extended reals. A directed cover in both directions gives equality of all eight coordinates, equality of defined \(R\)-penalties, and agreement on baseline unclosability. If a symmetric correspondence in the accepted T03 sense holds at the original entries, the lifted covered-belief pairs satisfy the directed requirements in each direction. The converse fails for finite typed contracts, and equality of signatures alone does not imply covers. On the explicit unfrozen belief graphs the greatest directed cover is computable and checkable by finite relation elimination in polynomial time in their expanded sizes. If a directed cover additionally admits a surjective \(\sigma\) and equality of move costs for each matching choice, it transports each proper source policy at exactly its worst-path cost; the respective two-way condition gives equality of attainable proper worst-path cost sets.

## M01.3 · interpretation boundaries

**Claim.** A deterministic typed source program with a deployed receiver and a lawful readback has two compatible slot assignments with the same fixed source program and identical complete traces in its permitted evaluation setting, and with opposite values of one fixed binary deployment failure target. The evaluating setting does not select the installed receiver record. A read-only measurement of this record separates the pair, and fixing it within that two-assignment evidence class identifies the target. Both assignments use the same independently fixed criterion for successful physical receipt.

**Claim.** A terminating exact compiler deciding the existence of a finite proper closer for all valid unbounded-memory, deterministic small-step sources would decide machine halting, even when there is a unique interpretation and the controller has a single possible tick action while running. This claim concerns extraction/evaluation of stronger source languages. The finite compiler is not asserted to infer that a physical installation matches a chosen source language or slot schema.
