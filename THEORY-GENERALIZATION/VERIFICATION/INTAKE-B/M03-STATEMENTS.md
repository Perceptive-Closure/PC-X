## M03.1 · Joint trajectory risk from local conditional and event restrictions

Fix a nonempty finite roster D of declared deployment runs, each with a fixed authorized observation-history controller and finite horizon, a finite context alphabet X, finite outcome letters Y, and a trusted terminal event V in {0,1}. The same physical predicate defines V throughout. A cell (d,x,y,b) specifies context, a complete trajectory or a checked refinement of an outcome letter, and that predicate's value b. Only trace cells admitted by the operational interface, semantic scoring contract, and positive support restrictions under consideration may have mass. Membership assertions about scoring, legitimate actions, policy and coverage for the proposed interface are independent premises.

Use an explicitly finite nonempty set of semantic modes m. In each mode introduce, for all d and x, deployment masses q_{dxyb}, context weights w_{dx}=Σ_{y,b}q_{dxyb}, and auxiliary reference masses s_{dxy} with Σ_y s_{dxy}=w_{dx}. All are nonnegative and Σ_x w_{dx}=1 for each d. Write a_{dxy}=Σ_b q_{dxyb}. At any w_{dx}>0, s_{dx}/w_{dx} is the reference conditional outcome row for the declared comparison cell, or a free dummy probability vector if the cell has no reference bridge. The deployment outcome row is a_{dx}/w_{dx}. Independently established premises, when furnished, are

- a row condition H_{dx}(s_{dx}/w_{dx})≤h_{dx} on the reference outcome probabilities and a bound TV(a_{dx}/w_{dx},s_{dx}/w_{dx})≤δ_{dx};
- finite justified bounds ℓ_{dxy}≤Pr_D(V=1 | d,x,y)≤u_{dxy} whenever a_{dxy}>0;
- exact linear equalities and closed weak inequalities on the joint mass arrays and any bounded auxiliary variables, including constraints that couple contexts or runs.

No row or conditional event value is inferred at a null denominator. An unsupported comparison may be represented by the full reference simplex and δ_{dx}=1, an unchecked event rate by [0,1], subject to independently given permitted trace support. All declared modes and constraints together are the chosen completion class, with an operational realization of each feasible array if exactness is claimed for physical completions. An independently verified outer inclusion without this realization yields sound outer bounds only. For an outcome letter that is a full visible trace of a fixed stochastic policy, its required action flows may be imposed on a full mass refinement by prefix equalities using the policy's genuine declared conditional kernels. Merely imposing one-step frequencies across the whole roster does not enforce these flows.

1. The mass representation in each mode is a compact polytope using H_{dx}s_{dx}≤h_{dx}w_{dx}, a linear lift of Σ_y|a_{dxy}−s_{dxy}|≤2δ_{dx}w_{dx}, and ℓ_{dxy}a_{dxy}≤q_{dxy1}≤u_{dxy}a_{dxy}. Auxiliary TV variables may be constrained to [0,1]. Conditional normalization at positive denominators and arbitrary choices of null rows give the reverse representation.
2. Put r_d(z)=Σ_{x,y}q_{dxy1} and R(z)=max_{d∈D}r_d(z). For each nonempty mode the exact image of R is a closed interval [L_m,U_m]. Compute L_m by a single epigraph linear program and U_m by maximizing each r_d and taking the largest value. The full exact risk image is the union of the nonempty intervals. Both extrema of each interval have completion witnesses. For a threshold τ, the target is PASS iff R≤τ and FAIL iff R>τ. If every mode is empty, return INCONSISTENT. Otherwise uniform PASS means max_m U_m≤τ, uniform FAIL means min_m L_m>τ, and every other case has PASS and FAIL witnesses.
3. Suppose K≥1 registered primitive Bernoulli frequencies (including indicators of a categorical result) are individually estimated in faithful, independently repeated, prespecified fixed batches. In cell i, each observation has the same actual mean and the batch size n_i≥1. Cross-cell independence is unnecessary. On a common event of probability at least 1−α, the absolute errors for all cells are at most ρ_i=sqrt(log(2K/α)/(2n_i)), for 0<α<1. A registered reference-event estimate at the corresponding setting can supply deployment-event bounds by clipping its confidence interval expanded on both sides by an independently established bound η on the difference of the two conditional event probabilities at that positive-mass cell. Reference outcome rows, weight restrictions and event bounds may use such intervals when the sampled frequencies are justified to constrain the designated quantities. The confidence claim supposes actual source/deployment membership in an admitted mode before statistical restrictions and validity of the frozen bridges, readouts, policy and reset assumptions. If each outcome-dependent restriction used is valid on that common event, any selection from this registered family preserves actual membership there, and the joint envelope and homogeneous threshold conclusion are valid with probability at least 1−α. Wrong issuance has unconditional probability at most α. For rational input, outward rational rounding of the confidence limits preserves this guarantee. An empty or mixed set can lead to abstention. The claim does not guarantee that a certificate will be issued.

## M03.2 · Minimum sufficient independently checked anchor facts

Fix the M03.1 base class. Each anchor read is eligible, carried out faithfully, leaves the tested deployment target unchanged, and returns a fact from a known finite family of closed linear constraints in each mode. Facts can be mode-specific and can exclude an entire mode. They constrain recorded implementation facts or probability cells through externally checked evidence. Sum the nonnegative costs c_i of acquisition and verification for each distinct read, with the two line items available separately. Deployment execution cost is a different quantity. With multiple noisy reads, every statistical claim below requires simultaneous coverage as in M03.1 or an independently supplied replacement.

1. For a fixed jointly consistent vector of realized facts F_i from m_a anchor reads, consider a proposed certificate of a specified label ℓ in {PASS, FAIL} using a subset J. Let an opposing completion z mean a member of the base class with the other actual label, and D(z)={i:z violates F_i in its mode}. Then J certifies ℓ if and only if it intersects every such D(z). Cost minimization for the realized vector is exactly the weighted hitting-set optimum, including infinity when an opposing completion violates no fact. An algorithm alternates minimum-cost hitting set on the collected patterns and an LP-based search for a real opposing completion consistent with its selected facts. A returned opposing completion supplies a new pattern disjoint from the selected set. When one exists, the algorithm returns a minimum-cost sufficient subset, with at most 2^{m_a} different patterns ever added. LP witnesses and optimality certificates are in the represented class, with the strict threshold side handled as specified in M03.1. This is a retrospective certificate problem with the realized facts as input.
2. For prospective deterministic acquisition, fix also the jointly admissible response tuples over the catalog. In each mode and tuple their compatibility is given by a closed polyhedron contained in the intersection of the tuple's facts, and all such compatible possibilities are admitted. Their projections may overlap. Every base completion has at least one admitted tuple. Each read is available once and its cost is fixed. The hidden deployment array and its full admissible response tuple are fixed throughout the audit. At an observed partial tuple, keep all feasible mode, array and tuple possibilities consistent with the actual recorded prefix. The optimum worst-path cost of exact homogeneous classification is zero at a nonempty homogeneous state; at any other nonempty state it is the minimum over unread i of c_i plus the maximum optimum continuation cost over i's feasible next outcomes. A mixed state with no remaining certifying strategy has value infinity. The recurrence yields an optimal finite decision tree if a solution exists. It characterizes classification for every admitted tuple, which is a stronger requirement than confidence coverage at selected transcripts.
3. If a faithful target-resolving direct read is additionally available at constant cost d_0≥0 at every reachable state including the root, the combined optimum in part 2 is min(d_0,W), where W is the semantic-only optimum. Strict semantic-only advantage over this direct read holds exactly when W<d_0. This statement uses worst supported path cost, exact direct readout, and unchanged future interface and target.
