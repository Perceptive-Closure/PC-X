## M02.1 Staged exact completion and interventions

Let \(U_i=\bigcup_{j\le i} B_j\) and \(V_i=\bigcup_{j\ge i}B_j\). By the interval property their overlap is \(B_i\). The past factors and historical readback equations use only \(U_i\). All future factors and future registered readback constraints use only \(V_i\). Two solutions on the respective sides agree on the overlap exactly when their restrictions to \(B_i\) agree. Gluing them gives one complete immutable assignment. Thus for any proposed future legal reply history its feasibility is precisely the existence of a suffix solution whose \(B_i\) restriction belongs to \(R_i\). This also proves that label sets are exact, since \(t\) is in the overlap and compatibility requires the same \(t\).

Compute the suffix relation \(W_i=\pi_{B_i}(\bigjoin S_i)\) by backward elimination, filling absent coordinates with their declared domains. The exact current posterior projected to \(B_i\) is \(R_i\cap W_i\). At the root test this intersection for inconsistency. The equality constraint for reply \(a\) filters \(R_i\), and only replies whose filtered relation intersects \(W_i\) are feasible. A same-stage successor retains that filtered relation. To advance, slide one bag at a time, existentially project onto the shared coordinates, extend newly introduced variables over their domains and join factors assigned at the next bag. This also applies to public updates that jump across stages. Single-interval occurrence ensures a forgotten variable can never constrain later bags again. The resulting relation is precisely \(R_j\) at the new state. Neither the suffix feasibility check nor a change of menus can add a hidden observation to the model.

For nonempty posterior, stop at zero remaining cost if its label is homogeneous. Otherwise take the minimum over legal query occurrences of the query price plus the maximum value over all feasible replies. With no certifying continuation the value is infinity. All stops and choices in the recurrence depend on \((z,R_i)\) alone. Evaluating states in reverse public-graph order proves the optimum by induction. The deterministic recurrence accounts for exact certainty under any randomization as well, since finite choices, replies and source assignments permit fixing a private plan succeeding for all completions when success and budget hold with certainty.

There are at most \(|Z|2^K\) pairs of public state and relation. Each finite join, projection, reply test and update costs polynomial in \(L,K\). For instance each new bag and its separator can be scanned using tables of at most \(K\) rows, and enumerating matching row pairs costs at most \(K^2\). A certificate records suffix relations, reachability and update equations, feasible reply sets, exact optimum checks and a minimizing choice at every finite mixed state. A finite value is a sum of at most \(|Z|\) input prices, so its bit length is polynomial in \(L\), independently of the numerical budget. Exact class nonemptiness and witnesses follow by replaying the constrained forward and backward joins, choosing a matching row and backtracking the two sides. An absent label has no row with its value in the intersection. An opposite-label pair uses separate glued assignments, and a singleton includes a separate nonemptiness check. This proves the claimed \( |Z|2^K\operatorname{poly}(L,K)\) bound and FPT when \(K\le q^w\) for maximum domain \(q\) and bag cardinality \(w\).

The public state can change the optimum with identical projections. With one hidden label record, let two public-only advancement commands with constant replies lead to distinct states, each with an exact read of that record. Price the read at \(1\) in the first and \(2\) in the second. The posterior is unchanged, while remaining costs differ. For dynamic inaccessibility without a universal indistinguishable pair, use distinct named measurable coordinates \(u,v\) constrained to \((0,0),(1,0),(0,1)\), and target \(u\lor v\). At the root one may read either coordinate, after which no further queries are legal. Each opposite pair is split by a legal one-read protocol. Each root query also has a zero-reply branch containing both classes, and stopping immediately fails. This is valid with a single bag containing \(u,v,t\).

For a fixed intervention mask retain all original response and update computations. Add a deletion price \(d_{zq}=0\) if the query is kept and \(+\infty\) if it is deleted to each query term in the shared value DAG. The same recurrence with \(\min,\max,+\) now works with these encoded inputs. Raw numeric Boolean leaves in \(\{0,1\}\) with only those operations do not suffice on a literal reading. With one necessary unit-price bit read the desired value is \(1\) at keep \(1\) and \(+\infty\) at keep \(0\), whereas every such raw expression is nondecreasing in its input. The deletion-price encoding or an explicit keep guard gives the finite expression of the stated size.

A budget-feasible proper audit under the mask has identical reachable histories when restricted to its own used queries, including any permanently kept ones. Thus sufficiency holds exactly when one budget-feasible audit support is contained in the kept set. When only some queries are variable, use the projections of the supports to those removable occurrences, with the permanent ones understood. Minimal supports and inclusion-minimal sufficient sets coincide. A deletion destroys feasibility exactly when it hits every minimal support. An empty support makes destruction impossible, and an empty family of supports makes the empty deletion sufficient. The DAG evaluation bound provides no bound on enumerating the minimal sets.

## M02.2 Route and gate realization

Use opposite-target worlds \(0,1\), open history \(h_o\) compatible with both, closed \(h_c\) compatible with \(0\), and two unused representatives with empty direct compatibility. The entry has \(h_o\), discrete certificate equality, and a fixed authority set including all registered site atoms. The guard of atom \(j\) enables it only at that entry when \(r=j\). Its sole positive successor changes admission to \(h_c\). For \(g_j=1\) it also merges the two unused equality representatives. Its cost is one and there are no actions at its endpoint. The registry, entry, \(\omega\), support weights and compatibility can be common to the family. The unique entry atom is E-only if \(g_r=0\) and touches both E and R if \(g_r=1\). Thus the same-entry R-freeze threshold is \(1-g_r\).

The audit reads immutable, independently declared site and route records through the prescribed menus. It advances permission only at the separate public audit state and leaves the plant unchanged. With static commands no cheap command can be legal on every resulting transcript, since every route can occur with every gate vector. Omitting site \(j\) leaves \((r=j,g=0)\) and \((r=j,g=e_j)\) indistinguishable with opposite targets. Omitting the route leaves \((r=j,g=e_j)\) and \((r=k,g=e_j)\) for \(k\ne j\) indistinguishable with opposite targets. These witnesses also show the separate operational content despite Hamming-weight correlation. Reading every required record attains \(a+\sum_j c_j\).

The adaptive strategy reads \(r\) then the cheap instrument for that precise site, costing \(a+s\). Some path must use a route read, since on a fixed nonzero gate vector all gate answers agree across differing routes. If the first command is a gate read it costs at least \(s\) on every path, including a route-using one. If the first command is the route read, the posterior for each route still includes its zero and unit gate vectors, so a further gate read of price at least \(s\) is necessary. Unit-scale choices give a growing gap.

## M02.3 Matched physical family and noisy lower bound

Use the same types of histories, compatibility and common discrete entry equality as above. Declare a fast action of cost one, guarded at the entry by \(x_0=1\). It goes straight to \(h_c\) and removes itself from the extensional permission set. Its mask is \(\{E,A\}\). Declare also a slow entrance of cost one which merges the two unused representative classes and leaves admission and permissions fixed, with mask \(\{R\}\). It advances to raw location \(0\). At each raw location \(i\) two registered advance atoms have costs \(1\) and \(1+2^i\). Exactly the atom matching \(x_i\) is enabled. It advances to \(i+1\) leaving all three resource fields unchanged. At location \(d\) a last action of cost one changes admission to \(h_c\). Every admitted action has a unique deterministic successor. Location guards disable all actions at closed endpoints. Every completion has precisely this registry and entry. In R freeze the slow entrance is removed and the threshold is \(x_0\). In A freeze only the slow path can close, and its cost is \(2+d+\sum_i 2^i x_i\). This value is injective on the product class. Each instrument reads the installed bit at its own source locator without mutation.

In the exact noiseless model, varying a coordinate never read at a terminal history keeps all answers there fixed. To report identity every path must measure all \(d\) coordinates. To report the threshold \(x_0\) every path must measure \(x_0\). The respective exact query lists attain \(d\) and \(1\).

For the noisy upper bounds, use majority after \(n\) independent calls per relevant bit, where \(n\) is an integer with \(n\ge \log(m/\delta)/(2(1/2-\eta)^2)\) for \(m\) relevant bits. Each bit's error is at most \(\delta/m\) by the one-sided Hoeffding bound and a union bound controls vector recovery. This gives bounded worst-path cost \(O_\eta(m\log(m/\delta))\) with \(m=1,d\). [Hoeffding's bounded-sum inequality](https://www.cs.rpi.edu/academics/courses/spring06/random/hoefding.pdf)

For the lower bounds put the independent uniform prior on all \(d\) bits. Include the auditor's private random seed in its terminal record. Given that seed and the complete realized finite transcript, every choice and stop constraint is fixed by the transcript, so its likelihood in the latent bits is proportional to the product of the observed channels, grouped by bit. The posterior remains a product even with data-dependent choices and stopping. Write \(N_i\) for bit \(i\)'s call count, \(N=\sum_i N_i\) and \(\rho=\log((1-\eta)/\eta)>0\). The absolute log odds of bit \(i\) are at most \(\rho N_i\), so its optimal conditional error is \(e_i\ge(1/2)e^{-\rho N_i}\). Consequently target error at most \(\delta\) implies by Jensen \(\delta\ge (1/2)\exp(-\rho\mathbb E N_0)\), giving \(\mathbb E N\ge \rho^{-1}\log(1/(2\delta))\).

For identity the best conditional success is \(\prod_i(1-e_i)\). Using \(1-u\le e^{-u}\) and Jensen over the bit counts, its error is at least
\[
F(N)=1-\exp\!\left(-\frac d2\exp(-\rho N/d)\right).
\]
Uniform success implies \(\mathbb E F(N)\le\delta\). Thus \(F(N)\le2\delta\) with prior-and-audit probability at least one half. On this event
\[
N\ \ge\ \frac d\rho\log\!\left(\frac{d}{2[-\log(1-2\delta)]}\right).
\]
Since \(-\log(1-2\delta)\le 8\delta/3\) and \(d/\delta\ge8\), the lower bound on that event is at least a positive constant times \(d\log(d/\delta)/\rho\). For instance \(\log(d/\delta)-\log(16/3)\ge(1-\log(16/3)/\log 8)\log(d/\delta)\). The worst expected cost dominates the prior mean, and the worst-path bound dominates the worst expected cost. The lower bounds apply to both, so together with the fixed-batch upper bounds they establish the two claimed orders. This joint posterior argument is needed for the dependence on \(d\) at fixed confidence. Summing neighboring-input single-bit testing bounds alone gives only the separate \(\log(1/\delta)\) scale per bit.

## M02.4 Succinct exact boundaries

For the pair predicate a certificate consists of two polynomial-length latent vectors satisfying the compatibility circuit with different evaluated target values. Given a Boolean satisfiability instance \(\varphi(u)\), take the product class of \((b,u)\) and target \(t=b\wedge\varphi(u)\). A witness with \(b=0\) is known. Implement the target by the same open/closed admission construction, with one E-only unit-cost physical closer whose ordinary guard at the entry is \(b\wedge\varphi(u)\). A true guard supplies its unique closed successor, and a false guard leaves the well-formed open entry actionless. An opposite-target pair exists exactly when \(\varphi\) is satisfiable. Membership and the reduction prove NP-completeness on the stated restricted family, and its zero-query singleton complement is coNP-complete on the nonempty domain. [Cook's satisfiability reduction](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~15455/resources/Cook1971-complx-thm-proof.pdf)

For PSPACE membership, exhaust legal actions and their feasible responses depth-first along a transcript of length at most unary \(H\). To check response feasibility or posterior label homogeneity, enumerate all polynomial-length latent vectors with a polynomial-sized counter, replaying the circuits along the stored path. At a mixed state, at least one legal affordable query must have all feasible continuations succeed. Prices are nonnegative and have polynomial-sized accumulated arithmetic. With polynomial path depth this exhaustive alternating search uses polynomial deterministic space.

For hardness start with a closed quantified Boolean formula with \(m\) stages and matrix \(\varphi\). Take an immutable product latent vector of one bit per universally quantified variable and a separate target record bit \(b\). The same-entry physical unit-cost E-only closer is guarded by \(b\), so the realized R-freeze threshold is \(b\). The audit register stores stage and all variable values that have been committed. At an existential quantifier exactly two named commands are legal, each returning a fixed licensed acknowledgement and publicly recording its own selected bit. At a universal quantifier one named read is legal, measuring the corresponding fixed latent bit and storing the answer. At the end, the ordinary public guard for the separate read of the installed \(b\) is the polynomial circuit \(\varphi\) on the stored values. The commands are pure with respect to plant and target. The final response measures a specified independent record, whose deployment effect follows from the physical guard. Before that response both \(b\) values are consistent with the full transcript. Set horizon and budget to \(m+1\) with unit prices. All future universal outcomes are possible until read, through fixed product assignments. Thus a winning quantified existential strategy is exactly a proper policy that reaches the final target-fact read on every branch. The register and circuits have polynomial size, and depth increases on each call. [Stockmeyer and Meyer's quantified-formula completeness, Theorem 4.3](https://people.csail.mit.edu/meyer/meyer-stockmeyer-word-probs.pdf)

The accepted static binary-budget subset-sum obstruction uses its static opposed-score oracle. It remains a different boundary from the succinct dynamic rank search. ## M02.5 Certified partial expansion and approximation boundary

Every local exact recurrence is monotone in its child values, so replacing unknown values at any frontier with valid lower and upper endpoints bounds the root. This requires keeping all legal query choices and their exact feasible branches at each expanded node. Suppose a formerly unexpanded node had an independent bound \([l,u]\). Its newly expanded raw recurrence interval may be wider. Intersect that interval with \([l,u]\) at the node and retain the bound in future expansions. New bounds at every old node then tighten by a reverse-order induction, so the root interval tightens. As a concrete widening case, a unit-cost fixed-reply advance leads to a mixed state with only a cost-four revealing read. An independently checked root interval \([5,5]\) is replaced by \([1,\infty]\) if the child starts at \([0,\infty]\) and the root interval is discarded. An exact root terminal value or a minimizing action on finite upper endpoints, with realizing policies at every relied-on finite frontier upper bound, reconstructs a proper audit of cost at most \(U\). If a retained independent upper bound sets the answer at an expanded state, its policy witness is also needed for this reconstruction. \(0<L\le\mathrm{OPT}\le U\) gives the certified factor \(U/L\).

For the succinct boundary augment the quantified-formula reduction by a distinct authorized direct measurement of the same installed \(b\), available at the root and at every nonstopping depth before the bound. It terminates acquisition with the record at price \(M=2^N\), where \(N\) is the quantified-formula input length. The binary price uses \(N+1\) bits. Keep the unary horizon \(m+1\). A true formula permits the cheap policy of price \(m+1\), and the optimum is positive because \(b\) is initially ambiguous. For a false formula any proper policy must use the new read on some supported path, or else it would be a proper policy in the original false-formula instance. The optimum is exactly \(M\) by taking the direct read at the root. The reduction length \(L\) is polynomial in \(N\). For every fixed polynomial \(p\), eventually \((m+1)p(L)<M\). A polynomial-time estimator guaranteed to lie in \([\mathrm{OPT},p(L)\mathrm{OPT}]\) distinguishes the two cases by whether its value is below \(M\). The remaining bounded input sizes are finite and can be decided directly. A polynomial-time optimizer with such a certified output would give the same decision procedure. This argument preserves exact source validity, constant target criterion, and positive finite optimum in both cases.
