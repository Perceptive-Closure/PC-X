# M02 · Primary-source comparisons

## Width, exact inference and optimization

- **Dechter (1999), Theorems 1–2.** Adaptive consistency processes an ordered constraint network into equivalent tractable-to-replay constraints. The induced width controls the size of intermediate elimination buckets, and time and space are exponential in that width. M02.1's prefix projection and suffix table are standard variable elimination specialized to a supplied path of exact factor scopes. A single observed transcript or pair query benefits from that mechanism directly. The outer minimax policy must compare many possible exact posterior boundary relations, which yields a separate powerset bound. A one-call elimination bound alone does not give an exact adaptive optimum. [Bucket Elimination](https://ics.uci.edu/~csp/r76A.pdf)
- **Bryant (1986), §3 and the Apply algorithm.** Fix a variable order and use reduced ordered Boolean functions to obtain canonical graph representations. For Boolean combination, the graph operations can exploit sharing and traversal of graph-node pairs. Such a graph can store the compatibility/target relation and readback restrictions, and can substantively compete with the explicit bag table on fixed-order problems. Graph size and intermediate growth depend on order and representation, and a graph for one conditioned relation does not by itself synthesize the best contingent policy. In M02.1 the explicit register and bag relation track dynamic queries and tariff differences. No speed comparison against optimized decision-diagram implementations has been made. [Graph-Based Algorithms for Boolean Function Manipulation](https://www.cs.tufts.edu/comp/150FP/archive/randall-bryant/bdds.pdf)
- **Krause and Guestrin (2005), Theorems 1–2.** Chain conditional independence permits an exact dynamic program for conditional observation plans, splitting an interval at a selected observation and combining local Bayesian rewards. Their smoothing bound for budget \(B\) uses order \(d^3 B^2 n^3\) local expected reward evaluations, with sharper cases for filtering or no budget. This is a substantial established specialization of structured adaptive acquisition. M02.1 uses a set relation and the exact remaining worst-path cost of a certain fixed-target report. It allows supplied history-dependent lawful menus and prices, and requires a nondecreasing path of query scopes. The two models have different objective and interface assumptions. The chain result does not directly give the same width bound or a checkable whole-occurrence intervention result. [Optimal Nonmyopic Value of Information in Graphical Models](https://las.inf.ethz.ch/files/krause05optimal.pdf)
- **Bonet and Geffner (2013).** Their causal decomposition tracks beliefs for deterministic planning with sensing over local causally relevant variables. It separates space exponential in causal width from exact-completeness conditions and develops a smaller incomplete approximation. M02.1 uses a different explicit interval-separator premise and strict preservation of source facts during audit. Equality of separator projection is sufficient for exact whole-transcript and target feasibility by a direct gluing argument. The planning result addresses more general effects and illustrates that local summaries are not automatically jointly realizable. M02.1's result combines exact tracking with the stated worst-path identification synthesis. [Causal Belief Decomposition for Planning with Sensing](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2013/336)

## Adaptive identification, target regions and interventions

- **Chandrasekaran, Karp, Moreno-Centeno and Vempala (2011).** An implicit hitting-set oracle either confirms that a presented set hits a hidden hypergraph or returns a missed edge. The finite refinement algorithm supports exact optimization on a set of learned edges. This directly covers the accepted T14 static methodology once the paired source oracle is available. Dynamic query exclusivity can make each individual opposite pair distinguishable while no uniform audit exists. Hence the one missed-pair oracle does not certify the same problem. In M02.1 a validated value table proves feasibility or obstruction against each legal command and its feasible ongoing branches. Its occurrence blocker duality still uses the elementary static hitting interpretation of fixed deletion masks. [Algorithms for Implicit Hitting Set Problems](https://karthik.ise.illinois.edu/pubs/implicit-hitting-set.pdf)
- **Golovin, Krause and Ray (2010), Theorems 3–4; Javdani et al. (2014), Theorem 1.** For equivalence classes, EC2 gives a Bayesian expected-cost approximation for a finite test menu under its priors and noise assumptions. Decision-region determination characterizes when all consistent hypotheses fit inside one possibly overlapping region through exact cut hyperedges and also develops expected-cost approximation. Label constancy in M02.1 is the special case of disjoint decision regions. Prior expected objectives and the all-tests comparison are different from a uniformly correct worst-path policy with noncommuting public query permits. These papers identify serious competitors under their applicability conditions. They do not give M02.5's universal approximation ratio in the succinct public regime, and M02.5 places no lower bound on their applicable subproblems. [Near-Optimal Bayesian Active Learning with Noisy Observations](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2010/file/1e6e0a04d20f50967c64dac2d639a577-Paper.pdf) · [Near Optimal Bayesian Active Learning for Decision Making](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v33/javdani14.pdf)
- **Hyafil and Rivest (1976).** The input lists objects and their binary test responses, and the sum of their identification depths gives the expected uniform-prior objective. Optimizing that tree is NP-complete. The accepted PC-X binary numerical pair obstruction instead concerns a compact fixed-interface oracle. M02.4's PSPACE bound concerns succinct public dynamics with exact uniform correctness after deterministic reads, even when individual fact membership is simple. The different objective and representation prevent reading one classification as the other. M02.2 adds an exact governed, correlated and authorized-cost realization to the standard route/access separation. [Constructing Optimal Binary Decision Trees Is NP-Complete](https://www.cmi.ac.in/~madhavan/courses/dmml2025/literature/HyafilRivest-ConstructingOptimalBinaryDecisionTreesIsNPComplete.pdf)

## Noise and confidence

- **Hoeffding (1963), Theorem 2.** A bounded independent sum satisfies a subgaussian upper tail with exponent controlled by the squares of its summand ranges. The bitwise majority and union bound in M02.3 are direct standard consequences. [Probability Inequalities for Sums of Bounded Random Variables](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830)
- **Feige, Raghavan, Peleg and Upfal (1994), §2 including Theorem 2.8.** A noisy Boolean decision tree receives independent local corruption when querying a coordinate. The depth needed for parity on \(d\) inputs has a \(d\log(d/\delta)\) order at fixed positive noise and uniform failure bound. Full vector recovery yields parity without extra queries, so the bounded-worst-path lower order in M02.3 has direct prior derivations. The main result here persists as the operationally injective authorization construction and matched target/identity objectives. [Computing with Noisy Information](https://cadmo.ethz.ch/education/lectures/HS18/SAADS/papers/computing_noisy_information.pdf)
- **Evans and Pippenger (1998), §§3–5.** Under the uniform product input law and fixed independent noise, the conditional input law at a noisy decision tree leaf factorizes. Their §4 inequality bounds the largest conditional probability of one vector by \(\exp[-(d/2)\exp(-\lambda C/d)]\) for a leaf reached with \(C\) queries. This is the principal quantitative inequality used in M02.3, across adaptive stopping, and their parity result gives prior expected complexity bounds. M02.3 gives an elementary direct use for the full-vector report and a Markov argument for the same worst-expected uniform-confidence order. The logical argument does not extend automatically to a correlated source prior or correlated measurement noise. No priority claim is made for the statistical rate. [Average-Case Lower Bounds for Noisy Boolean Decision Trees](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/tr/1996/TR-96-19_0.pdf)

## Succinct complexity and approximability

- **Papadimitriou and Tsitsiklis (1987), Theorem 6.** The finite-horizon partially observed expected-cost problem is PSPACE-complete under their bounded horizon condition. QBF alternation is implemented by decisions and observed stochastic transitions. M02.4 specializes the alternation to immutable independent records, deterministic pure measurements, and authorization registers whose final eligibility gate enables an independent target read. Its thresholds are uniform worst-path constraints, and its circuit public encoding is essential to the stated comparison with M02.1. The opposing-pair lower bound is separately ordinary satisfiability of a compact source gate. [The Complexity of Markov Decision Processes](https://web.mit.edu/jnt/www/Papers/J016-87-mdp-complexity.pdf)
- **Rintanen (2004).** The paper separates planning complexity across observation and nondeterminism models and uses alternation to obtain conditional-planning lower bounds. The general succinct partially observed problem with much richer nondeterministic state effects can reach doubly exponential complexity. The shorter bounded-horizon reduction here uses only choices in the public audit instructions and measurements of fixed latent records. The broader planning result neither proves PSPACE membership in that broader class nor supplies the precisely restricted hardness embedding. [Complexity of Planning with Partial Observability](https://gki.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/papers/rintanen-icaps04.pdf)
- **Lusena, Goldsmith and Mundhenk (2001).** Their variants of partially observable policy design admit reductions that separate achievable reward values so a constant-factor or constant-error polynomial algorithm would collapse complexity classes. M02.5 uses the same established gap-reduction method, with a binary-priced direct fallback that keeps both exact audit optima positive and finite. It bounds a different minimization objective, with deterministic reads and pure dynamic public tariffs. It leaves open approximation under extra structural promises and gives a finite checkable instance-dependent interval via the monotone recurrence. [Nonapproximability Results for Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs/project/jair/pub/volume14/lusena01a.pdf)

## Scientific scope

The exact staged solver is an adaptation and a finite-scope strengthening of the accepted PC-X structural audit result. The width promise, dynamic-obstruction witness and resource-grounded availability interface are the specific mathematical application. The routing and noisy-rate arguments specialize established decision-tree mathematics to operationally meaningful records and independent evidence. Succinct hardness and approximation gaps use standard techniques grounded in these interfaces. Priority beyond the examined formulations remains NOVELTY_UNRESOLVED. There is no claim of generic superiority over bucket elimination, ordered decision diagrams, prior-aware selection methods or full supported planning.
