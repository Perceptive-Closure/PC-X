## Exact local commands

From the repository root run **python -B THEORY-GENERALIZATION/WORKSTREAMS/M02/check_m02.py**. It compares the factored recurrence with the full-assignment transcript tree, including inconsistent classes, forward skipped bags, and observable prices represented as large binary integers. It enumerates complete occurrence masks on a fixed correlated source and checks minimal-support feasibility. Separate controls enumerate exact static and adaptive routed costs, calculate rational posterior factorizations under a specified adaptive stopping rule, and compare a succinct permission gate to a bottom-up QBF evaluator. The observed output is **256 factored audit instances, 1024 whole-query masks, 4 exact route cases, 168 exact stopped posteriors, and 2048 QBF gates**. The last controls check both the case with no fallback and the binary-price fallback. These are deterministic finite checks of calculations. They do not establish the general statistical or complexity bounds.

Run **lean THEORY-GENERALIZATION/WORKSTREAMS/M02/Separator.lean** with Lean 4.21.0. The kernel checks **PCX.M02.projection_preserves_feasibility** and **PCX.M02.same_projection_same_reply** and reports no axioms. These are abstract gluing and exact feasibility implications under the supplied key agreement and gluing hypotheses. The concrete scope conditions, minimized audit recurrence, complexity and stochastic theorems remain written proofs.
