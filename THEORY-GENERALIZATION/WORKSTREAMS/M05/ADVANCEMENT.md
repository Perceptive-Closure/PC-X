## T18 · Accepted input anchor

T18 constructs a common submeasure on finite canonical histories from approved observation and action correspondences, independent event semantics, shared public policy and lower bounds on primitive rows conditional on the appropriate full prefix. This supplies legitimate linear lower bounds when the decoded canonical event/row maps and support obligations apply. Exact shared normalized rows can instantiate exact comparison arms. A lower subprobability alone supplies partial mass constraints, so it belongs in T25.J's bounded-row representation when applicable. T26.C requires the complete native conditional distributions for the comparison arms. The workstream makes no claim to recover those rows from passive equality of tapes or to discover a generalized T18 anchor. Optional caps on missed mass and conditional false negatives and a false-positive floor cut the two-dimensional rectangle. Sharp worst-context endpoints need the corresponding context risks to be jointly attainable. The baseline supplies direct arithmetic bounds in this scope. T25.J replaces the freely varying residual cells and simultaneous marginal endpoint premise with a complete source-checked finite union of joint conditional occupancy polytopes. The original table is the specialization with one tree per context, flag and audit/unknown truth draws, fixed audited submasses and no cross-cell or cross-context cuts besides the optional T25 caps. The extra guarantee is exact joint feasible laws including correlated constraints, attainable conditional false-negative ranges even with null denominators in other completions, and the sharp shared worst-context extrema. When a trusted record is unavailable for a particular arm, its truth mass remains constrained only by the admitted model and other actual evidence.

## T26 · Fidelity through event-resolved coupling

The accepted T26.a compares a finite controlled kernel to a source-approved projection of its instrumented form. It uses the same admitted observational policy and lawful choices on matched histories, initial and matched-row TV caps, and terminal agreement on histories that remain matched. The product survival bound works without time-step independence and can operate with local upper bounds on projected discrepancy. T26.C removes the required common action policy, step-for-step projection match and absorbing mismatch penalty for the stated exact-row class. Two named lawful policies can consume different authorized observations, have different physical states and take distinct actions. The certificate optimizes the actual terminal event agreement under given history-conditional coupling constraints. The new class can certify probe-dependent policies with identical independently scored event risk. It can also reject a perfect terminal pairing that reshapes a native conditional law. With source-controlled matching and independently true terminal agreement on that match, the T26 coupling is a feasible witness when the new instance includes its exact native rows and permits its paired support. Its product formula then remains an upper bound. The exact native input requirements of T26.C are stronger than T26's use of TV bounds, so the two results cover overlapping operational settings.

## T27 · Conditional local and global guarantees on the same class

T27.a supplies a sharp clipped sum from upper bounds on the uncovered global event, each isolated violation and each directed retention allowance. The sharpness argument uses arbitrary disjoint violation atoms and separately admissible two-point isolated laws. T27 also gives a finite event containment check over fully supported terminal labels. T27.J replaces the abstract budget-only summary with independently justified joint transition/support constraints, event patterns and optional isolated row comparisons. It places no containment tolerance or local-retention cap in the premise of the exact global risk solver. Both can be additional evidence if legitimately established, or target tests about the shared law. The solver optimizes the global event jointly with local overlap and conditional event data. The eight-ticket model provides a strict separation at the same risk threshold even though exact containment and zero drift fail as universal claims. A conditional isolated cap still requires its own context evidence. Computation recovers the baseline's sharp sum if its independent abstract budgets exhaust the information available and its disjoint-atom witnesses remain permissible.

## Unresolved obligations

The exact finite results leave open independently verifiable recovery of these contracts from incomplete raw source, compact completion representations for unknown shared nonlinear parameters, uniform confidence with dependent or adaptively acquired label evidence, and end-to-end integration with policies chosen from the same observations under evidence and resource budgets. An independently validated linear confidence region can be used conditional on its coverage event, while deriving that event remains a separate statistical task. No scalar gate assigns a probability to an impossible completion or fills in a hidden label from an alarm.
