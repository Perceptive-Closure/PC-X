# M03 finite witnesses and failed weakenings

## Conditional transport with genuine uncertainty

A single authorized run labels each trace by letter y∈{0,1} and an independently scored event b. The physical event can be an independently observed completion slip. Source checks identify the stated admissible joint trace support, and every law satisfying the restrictions below is realizable by choosing a ticket before the fixed policy executes. Let the reference probability of y=1 be in [1/8,3/8] and let the independently supported TV bridge be 1/8. The deployment probability p of y=1 can range from 0 to 1/2. Let genuine deployment event rates be in [1/8,1/4] for y=0 and [1/2,3/4] for y=1. The exact risk image is [1/8,1/2]. Its endpoints use (p,γ₀,γ₁)=(0,1/8,1/2) and (1/2,1/4,3/4), respectively. The unused event rate on the first endpoint remains free. No common trajectory distribution or precise event-rate table was needed.

These widths can arise with three fixed calibration batches and α=1/20. For y, let the reference count be 50 of 200, with outward rounded radius 1/8. For the reference event following independently sampled y=0, take 768 of 4096 with radius 1/32 and a checked event-rate shift allowance 1/32. For y=1 take 640 of 1024 with radius 1/16 and shift allowance 1/16. The stipulated outward radii dominate the three Hoeffding values. These counts are an exact mathematical fixture. They are conditional pilot readouts, neither claims about an observed installation nor evidence for the bridges.

Add a latent unseen context with unknown deployment mass in [0,1/5], risk completely free and no observation that enters its support. The exact risk bound becomes [1/10,3/5]. Endpoint choices use maximal unseen mass and its event rates 0 and 1, respectively. Even a perfect covered estimate would leave opposite-threshold completions if the uncovered contributions cross the threshold. An observed sample that always stayed in the old context supplies no structural absence claim about the deployed context.

## Target witnesses with a joint acquisition bottleneck

Let two deployed contexts have occupancies 1/2 and let a,b be their event masses. The admitted class is a,b∈[0,1/2], a+b≤3/4. This coupling can be a verified cap on the proportion of episode tickets flagged by the independently fixed scorer. Univariate worst-case events could suggest risk one; the true maximum is 3/4. At τ=1/4 take three available already observed facts.

| Read | Realized primitive condition | Acquisition | Verification | Sum |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | a≤1/8 | 1/2 | 1/2 | 1 |
| B | b≤1/8 | 1/2 | 1/2 | 1 |
| C | a+b≤1/4 by an independent ticket-inventory cap | 2 | 1 | 3 |

All three hold at (1/8,1/8), a passing boundary. A alone leaves (0,1/2), B alone leaves (1/2,0), and both are actual failing completions. A and B jointly give risk ≤1/4 at cost 2. C alone also certifies and costs 3. No full mechanism identity is required. Replacing C by an unavailable risk label or a self-reported success rate would make the example's interface premise false.

A different coupled roster has (r₁,r₂)=(u,1−u) with u∈[0,1]. Each marginal attains zero. Every completion's maximum risk is at least 1/2, attained at u=1/2. Using separate marginal minima for the roster would leave an infeasible witness. A mode with risk exactly 1/4 is PASS at τ=1/4 and cannot serve as a strict failing witness.

## Anchor facts chosen before the results

A hidden installed selector s∈{0,1} specifies which of two independent registry slots determines a mandatory deployed action's receiving protocol. Its contents b∈{0,1}, a receiving designation, determine whether the fixed mandatory action violates the physical rule. The modes (s,b) have deterministic risks b. A root read S yields s at cost 1. Read Qᵢ costs 2 and returns b when s=i, and an unavailable-slot marker * otherwise. Reading S followed by Q_s certifies at cost 3, and every nonadaptive set certifying all four modes contains both Q₀ and Q₁, costing at least 4. Reading Qᵢ first has a nonempty branch needing Q_{1−i}; reading S first needs another read. Thus 3 is the exact minimum worst cost. The independently read slot is a source fact.

With an exact direct read costing 7/2 the semantic protocol wins in worst cost by 1/2. With direct price 5/2 the direct measurement wins. Neither price is inferred from X12. To see why expected cost requires a separate calculation, consider a once-only cost-1 semantic read that reveals the installed word with probability 9/10 and returns * otherwise under both labels. Assume no other semantic route. A cost-5 exact direct read only on * solves every path and has worst expected cost 3/2, against cost 5 for direct alone and infinite for exact semantic-only acquisition. Its worst path costs 6. The min(d₀,W) result is a worst-path theorem.

## Coherence and fidelity boundaries

- Equal unconditional margins admit two traces with first bit fair, where the second repeats it in one completion and flips it in the other. The target event of two ones has probability 1/2 versus 0. Matching a one-step marginal supplies no matched conditional laws for this target.
- A passive public display can render the same receipt under two hidden serving states while an installed registry or an independently logged physical event changes the true event. A perturbed read that changes that state cannot be used as a law bridge for the original process without an independent fidelity argument. This is also the structural lesson of X09 and X11.
- Equality of two *unknown* conditional rows can be nonconvex in joint masses when their occupancies vary. For two contexts a completion having occupancies (1/4,3/4) and common event probability zero has event masses (0,0). A completion having occupancies (3/4,1/4) and common event probability one has event masses (3/4,1/4). Their midpoint has occupancies (1/2,1/2) and conditional event probabilities 3/4 and 1/4. Enforcing a shared unknown normalized row cannot generally be done just by treating independently lifted reference copies as identical evidence. Exact analysis outside the declared mass-polytopal class requires another representation or an outer-envelope qualification.
- A hard equality check among learned rare-cell rates can exclude the true world at positive probability. Uniform calibration radii cover only the registered, faithfully sampled means. A test gateway that replaces deployed labels or an unrepresented missing stratum deserves separate treatment even if every confidence interval is tight.
