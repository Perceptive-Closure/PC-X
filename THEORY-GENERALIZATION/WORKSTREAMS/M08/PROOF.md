## V. Concrete strengthening and limits

Use three jobs of base tariff 1 and extra tariff 1. The first job is fresh in both installations, with an auxiliary mode tag \(j\in\{0,1\}\). For \(j=0\) the last two freshness bits are \((0,1)\), and for \(j=1\) they are \((1,0)\). The first common fast job reports the independently declared tag during actual useful service; later receipts can be uninformative. With \(D=4,H=3\), both paths have intrinsic score 4. The public controller performs the first fast job and uses the receipt to choose the correct two remaining commands, taking zero paid reads and serving all jobs at cost 4. If the first receipt is constant and no informative reads exist, the second job must be slow, reveals nothing and leaves the third slow. Budget 4 is then infeasible although all paths are intrinsically positive. This is a genuine correlated work-and-information effect. The later fast guards change physical frozen options in two real jobs. The Cartesian replacement includes both stale modes simultaneously, changing the target.

For a mixed cell, take two jobs with base tariff 1 and slow premium 1. The first job is always fresh and emits a legitimate meter enabling a paid second-stage query, with no earlier queries. The second job's bit is unknown. \(D=2,H=3\). Without that query every transcript before a second-stage command is identical, so neither accurate NO nor a common affordable service is available. Paying the query, then doing fast for bit zero or issuing NO for bit one, realizes exact worst acquisition price equal to the quoted price. Both examples rely on real paid or work-based authorized records, with no free installed guards.

The proof considers threshold correctness, explicit witnesses, completeness, exact physical correspondence, optimum worst total read cost, conditional physical transfer, real service and a bounded representation simultaneously under the same online serial class. It broadens T30.S to correlations and useful feedback, changing the audit timing and objective explicitly. The fixed predeployment static result is T30.C separately. ## VI. Positive-risk and strategic scope obstruction

Consider an actual useful one-stage service always callable at unit cost, with a terminal side event \(V\) whose actual occurrence is independently well defined. Two permitted installed laws have \(P(V=1)=1/4\) and \(3/4\). All side-event alternatives are positive at both installations. The permitted evaluator and any admitted joint query/work policy record exactly the same commands, outcomes, prices and progress, omitting the side-event. It follows by induction on public histories that all such transcripts have the same law, including randomized decisions and common stopping, despite opposite risk labels at cutoff \(1/2\). Summing the two wrong-or-abstain probabilities gives at least one. Source support equality proves neither chance-regime membership nor useful-event risk beyond zero in this setting. To extend T30.P specify independently justified probability or risk bounds and a transfer proof. For T30.A and T30.U one needs either validated separation in the permitted complete record law for strategic responses, or a declared anchor that excludes such coupled possibilities. The impossibility includes informative physical operations only when their actual observed joint rows still coincide. It cannot be inferred from equal benchmark marginals alone.
