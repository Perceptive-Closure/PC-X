# M01 · Primary comparisons

## Exact generation, validation and abstraction

- **Clarke, Biere, Raimi and Zhu (2001), §3**. Represent a finite transition system's initial and transition relations propositionally. The length-\(k\) prefix formula joins the initial constraint and \(k\) adjacent transition constraints, and a property encoding selects bounded witnesses. This mechanism directly supplies the trusted-trace component of M01.1 once the Boolean transition relation is built. The source-slot-to-canonical-kernel equivalence, authorized instruments, and all eight finite proper contingent policy values remain separate obligations. The belief contraction bound concerns strategies rather than ordinary bounded traces. [Bounded Model Checking Using Satisfiability Solving](https://fmv.jku.at/papers/ClarkeBiereRaimiZhu-FMSD-19-1-2001.pdf)
- **Pnueli, Siegel and Singerman (1998)**. Put source and translated target into a common semantic framework and use a refinement relation with a simulation check to validate each translation. Their demonstration translates synchronous SIGNAL to sequential C. M01.1 supplies local validation equations for exactly evaluating one finite typed source with free interpreted slots, and its checker can re-evaluate a concrete interpretation. The inference over all slot assignments and preservation of explicit authorization counterfactuals are additional tasks. This author-institution primary record supplies the result outline. Broader equivalence to their full validation algorithm is unassessed. [Translation Validation](https://weizmann.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/translation-validation-2/)
- **Cousot and Cousot (1977), §§5–9**. Elementary program constructs act monotonically on a lattice of properties. The induced equations define global properties by fixed points. An abstract interpretation can approximate the concrete semantics through a consistency relation, including cases where finite calculation is not exact. For M01.1 the source domain is fully finite and expanded, and sound negative and positive identification refer to one exact concrete Boolean denotation. The outer containment check is a finite application of the same soundness distinction. Expanding the exact transition system and proving both licensed feasibility and membership are the additional costs. [Abstract Interpretation](https://cs.nyu.edu/~pcousot/COUSOTpapers/POPL77.shtml)
- **Graf and Saïdi (1997)**. Given concrete transitions and predicates, candidate abstract transitions are validated against the concrete predicate cells by theorem proving. This supplies a closely related automatic overapproximation of concrete reachability, with uncertain candidate edges handled conservatively. M01.1 fills bounded source and environment fields, verifies their compatibility globally, and certifies that target results have realizers. For example, a target computed on a rectangular approximation to two admitted typed models can be spurious. Predicate abstraction could help after picking an independently justified abstraction for the four PC-X boundaries. No proof here supplies such a general abstraction. [Construction of Abstract State Graphs with PVS](https://www.csl.sri.com/papers/grafsaidi97/)
- **Cruz-Filipe et al. (2017)**. LRAT supplies hints to validate propositional refutations with small independently verified checkers. M01.1's complete resolution checker is an elementary existence argument for the emitted CNF. A validator like this can be used for a practical prover when its claimed checked fact is tied to the same source and CNF. Neither proof logging mechanism checks the external legality or read-only character of an instrument by itself. [Efficient Certified RAT Verification](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~mheule/publications/lrat.pdf)

## Observable strategy refinement

- **Alur, Henzinger, Kupferman and Vardi (1998), Definition 1 and Theorem 3**. At a related pair of alternating states each system-side move in the source has a single receiving move chosen before the opponent outcome. For every receiving outcome an allowed source mate retains the relation. The paper starts from label-equal pairs and eliminates pairs until a greatest relation is reached. This gives polynomial-time checking on an explicit arena. The move quantifiers and elimination in M01.2 are this standard alternating simulation on the belief arena, with containment in the authorized stop, cost and touch orders. Its PC-X specialization derives the changes in whole-atom legality for every resource freeze and links belief moves to actual observable histories. This is a restriction-free comparison with the old observation-indexed injection within the finite D-35 policy language, and a cost/touch application of a known refinement method. [Alternating Refinement Relations](https://www.cs.huji.ac.il/~ornak/publications/concur98.pdf)
- **Chatterjee, Doyen, Henzinger and Raskin (2007)**. Finite imperfect-information games with an observation-based controller admit a knowledge-state construction for sure winning. One move must work at compatible states, and the adversary chooses the next observed information set. M01.2 uses that knowledge-state structure before checking support refinement. The feasibility contraction and support circuit specialize it to proper finite resource-freeze authorization and prices. The cited machinery supplies no conjunctive license to invoke separate hidden-state command realizations behind one projected label. [Algorithms for Omega-Regular Games with Imperfect Information](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2619)
- **Reissig, Weber and Rungger (2017), Definition V.2 and Theorem V.4**. Their feedback refinement relates set-valued control systems under a static quantizer. Every enabled abstract input must be admissible at each related concrete state. The abstract successor set contains the related image of the concrete successors under that same input. This is a close source-action implementability control. Compared with the common input alphabet in that particular condition, M01.2 picks an actual receiving command using the pair of public belief nodes and an observed reply map. Its scope is finite proper robust reachability, with the resource-mask order carrying the comparison across all freezes. The more general synthesis goals in the source paper remain outside this result. [Feedback Refinement Relations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.03715)

## Disposition

M01.1 combines finite semantics, bounded reachability, knowledge-state calculation and propositional certificates for the typed D-35 evidence interface. M01.2 gives a strict theorem-level generalization of accepted T03 under a known alternating simulation mechanism. M01.3's missing record is an exact source-fragment instantiation of the accepted two-bin T16 witness. The method classifications are adaptations. General extraction and stronger originality remain unresolved. The search has not ruled out more general weighted refinement treatments with monitor and intervention annotations.
