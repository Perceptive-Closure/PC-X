## Teach-back

The engineering team wants to entrust actual work to an agent while retaining a production authorization guarantee. The question is which facts justify that particular decision on the real installation. A million successful rehearsals can leave the relevant distinction untouched if they never use the production finalizer.

1. **Identification.** Keep every system consistent with the warranted evidence and compute the property in each. A nonempty set of identical answers identifies the property. Two explanations with different answers and identical accessible reports demonstrate an obstruction. An empty set indicates a broken premise.
2. **Acquisition.** A fixed collection of exact, jointly available reads suffices when it distinguishes every pair with different target answers. Actual acquisition rights and costs determine what can be bought. An adaptive policy can use an early receipt to choose its next read. A particular cheap certificate need not give a cheap procedure in the worst case.
3. **Transport.** State which records, lawful commands and physical events correspond in evaluation and deployment, and place conservative bounds on differences. An untested production route or an independently varying row silently substituted for a fixed installed parameter can invalidate the calculation.
4. **Strategic resistance.** A responder may remember the audit and react to its verdict. The auditor gets the stopped records actually exposed by the permitted experiment. If opposing mixtures of worlds induce the same record probabilities for every plan, a guaranteed binary decision can do no better than chance in the worst case.
5. **Verification.** Verify the relation between the monitor and the actual violation, the effect of installing the instrument, and the event relation at component handoffs. Identical alarm frequencies can hide different actual risks. An instrument can even change the outcome being measured.
6. **Governed realization.** One implementable policy must be capable of taking every action it promises and delivering the required work on every applicable branch. Separate successful policies chosen with hidden knowledge do not give the team a common policy. Refusal may meet a safety requirement while failing the work requirement.

The central synthesis puts decisions, purchases, actual work, strategic response, and physical scoring on the *same installed run*. The one-time guard price, per-attempt expected price, maximum possible charge and real work measure different things. A different information menu or deployment mechanism changes the conclusion.
