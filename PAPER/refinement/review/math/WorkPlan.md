# Mathematical review work plan

The reviewer writes only the new review records. The author owns the manuscript and its corrections. Historical review files remain evidence of the earlier campaign.

1. Compare the source intake with tracked statement seals, proofs and corrections. Independently derive the proof dependencies for each promoted theorem and check the closest prior results.
2. Upon receipt of the revised draft, audit each changed assertion in context, review all displayed theorem boundaries and verify corrections against exact manuscript locations. Record objections and dispositions.
3. After fidelity and reader corrections, perform a separate adversarial scientific review. Identify surviving objections, compulsory corrections and the scope of acceptance. Record which evidence was directly inspected.
