## Prior work directly compared

Approximate probabilistic bisimulation already gives a tight finite-horizon trace bound. T18 specifies how independently validated conditional components, callable actions and an event decoder supply an applicable common sublaw. [Bian and Abate](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.04547)

Robust common policies over finitely many hidden environments already have exact and approximate algorithms. The PC-X claim locates the simultaneous metric and resource guarantees in an independently represented authority and event interface. [Bovy et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23744)

Deployment protocols with strategic opponents and multiple safety/usefulness objectives already admit game-based formalization and synthesis. Evidence of usefulness in actual LLM tasks exists in the AI-control experiments. A controlled PC-X example adds a priced identification requirement and a source-linked deployment envelope under specified assumptions. [Griffin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.07985) [Greenblatt et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/greenblatt24a.html)
