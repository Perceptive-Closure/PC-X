# Hostile scientific review of the corrected manuscript

This pass was conducted after the mathematical corrections and first reader revision. It tests the scientific interpretation of the corrected main paper separately from proof fidelity. The evidence base includes the tracked reconstruction and generalization proofs, the X16/X17 reports, the manuscript's comparison sections and the primary sources below.

## 1. Can a reader find a central contribution?

The release example in the abstract and opening distinguishes an answered question from a runnable common policy. The three-installation action table in the strategic section makes the distinction observable even with an identified target. This survives removing the theorem identifiers. The extensive appendix still imposes a high cost on someone seeking to verify the complete package. The appropriate reading claim is that the scientific argument is accessible through the main text.

## 2. Do established methods explain the results?

Most numerical and optimization techniques have direct predecessors. The point-query identity lower bound follows from unstructured search. Perfect-recall sequence form explains the affine policy representation. Robust common policies over a fixed hidden environment and game-based safety/usefulness synthesis are established. The finite-sample empirical checks do not establish priority. What survives is a set of conditional mathematical results linking a fixed decision, observable and priced actions, complete scoped worlds, a genuine physical event and useful continuation. For example, changing a single link from a real receipt to a hidden one can destroy an otherwise individually serviceable problem. The evidence supports a contribution in formulation, the specified constructions and certified common realization. It provides no general improvement over robust control or active testing. [Bovy et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23744) [Griffin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.07985)

## 3. Is the general machinery stronger than the examples?

P.G01.1 decides feasibility for linked semialgebraic families on finite explicit trees. U.G1 represents simultaneous performance across a continuum for finite polynomial factor inventories and deterministic updates. U.G2 certifies physical lower and upper values under verified uniform discrepancies. These claims are logically stronger than solving the two-probe fixture. The U.G3 optimum itself is explained by two matched endpoints and a static lottery over probe qualities. The optimal verdict uses the purchased bit. The route receipt remains essential to invoking the correct literal action, and the responder's memory changes physical event probabilities. I asked for this decomposition to be explicit so that a joint realization cannot be read as evidence for a strict adaptive probe-selection advantage. U.G1's counts prove a representation separation in the stated family. They do not prove an implementation-independent running-time lower bound.

## 4. What would fail under a plausible change of assumptions?

Replacing one fixed bias by independent adversarial row choices changes the two-draw disagreement maximum from one half to one. Removing the outside-cell guard can turn an uncallable command into a spurious policy. A zero-probability issuance event can make a signed selective inequality vacuous. Selecting a model class after seeing calibration data requires separate membership and conditional deployment hypotheses. The corrected transport, strategy, joint-synthesis and limitations sections retain these distinctions. Another frequently hidden assumption is the charge for useful intake itself. I asked to replace the abstract's “for free” wording with the narrower claim of zero additional paid audit charge. X16 still requires resources and a legal work move.

## 5. Could sampling or comparator choice explain the empirical claims?

Yes, for claims broader than those reported. X17 has eight held-out settings on one authored service with two role implementations on a shared host. The 327,680 trials report repeated-run behavior, not an effective sample of that many installations. Sixteen issued-case intervals support 3/8, whereas only four of eight settings per role support the uniform label-error threshold. Exact algebra establishes the worst-world menu optimum independently of these frequencies. A stronger direct observation costs 1/6. In X16 the known-installation baseline can avoid paid acquisition altogether. On matching unknown-path information, an explicit solver has better median runtime on six of eight profiles. These are concrete boundaries to the claims and appear in the body. The consistent signed score and conditional ratio have different sampling intervals, and the paper reports both. A field claim would require externally operated sources and genuine independent installation sampling.

## 6. Do the comparisons handicap stronger approaches?

The paper distinguishes Bayesian expected decision-region acquisition from its finite worst-case target prices, robust fixed-environment POMDPs from the source-specific deployment constraints, and game-based control from the priced complete audit law. Approximate trace bounds, causal transport, safeguarding and parametric verification receive explicit credit. Forcing the PC-X group to use only its base interface while allowing an authenticated direct read immediately reverses the apparent advantage. The paper reports that control separately. It offers no matched empirical leaderboard against commercial assurance systems, no exhaustive priority search, and no comparative performance guarantee for arbitrary environments. [Javdani et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v33/javdani14.html) [Bian and Abate](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.04547) [Greenblatt et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/greenblatt24a.html)

## 7. Does the frontier motivation generate a mathematical question?

The model can express a verdict-aware responder by fixing its whole program and letting it receive the actual issued verdict before a physical action. Sharing public transcripts across target-opposed worlds then has a precise minimax meaning. A receipt that changes which literal command is callable gives a precise deployment obligation. Neither correspondence licenses a claim about the coverage of a real frontier system's responder class or a real monitor's false-negative rate. The paper identifies both as independent source obligations. It supplies no measured cost for discovering all such routes in an externally operated frontier agent.

## 8. Is the scientific question interesting without counting theorems?

For the running release service, ask which observation changes the deployment answer, whether the team can legally acquire it, and whether a common policy can then deliver the job. A check of the real guard can identify the relevant property while leaving compiler identity unresolved. A single suppressed receipt can remove the ability to deliver the job. Those statements have distinct operational consequences and are falsifiable on a specified executable interface. Counting proofs would establish neither of them.

## Review disposition

The framework's strongest defensible claim is a source-conditional foundation and a set of constructive examples with explicit boundaries. Physical completeness, strict adaptivity gains on U.G3, universal speedups and field effectiveness remain outside the demonstrated evidence. Two scientific wording corrections were sent to the author. The mathematical pass separately records the earlier original-guard and evidence-threshold corrections.

The author incorporated both corrections. I verified the abstract at main.tex line 40 now states “without a separate paid audit” and unified.tex line 60 explicitly states when the quality lottery may be drawn and that the prelude confers no additional price gain for this choice. The same passage retains the route dependence and memory-dependent finalizer. No material objection remains to the revised claims within their stated scope.
