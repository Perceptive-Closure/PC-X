\section{Experiments, comparators and reproducibility}\label{app:experiments}
\begingroup\setlength{\emergencystretch}{2em}

The experiments answer different questions. A computed optimum tests a finite model, a held-out execution tests a forecast against a particular service, and an independently scored physical record tests whether a reported verdict corresponds to work performed. We retain the unit and comparator for each result.

\paragraph{X01. Finite recovery.} Two independent evaluators computed eight intervention costs on 243 finite contracts. All 1,944 coordinates agreed. Giving the controller hidden state understated cost in six of 18 designed cases. In three it even made an impossible task appear feasible. The workload was generated, with no population sampling. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/a52d45f1b8c0036b1a69b1fc024f95e48d4ce012/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X01/results/summary.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/a52d45f1b8c0036b1a69b1fc024f95e48d4ce012/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X01/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X02. Correlated completions.} An exact joint enumeration retained 249 compatible completions across 94 cases. Replacing joint constraints by separate coordinate restrictions enlarged the joint target in 29 of 83 nonempty cases. Nineteen identified coordinates appeared ambiguous, and 36 finite upper bounds became infinite. The transition kernel was fixed. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/18bc904d7073df3536d9794f18b868b9a3de53bc/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X02/results/summary.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/18bc904d7073df3536d9794f18b868b9a3de53bc/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X02/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X03. Minimum certificates.} Independent subset enumeration checked 59,971 subsets over 865 domains and 3,828 global or local scopes. There were 3,102 certificates, 725 indistinguishable target-conflicting pairs recorded as impossibility witnesses across scopes, and one inconsistent scope. Mean optimum on finite uniform scopes was 2.132 units versus 2.208 for greedy selection. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/d85fe1cdb76cbc6d03e58e34f320c4c58a3f243c/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X03/results/scopes.csv}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/d85fe1cdb76cbc6d03e58e34f320c4c58a3f243c/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X03/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X04. Adaptive acquisition.} Exact minimax search and independent policy replay covered 88 cases and 21,062 paths. In 47 feasible cases the mean worst cost was 218/47 for adaptive minimax, 253/47 for optimal static acquisition and 586/47 for buying every query. Greedy partitioning attained the optimum on 25 cases. The five-site routed example needed two adaptive units or six static units. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/b6ce562aa91323f2913615b92e91ffb2b9cc4731/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X04/results/summary.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/b6ce562aa91323f2913615b92e91ffb2b9cc4731/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X04/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X05. Symbolic computation.} A serial model with 64 stages and three local bits represented 2 to the power 192 compatible contracts. Computing without listing them was possible, but both target and full identity cost 677 on that matched case. On jointly solved cases lazy search took longer than direct partitioning on 20/28 and eager search on 22/28. These are host-specific timing comparisons. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/e288a6b5b8ddac24af349913bc68b640a691b2b2/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X05/results/measurements.csv}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/e288a6b5b8ddac24af349913bc68b640a691b2b2/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X05/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X06. Authorization rules.} A bounded adapter evaluated a frozen upstream policy using Open Policy Agent. The opening contained 112 held-out native decisions, split equally between allow and deny, and five development controls. All eight forecast verdicts and 64 intervention coordinates agreed. The terminal-only baseline made four false positive judgments. Admission semantics came from the adapter. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/d3d8f0d6f14d548ce4b95dfa4341d8db54ad7876/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X06/results/native.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/d3d8f0d6f14d548ce4b95dfa4341d8db54ad7876/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X06/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X07. Certificate diagnosis.} A finite certificate-chain fixture compared signed certificate fields, native validation, and a coarse diagnostic. The audit reversed two false failures among eight verdicts and retained six failures. Reading native fields also obtained eight correct verdicts. Fixture prices were assigned units. The measured suite provides no field prevalence. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/06242b250a7041d40f2f6af32bd8a11742813891/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X07/evidence/post-outcome/results.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/06242b250a7041d40f2f6af32bd8a11742813891/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X07/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X08. Equivalent benchmark observations.} Two parcel-routing agents shared the same benchmark law. Across 8,192 episodes in eight cohorts, the worse of two deployment conditions produced 651/1,024 adverse outcomes for A and 128/1,024 for B, on opposite sides of the 40 percent threshold. Benchmark extrapolation falsely passed A. The positive forecast relied on route calibration and enrollment balance. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/a8c6650a7ff9901ca9ac5ed4036a37f6cb7c9200/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X08/results/opening.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/a8c6650a7ff9901ca9ac5ed4036a37f6cb7c9200/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X08/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X09. Evaluation-aware behavior.} A crossed local simulation varied cues and history. Across 131,072 native episodes and 65,536 factorial draws all four admitted judgments agreed. Both midpoint controls triggered abstention and both unanchored scoring shifts yielded false favorable certifications. With exact laws adaptive and static costs were five and seven. Under sampled batches greedy tied the primary method. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/68624c9a0eea93ec304be8e4349727eda3381c76/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X09/results/summary.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/68624c9a0eea93ec304be8e4349727eda3381c76/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X09/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X10. Monitor blind spots.} Independent route-card scoring reconstructed 16,384 labels and alarms. In the peak mixture two intakes had miss frequencies 48.24 and 8.58 percent despite alarm frequencies 17.14 and 16.64 percent. The alarm-only rule falsely passed one intake. Four anchored threshold verdicts matched. Removing coverage or enrollment balance left a verdict unresolved. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/4a16ef37d0e90e856560ce153c0fd690c28016b6/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X10/results/opening.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/4a16ef37d0e90e856560ce153c0fd690c28016b6/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X10/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X11. Disturbing the measured system.} A paired design scored 8,192 blocks, each with four arm episodes. Passive and placebo labels matched the original. The disturbed probe preserved public transcripts but increased actual risk by 69.56 and 66.63 percentage points in the two contexts. Separate scoring used the local service counters and a balanced enrollment assumption. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/01f174c14669c92cd1c06dbf5158db29c4720b6d/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X11/results/opening.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/01f174c14669c92cd1c06dbf5158db29c4720b6d/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X11/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X12. Transport to deployment.} Two finite service families generated 98,304 outcomes. Six anchored decisions agreed and six abstained. The evaluation-only comparator falsely passed seven units. Full acquisition resolved ten of twelve for 786,444 configured operations versus six for 1,048,804 with the anchored policy. Fidelity, coverage and the operational cost model each affected the result. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/486671cfcb52405ad226de553ce1df992f66b4fc/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X12/results/summary.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/486671cfcb52405ad226de553ce1df992f66b4fc/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X12/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X13. Composition.} Four ports and two contexts yielded 512 joint and 1,024 isolated executions. Naively adding isolated bounds falsely certified three ports. At the faithful port the worst joint risk was 7/64, against a certified bound of 1/4. On the concentrated tickets both local violations coincided on 7/64, far above the product 49/4,096. Greedy matched the minimum static audit cost of 15. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/1b9e599a7333d9b9f85669298791557282a75c1b/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X13/results/summary.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/1b9e599a7333d9b9f85669298791557282a75c1b/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X13/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X14. Governed work.} A finite budget game classified 92 scenarios as 22 feasible, 69 infeasible and one inconsistent. External adaptive minimax cost was 11, versus 20 for static target identification and 25 for static identity. Realized external acquisition cost nine. Over ten tickets the governed arm was safe on ten, completed eight, and qualified 16/20 jobs. Refusal was safe on ten and completed zero. The fixed-left and single-contract arms were safe on four and six respectively. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/4ed49718f364afe9ba0723e96f4111c370a12101/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X14/results/summary.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/4ed49718f364afe9ba0723e96f4111c370a12101/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X14/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X15. Hostile reproduction.} An independent implementation and replay revisited the fourteen packages and tested six groups of deliberate perturbations. It recovered the reported finite results including the X05 acquisition tie, the X07 native-field tie and the adverse X12 comparison. X08 recorded verification and completion within the same whole second. X11 relied on the parent ledger for the opening time. Shared generators, upstream models and evidence prevent treating the fourteen packages as independent draws. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/9c3884846c895ead014ba06895cf74da2d15e726/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X15/results/independent.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/9c3884846c895ead014ba06895cf74da2d15e726/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X15/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X16. Useful work and audit costs.} Eight signed graph profiles tested a common controller against complete supported paths. Forecasts matched 178 path targets and 70 comparator values. Twenty-two installations were held out for primary tests and ten for controls. The native service persisted stage records and a second implementation replayed the experiment. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/a6bf9cefb59f63b8c9dea59daf7e588a82ba07eb/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X16/results/forecast-agreement.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/a6bf9cefb59f63b8c9dea59daf7e588a82ba07eb/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X16/README.md}{replay instructions}.

\paragraph{X17. Selective decisions with a guarded source.} Eight held-out rational parameter settings crossed with five policies gave forty cells. Each role ran 4,096 attempts per cell, yielding 163,840 valid attempts each. The two roles separately authenticated setup and checked resulting durable records. Statistical observations concern these eight settings. Source-level optimization used the specified law. \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/899f29dbdcc6e9a81a32bbaf387c3bf270501b10/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X17/results/statistics/summary.json}{Recorded observations} and \href{https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/899f29dbdcc6e9a81a32bbaf387c3bf270501b10/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X17/REPRODUCTION.md}{replay instructions}.

\subsection{The price and limits of a selective decision (X17)}
The local service uses an installed parameter throughout a run. A policy can skip or buy an observation, issue a binary decision, and perform work that leaves a stored record. A separate source check authenticates the guard for each installation. The trial-level probe charge excludes this one-time setup. On the base menu, the worst expected charge achieving the required uniform error bound of $1/3$ is $7/12$, with a maximum charge of one on any path. Restricting the menu to the high-price observation and skipping gives $2/3$. With only the low-price observation the endpoint pair forces error at least $3/8$. Guard setup costs $1/4$. A richer interface with an authenticated direct read achieves the same error goal for $1/6$.

The preregistered family comprises 640 intervals across eighty world, policy and role combinations. It allocates total error probability $1/20$ under independent repeated trials within each cell. Seven bounded scores use radius $1/25$ at 4,096 trials. For the eighth score, risk conditional on issuance, let $m$ be the number of issued records. When $m>0$ its radius before clipping the interval to $[0,1]$ is $\sqrt{\log(2\cdot640\cdot20)/(2m)}$. The signed alternative averages the issuance indicator times the difference between the violation indicator and $3/8$. Across the eight settings the measured mean charges were 0.583797 and 0.582767 for the primary and independent roles. Among issued records, the simultaneous conditional intervals supported the physical violation cap $3/8$ in all eight settings for both roles. For $5/16$ the corresponding counts were six and six. The alternative signed-statistic test supported $3/8$ in six and five. World s04 illustrates the distinction: 1,017 primary issues gave observed conditional risk 26.45 percent and interval [19.39,33.51] percent. This supports the first cap and leaves the tighter decision unresolved. Each role supported the registered positive-world issuance threshold in two of four settings and the positive-world utility threshold in two of four. Mistaken positive decisions in negative worlds are included in selective risk.

\paragraph{Reproducing the archived evidence.}
Each link above opens the study at its governing Git revision. Obtain that revision in a separate checkout and follow its linked replay commands with the recorded dependency versions. X15 documents the predecessor-specific setup and the difference between deterministic content checks and fresh timing measurements. For X16 use Python 3.13 and Node.js 22, install the locked custody dependencies in a separate directory, verify the signed opening, regenerate the forecast, then run the independent reference and compare its agreement file. For X17 the linked reproduction guide gives commands from the repository root for frozen-input verification, forecast checks, the evidence verifier and separate primary and independent replay directories. Replaying a revealed draw checks the archived execution. Only the original time-stamped freeze and opening can establish its prospective order.
