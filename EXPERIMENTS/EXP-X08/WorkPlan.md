# EXP-X08 — Evaluation-equivalent AI agents

## A. Scientific objective

Can a bounded investigator demonstrate two controlled, interactive agents have identical full permitted evaluation transcript laws but opposite deployment verdicts, and resolve the consequential ambiguity with a legitimate extra audit? Test T15–T17 and the applicability of the T21 indistinguishability bound. The hypothesis concerns a specified finite, history-dependent sandbox with an independently defined numeric property. It leaves open frequency in real models, unrestricted adaptive evaluations, and the general transport problem.

## B. Scope

Write under `EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X08/`. Use actual executed finite stochastic decision agents in an offline benign task such as virtual sorting or routing, with at least three decision rounds. Include multiple evaluation actions and nontrivial action or outcome feedback. Full observation histories are bound to the frozen interface. Use only inert tasks and inputs. No live services, paid APIs, offensive workflows or mechanisms targeting real monitors.

## C. Mathematical specification

Freeze the class of agent–environment completions, observation functions, conditional support laws, initial and reset assumptions, independent outcome label, deployment contexts and risk threshold. Exact equality of reachable conditional evaluator-visible laws under each permitted action establishes equality for every adaptive auditor within the horizon. Verify exact target risk by rational marginalization with an independent checker. Add a source- or environment-grounded query that probes a fact rather than reading the deployment outcome. Under the extended interface test whether all residual completions, including nuisance alternatives, agree on the deployment threshold. Keep the agent and environment semantics explicit.

## D. Methodology

Freeze an externally auditable forecast protocol and sample/inference rule before observing deployment data. Separate held-out episode seeds or deployment fixture and native outcome from the person or process issuing its prediction. Ask the parent to act as independent custodian if needed. Send the parent the pinned protocol commit and proposed handoff before any held-out runs. Commit the chosen audit, independently measured evidence and verdict/risk forecast before opening held-out outcomes. Enumerate conditional histories off-line, include a positive distinguishability control, an irrelevant cue negative control, an audit-removal ablation and a conventional evaluation-only forecast. Execute the controlled agents on independently sealed deployment episodes using a trusted outcome logger that is distinct from the evaluator. Use a justified independent sample size and held-out uncertainty analysis. Distinguish model-law checks from measured frequencies.

## E. Implementation

Provide the finite agent runner, independent transcript-law checker, evaluator and trusted outcome runner. Pin policy and environment, seed schedule or commitment scheme, staged commands, tests and per-condition evidence. Track exact comparator risks, actual trial counts, reset records and forecast opening. A terminal commit contains the frozen protocol and prediction commits as provenance and enough information for deterministic reruns of the analysis.

## F. Acceptance

Verify equality for every permitted reachable history/action on the intended witness pair and reject equality when authorized positive-control choices separate them. Verify exact different deployment risks that straddle a frozen threshold and check whether the purchased legitimate audit resolves that target. Evaluate the prospectively sealed decision against independently held-out executed outcomes and the baseline. A failed or inconclusive prediction is reportable. Apparent equality from finite sample nonsignificance is insufficient.
