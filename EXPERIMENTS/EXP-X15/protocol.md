Append each remaining tip and the timestamp of independent confirmation in `inputs.late.jsonl` before retrieving or opening that package. Preserve both freezes in Git history. 3. Run all published suites and advertised complete replay commands. Compare deterministic artifacts to their published digests when available. Git chronology, hash commitments and reproduced outputs establish specific evidence relations. A shared-filesystem custody design cannot alone prove pre-outcome isolation. 5. Preserve each test/replay outcome, discrepancy, comparison, failure and relevant null result. Assess the supported fragment, admissible contract and audit interfaces, sample unit, denominator, target and comparator. Report every theorem entry T01–T30 with corresponding scope and remaining obligation. Theorem proof status requires an independently audited proof.

## Discrepancy rubric

| Code | Rule | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Integrity | Wrong exact revision, unauthorized or incomplete archive, changed frozen protocol/runner, inconsistent commitment or broken custody ordering | Stop affected replay or suspend the affected prospective claim. Preserve source and evidence. Escalate to parent. |
| Contradiction | Independent admissible witness, raw reconstruction or faithful replay disagrees with an exact published conclusion, primary verdict, gate, admissibility rule or claimed scope | Isolate a minimal case, quantify consequence, preserve the disagreement and escalate. |
| Material qualification | Result reproduces under a narrower assumption, inference depends on an unverified premise, or a consequential comparator, negative or denominator changes interpretation | Keep supported outcomes and give the applicable qualification. Escalate. |
| Reproducibility defect | An instruction, runtime input, evidence format or platform dependency requires a work-around without changing the target results | Record actual attempt and recovery; distinguish from scientific contradiction as evidence allows. |
| Expected variability | Wall-clock/allocator measurements or documented equivalent encodings vary, with deterministic invariant evidence unchanged | Record observed variation and compare conclusions conditionally on hardware, toolchain and sampling. |
| Agreement | Exact or permitted approximate checks meet the rules below, which include an independently checked negative where available | Report scope and coverage. |

Exact comparisons cover revision, semantic decisions, cardinalities, identities, inclusion/exclusion, positive-support branches, integer costs and episode counts. Deterministically replayed uncompressed data are byte exact where a digest is published. Re-encoding is tested against canonical contents and separately against any published compressed digest. For independently recalculated floating-point statistics use absolute tolerance `1e-12` in fractional units, or half of the last explicitly reported decimal unit in a rounded display. Recompute interval width from the frozen `n`, family cardinality and alpha rather than inferring them from matches. Repeated observations within a shared block are paired for contrasts. Hash mismatches and stochastic discrepancies are never waived as generic machine variability. A fresh-seed statistical control, if used, requires the original declared unit and method or a separate clearly declared simultaneous bound. X05 timestamps and allocator observations from a fresh run are recorded independently of the semantic comparison. No numerical hardware equivalence or performance trend is presumed from the committed timing replay.

## Independent falsification controls

Before assessing predecessor evidence, construct small counterexamples independent of the published fixture generators. Use an extensional all-support closure control and a controller-observation/action-legitimacy control. Check a correlated condition where coordinate envelopes lose the attainable vector and a weighted certificate whose global and local choices differ. Test a denominator and family-size perturbation, block pairing, and outcome-dependent threshold reuse. Construct distinct systems with the same observed evaluation and unmeasured deployment divergence. Check separately the hypotheses required for monitoring, fidelity and event containment. Construct an observation-only two-completion utility game where each completion permits a policy but no common robust useful policy exists. Keep each control's concrete inputs and expected outcome. Additional independently specified controls are exploratory and dated.

Initial results and counterexamples will be tagged agreed, qualified, contradicted, or blocked with a specific reason per package. The final report requires all fourteen terminal revisions, published replays, source verification, independent calculations and material discrepancies. New observations or documented platform adjustments do not modify this freeze.
