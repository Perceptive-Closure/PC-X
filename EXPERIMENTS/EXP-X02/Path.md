## Phase 1 · Frozen design

The master checkout is pinned at `db692b0723c085ddc89e8a20670a8ecebe81f748`. The predecessor branch was fetched from [Perceptive-Closure/PC-X](https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X). Its fetched tip and imported API are pinned at [X01 a52d45f](https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/tree/a52d45f1b8c0036b1a69b1fc024f95e48d4ce012/EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X01). The source PDFs matched the hashes in the master work plan. The scientific reference is charter pp. 2–9 and 13–17, plus original Perceptive Closure pp. 3–6 and the claim boundary on p. 17.

The preregistered fragment uses acyclic source graphs with observable checkpoint distinctions, fixed atomic candidate locators and positive-support outcome records, a fixed sealed world-to-class map, and a common terminal/open pattern and ordinary availability after fixing a case. Semantic state coordinates are admitted history, an equivalence partition, and the extensionally admitted interface set. Each may depend on Boolean evidence features. Construct every touch from equality of the state coordinates across all supported successors. Availability changes, support changes, and changes to target classes or observable boundaries across members of one purported common kernel must be refused for the comparison. Planned one-successor and authority mutations are negative checks.

A completion is a source-compatible assignment satisfying the case's finite Boolean CNF. Compare prefix-pruned enumeration against a Cartesian Boolean truth table evaluated from the clauses. Compute all eight freeze values using minimax dynamic programming, the frozen X01 engine, and unrestricted proper policy trees filtered by their action support sets. Independently round-trip attainment witnesses for each joint signature and each lower and upper bound. A witness gives its assignment and one attainable signature. Empty Boolean evidence is inconsistent, even if an evaluator could return infinity for every freeze on some nonempty case.

For each source action locator, the rectangular comparator gives independent choices among its observed extensional touch sets. For a freeze, its lower endpoint removes locators with intersection in every such option. Its upper endpoint removes locators with intersection in at least one option. The comparator will both enumerate the joint product and independently check these monotone endpoints. This generalizes the removal endpoints of original Proposition 3 within a fixed kernel. The coordinate-by-coordinate product of the exact marginals is a distinct relaxation, which may introduce false joint signatures even when the touch comparator has no error. Differences mean loss from relaxation, measured by attainable value overcoverage, unattainable marginal combinations, endpoint distance including infinity, and false coordinate ambiguity. A distinct corpus flag records equality, so null cases remain.

Controls freeze the charter XOR pair, its unconstrained counterpart, co-occurring R effects with a missing intermediate attained cost, asymmetric mutually exclusive routes for a joint trade-off, a fixed E-only fallback with a strictly finite endpoint gap, implication constraints, a genuine side effect on the second positive-support branch and its first-only ablation, fully observable contingent branches with unequal downstream costs, and a contradictory CNF. The root history admits a permit-compatible fact under a permit/deny alias. A closing node either admits a different permit-only history, refines the alias, or does both. The authority feature changes admission of a declared reserve interface that the fragment never offers at an open checkpoint. Its authority change therefore leaves ordinary action availability anchored. The mixed-support case's E and R effects are computed on the whole source action.

The corpus is ten hand controls, the 16 arbitrary truth masks of the two Boolean features with each of three action cost pairs, and 36 seeded cases. Each seed uses Python's `random.Random(seed)` for 12 instances with 3 or 4 features alternating by instance parity, topologies cycling by parity in pairs, and the cost and CNF ranges in [protocol.json](protocol.json). Seeded direct cases offer one route per feature with a constant E-only fallback. Seeded forks inspect two distinct open checkpoints whose downstream action sets offer a feature-conditioned route and a fallback. Each case tests both polarities of its first feature and then both polarities of its second under each first-feature refinement. Every case is exact over its declared finite support. The seeded workload is a deterministic coverage panel with no population inference. No production systems or paid dependencies are in scope.

The design and generator parameters were frozen before generating results. Independent verification failures, unsupported cases and any deviations will be entered here before acceptance.

## Phase 3 · Results

All three freeze evaluators agreed on 1,992 cells from 249 compatible completions and eight freezes. Both evidence enumerators agreed on every case and refinement. Independent round-trip checks covered 1,328 endpoint witnesses, 191 joint-signature witnesses, all target-disagreement pairs, and all definite/possible removal extrema. All 564 nested refinements obeyed identified-set inclusion, with 304 strict reductions. The suite returned 22 point-identified, 61 partially identified, and 11 inconsistent cases. Contradictory evidence and an all-infinite signature were distinguished by a dedicated test. Source support, ordinary action availability, action location, and checkpoint-closure mutations were all refused.

The rectangular relaxation contained every exact signature and agreed with the exact joint set in 54 of 83 nonempty cases. It strictly enlarged the set in the remaining 29 cases, adding 43 joint signatures across the corpus. It added attainable values in 76 of 664 defined freeze coordinates and widened bounds in 51. Nineteen point-identified coordinates appeared ambiguous after relaxation, and 36 finite upper bounds relaxed to infinity. The joint product of exact coordinate marginals omitted cross-freeze dependence in 58 cases, introducing 1,582 unattainable combinations across them. These are counts on the frozen designed panel rather than prevalence estimates.

For the observable fork, a proper policy must close both branches, so its value uses the more costly branch.
