## Contracts, observations and the task

A contract fixes the allowed entry lane, the panel codec, membership in the relief program, and a signature bit. The bit authorizes the optional plate read. It has no physical effect. Source admission begins with two job slots. A claim of a third slot is incompatible. Every support row starts empty with relief unlicensed and the default codec. The first physical job on a regular or relief bench has two supported routing slips, indexed 0 and 1. Both are possible in every admitted contract. The second regular dispatch uses the entry lane for slip 0 and its opposite for slip 1. A both-lanes workshop accepts either regular lane under either slip. A closed entry lane accepts neither. A granted relief bench accepts both jobs. A valid dispatch records exactly one new job. The buffer accepts the first job safely but occupies the feeder and blocks all further work. Refusal ends work safely. An authority rejection precedes a bench safety check.

An indexed panel shows `offer-0` or `offer-1` after the first regular or relief dispatch. A masked panel shows `queued` for both. Finishing shows `complete`, buffering shows `buffered`, and refusal shows `stopped`. A lawful layout read returns the installed entry marker and admits that evidence. A plate read returns the constant two-slot record and admits it when authorized. A lawful codec refinement interprets the same already admitted pair of slip types with separate indices, returning the current index when a slip is pending and `reindexed` otherwise. A lawful grant is available only in the relief program and adds the relief action to the licensed operations, acknowledging `granted`. Repeat audits are allowed. Extensional E, R and A compare the admitted facts, codec partition and licensed operations at each predecessor and all supported successors of an audit. Repeat actions can have empty touch. Interface removal and runtime authority are separate constraints. Operational observations are never supplied from the physical scoring ledger.

Immediate safety requires a licensed action and compatible physical bench on every supported successor. Use every supported successor without weighting. The objective is the minimum worst-case total audit cost over winning policies. Ties use configuration action order. Cost caps are additional decision problems at the same horizon. Under budget 0, no positive-cost action enters a certificate. Budget horizon times maximum audit cost covers every bounded policy.
