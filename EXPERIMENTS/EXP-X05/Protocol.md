## Algorithms and verification

The primary method evaluates no full completion catalog. It separately establishes evidence feasibility by a witness or an empty local table. Each oracle conditions the two completions on equality of the selected local query outputs. In each selected-answer cell, retain a cheapest left stage mode and a dearest right stage mode, with infinity last. Compose oriented cost pairs along the chain. Cap right cost at one above the budget, discard left costs above budget, and discard a pair when an attainable pair has no higher left cost and no lower right cost. Retain ancestry for returned witnesses. A left cost at most the budget paired with a higher right cost proves ambiguity. Swapping one compatible local mode at a time along the witness yields a first threshold-crossing pair differing at one stage. The learned edge contains its differing query answers. An empty edge gives inaccessibility. A zero-cost empty audit with a conclusive nonempty domain gives point identification.

The lazy optimizer minimizes weighted transversals of discovered edges. All learned edges are local, so separate exact small transversals add across stages. Every round retains the candidate, lower bound and observed counterexample or checked absence of disagreement. A partial round cannot turn its bound into an optimum. The absolute cost of buying every legitimate query is recorded with a sufficiency flag, since whole-interface access can fail. The no-dominance ablation uses the same local extrema, pruning of overbudget left sums, capping, cost-pair deduplication and optimizer. It disables only Pareto dominance.

A separate verifier enumerates local assignments from literal clauses and local policies from raw source. It reconstructs history classes, pairwise representation equivalence, authority differences and support on its own. It evaluates small global completions and uses exhaustive selected-answer partitions for optimum checks there. At scale it independently checks paired UNSAT and minimum audit cost by an outside-cost construction. If any pair agrees on selected answers and straddles the budget, replacing its modes one stage at a time finds two such completions that differ at a single stage. For each stage use exact attainable sums of all other stages and the terminal. Enumerate independently which local pairs can cross the budget for an identical outside context. These pairs are necessary and sufficient for global verification because local evidence and query equalities factor. Every local transversal is then checked directly against all of these pairs. Their individual minimum weights sum to the global optimum. The checker independently validates discovered-edge witnesses, all reported oracle and cost claims, the stage-extensional costs, and any nonempty-evidence assertion. Exact attainable cost sets come from finite-sum composition and an infinity flag.

The direct-partition baseline materializes all compatible completions within its cap. It tests subsets ordered by total price against attainable answer cells. The eager-disagreement baseline materializes that same catalog, builds every target-distinct pair's bit-packed disagreement, and solves the resulting hitting problem. For this fragment its eager table can be reduced to local edges after generation, because a separating path yields a local edge contained in any global edge. Retain query-all, eight seeded random orders and a greedy score on unresolved opposite-target pairs per unit cost for tractable direct-partition runs. Greedy and random orders stop as soon as their partitions certify the target, or report failure after all available questions.
