# EXP-X05 — Symbolic scalability

## A. Scientific objective

Can source-grounded structure support verified constructive identification and minimum-cost audit synthesis without full completion enumeration? Test bounded algorithmic realizations of T10–T14, and report potential relevance to T30. The hypothesis is exact small-instance agreement and useful performance on at least one nontrivial growing contract family. General tractability, all solver fragments and universal computational advantage remain open.

## B. Scope

Write below `EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X05/`. Use symbolic finite contracts with explicit versioned restrictions such as an acyclic layer/authority graph and correlated Booleans. Extract touch from extensional changes on supported atomic edges. Use setup within your workspace and pin all external solver versions and reproducibility settings, if needed. No native systems. Pin X01, X02 and X03 check inputs or their accepted methods and references.

## C. Mathematical specification

Encode evidence constraints, support/closure semantics and target evaluation exactly. Compare two compatible completions with opposite target answers under equality of candidate query outputs. First separately check existence. Return a verified witness, checked point identification, audit-inaccessibility pair, inconsistent evidence or unresolved status. Build lazy exact minimum-weight transversals with a lower bound from discovered edges and a conclusively checked no-disagreement condition. Independently check the proof obligation on supported large instances by a verifier or separate structural derivation. Treat timeout, solver unknown, overflow, or unsupported classes as unresolved.

## D. Methodology

Freeze tested widths, correlation strengths, sizes, repetition counts and timeout/resource budgets. Compare a structured symbolic method to exhaustive completions and an eager disagreement baseline on tractable sizes. Add a structural ablation such as loss of pruning or correlation handling. Include both satisfiable and inconsistent evidence, genuinely varying target families, unreachable/inaccessible target pairs, exact optima and forced unresolved runs. Record theoretical completion count where justified, actual materialized counts, oracle calls, optimizer bounds, time, peak memory and abstention. Retain unfavorable crossovers and timeout conditions.

## E. Implementation

Provide source contract generator, symbolic engine, independent small exhaustive checker, independent witness and UNSAT/certificate validation, pinned environment, seed/config, raw timings and deterministic invariant summaries. Distinguish proof or exact structural checks from unverified solver reports.

## F. Acceptance

Require zero unexplained discrepancies on supported tractable cases, check every affirmative verdict and optimality gap, verify all witnesses, and show exact identification refuses inconclusive evidence. For accepted scalability, demonstrate measured results at completion-space sizes outside the executable exhaustive range, with meaningful independent variables affecting legitimate contracts and independent validation. A slowdown or bounded failure must be reported on its own merits.
