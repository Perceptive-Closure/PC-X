# Fixed statistical analysis plan

## Registered allocation and population

The eight individually scored good worlds use five declared native policy arms. Run 4,096 attempts per world and arm in each of the two roles, giving 32,768 per arm per role and 163,840 per role. The primary and independent runs use separate 32-byte operating-system random inputs committed before opening, then mixed with the authenticated beacon and purposes. Samplers produce exact rational event probabilities under uniform bits via rejection without modulo rounding. Statistical coverage is interpreted under the declared independent repeated-attempt model for the role-separated seeded pseudorandom streams. All inputs and actual results are reproducible from the subsequently revealed synthetic seeds. This is local software-randomness validation and uses no physical randomness claim about actual deployments.

The fixed-sample family comprises 80 world/arm/role cells and eight scalars per cell, \(K=640\). The fixed registered family error is \(\eta=1/20\). Seven fixed-count width-at-most-one scores are wrong declaration, issuance \(I\), violation \(V\), joint \(VI\), useful utility \(G\), paid cost \(C\), and \(Z=1_I(1_V-3/8)\). Use unrounded sums and two-sided intervals of radius \(\rho=1/25\) clipped to each physical range. Since \(2K\exp(-2(4096)\rho^2)\le\eta\), this is simultaneous at the stated worst-case range under the registered repeated model. The signed score ranges from \(-3/8\) to \(5/8\). Charge and utility use their actual ledger-derived units. Operation counts, timing, extra paired witnesses, and controls are separately descriptive or exact.

The eighth scalar is selective risk in the actual received issuance population. Given positive issued count \(m_I\), use the interval centered on \(\widehat V_I=\#(V\cap I)/m_I\) with radius \(\sqrt{\log(2K/\eta)/(2m_I)}\) clipped to \([0,1]\). Under independent identical pairs in that cell, the conditional issued violation count is binomial given \(m_I\), so this allocates one of the same \(K\) two-sided events. At no issuance emit undefined. The separate issuance interval remains necessary for a coverage threshold. Report both the signed interval and conditional interval. Use outward rounding for any displayed or serialized limits with numerical bounds.

## Decisions before sight of data

1. **Main point forecasts.** Record whether each predeclared point lies in its simultaneous interval. For an equivalence claim the entire interval must be within forecast \(\pm2/25\). A point outside the interval is a point mismatch with a simultaneous error budget under the model. A compatible point with an interval beyond the resolution is inconclusive about equivalence. Compatibility is a finite test and equality is proved only where source/math gates justify it. Selective estimates with fewer issued paths can have wider intervals.
2. **Thresholds.** Report each endpoint separately. For upper threshold use an interval entirely at/below the threshold for support, entirely above for contradiction, and inconclusive otherwise. For lower thresholds use the reverse. \(Z\)'s upper bound at zero and positive issuance give one selected-risk criterion, and the registered conditional risk interval gives a second criterion from the same simultaneous family. State which established the threshold. The explicit \(3/8\) cap and accepted stronger \(5/16\) (\(5/32\) on positives) have distinct disposition. Apply all-world risk to mistaken positive reports in negative worlds too.
3. **Simultaneous performance.** Report per-cell and worst-cell bounds. On the primary, test wrong at most \(1/3\), all-world issuance at least \(1/4\), positive-world issuance at least \(2/3\) and positive actual utility at least \(25/32\), with the separate exact claims. On always-high test the same error and positive-coverage thresholds, clear margin outcomes separately. Always-high has exact error at most \(1/4\), positive issuance at least \(3/4\), selected risk at most \(5/16\) (positive \(5/32\)) and positive expected utility at least \(209/256\). It generally has less negative-world issuance, and the primary-specific \(1/4\) floor is not imposed on it.
4. **Independent evidence compatibility.** Compare primary and independent fixed scores via overlap of their simultaneous intervals, and compare the conditional intervals analogously. Both runs separately face the same forecasts and threshold rules. Overlap is compatible evidence, not evidence of identical sample paths or equality of distributions. Exact source, policy, certificate and decoder results must match exactly.
5. **Failures.** Record every attempt. Unexpected/invalid source, receipt, authority, material decoding or durable work issues receive their raw retained record. Do not resample to remove invalid attempts. Any affected endpoint is explicitly invalid for the registered estimator unless valid bounds with the full original denominator were preregistered. Implementation amendments after freezing are new revisions with renewed prospects if affected. No optional stop or added sample based on interim outcomes.

The setup/control observations have distinct units. Run ordinary source samples 64 times per selected world per role and paired nominal/actual PC-X samples 128 times per selected world per role for descriptive artifacts, complete record matching and per-draw correctness. The two shadows in a pair use the same physically allowed public/policy inputs and raw seed under the matched source relation. These diagnostic repeats have no added confidence claim and are excluded from the 640 main scalars. Treat signed source/optimality proofs, CI/precision, actual risk \(\varepsilon=3/8\), declared target error \(\delta=1/3\), and confidence \(\eta=1/20\) distinctly. Finite rational installations do not by themselves establish a universal continuous-domain assertion.
