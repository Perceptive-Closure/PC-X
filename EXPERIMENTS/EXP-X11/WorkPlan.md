# EXP-X11 — Intervention fidelity

## A. Scientific objective

Can independent evidence distinguish faithful measurement from disturbance in a controlled interactive system? Test T18–T19 and T26, with evaluation equivalence from T15/T17. Hypothesize a faithful probe preserves risk within a frozen tolerance while a task-relevant perturbation causes a detectable mismatch. A nonsignificant contrast alone does not establish fidelity. General mechanistic validity remains open.

## B. Scope

Use `EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X11/` and benign local policies with explicit observation, timing and resource boundaries. Use a passive logger and a controlled change of a harmless task budget or display. No operational changes, real infrastructure, offensive tooling or paid APIs. Pin X08 outcome validation approach.

## C. Mathematical specification

Define original and intervened risks over the same label and declared contexts. Define an equivalence or transport margin. Justify preservation or a bound from independent anchors such as supported-state hashes, observation deltas and matched dynamics. Preserve treatment independence or justify shared randomness. Separate intervention from independent truth measurement. Create completions compatible with initial observations but with different fidelity properties. A legitimate query checks provenance or an input/transition fact.

## D. Methodology

Pair or randomize original, passive, placebo and disturbance conditions. Freeze correspondence, order, reset, margin, trial count rationale and valid simultaneous intervals. Include an observation-held-constant/disturbed-transition case and no-anchor ablation. Compare naive direct transfer with anchored risk bounds. Parent custodian seals held-out contexts or seeds. Forecast before opening and retain policy/state traces and control results.

## E. Implementation

Provide local harness, probe variants, independent trajectory labeler and anchor verifier, matched analysis, tests and exact reproduction. Pin randomness and retain held-out observations.

## F. Acceptance

Require independently supported intervention scope and valid comparisons within declared tolerances. Check intentional perturbation and placebo outcomes and report abstention without a supported anchor. Retain unsupported or failed transfer and claim limits.
