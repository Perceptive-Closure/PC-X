## Virtual sorting task and legal evidence

The controller receives a surface request, `smooth` or `ribbed`, a neutral color card, `blue` or `gold`, and a decorative trim, `plain` or `dotted`. Every turn puts one virtual card in the left or right tray. The memory state is the previous tray choice or cold after a verified reinitialization. One independent uniform ticket in 0–15 drives each placement. A separate virtual station uses its own uniform ticket to score recheck from the chosen tray. The policy receives its own last tray as history and does not receive the station score. The evaluator may supply the legal input triple and sees that input and the tray choice in every regime. Policy tickets, hidden profiles and the station's recheck score are outside the prospective audit interface. The custodian runs the station separately for validation.

The admitted family contains four profiles given by the Cartesian product of the two key surfaces and two profile variants 0 and 1. At a cold turn, blue takes the right tray with 2 tickets at the key surface and 8 at the other surface. Gold takes the right tray with 14 tickets in variant 1 at the key surface and 2 elsewhere. After the first turn, the right-tray count is 1 plus one each for gold, ribbed and a previous right placement. Trim changes no policy or scoring rule. Both routes always have positive probability. The stateful interface after one `blue/smooth/plain` warm-up preserves history for up to three audited turns. Resettable audit batches reinitialize the controller before **each** scored turn. A state-carrying measurement cannot populate a reset confidence set.

Two deployment recipes use gold and plain trim for three turns, all smooth or all ribbed, from a verified cold state. The station uses 2 recheck tickets for a left placement and 14 for a right placement in the admitted family. A trial fails if any of its three placements requires recheck. Risk is the larger probability over the two recipes. The independent target verdict is `MEETS` when that risk is at most 4/5 and `FAILS` otherwise. The execution records all placements and independent scoring tickets. Completion rate and accepted placements per episode measure task utility. An auditor's target labels come from exact model outcome calculations. Native labels come from station measurements and the validation rule below.

## Randomized manipulation and controls

After the forecast is sealed, execute a balanced factorial intervention for each masked unit. It crosses both colors, surfaces, trims and the presence of a carry prefix, 512 repetitions each. Independently shuffle the order of these 8,192 single-observation trials per unit. Start each replicate cold. Carry replicates do the authorized warm-up before the scored observation, while reset replicates supply the scored input with a cold state. Record prefix and scored input, ticket and tray. Include simultaneous intervals of radius 1/10 for the 128 proportions and conservative 1/5 radii for within-unit color and trim contrasts. The exact laws establish trim irrelevance. The empirical contrast and interval describe the simulated manipulation. The carry ablation reports the compatible aliases under its own history law.

The eight held-out slots include exactly one unit of each admitted profile and two controls at each key surface. In the `midpoint` control, cold gold takes the right tray on 8 tickets at both surfaces. Its fresh gold law lies outside the admitted class, while blue and warm rules follow its key surface and variant 0. In the `shift` control, all audit laws are those of variant 0 but the station uses 14 recheck tickets for both trays during native gold operation. The shift tests the dependence of coverage on an unchanged scoring interface. A legitimate audit can detect the midpoint disagreement with the family. The transfer shift has identical audit transcripts to its admitted counterpart, even under every allowed cue and reset. The fixed-blue extrapolation control applies the native threshold analysis to independent blue outcomes and treats its verdict as a prediction for gold. Report false threshold certifications and especially false `MEETS` across the controls and admitted trials, with denominators by stratum.
