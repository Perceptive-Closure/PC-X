# EXP-X09 — Evaluation-aware AI systems

## A. Scientific objective

When can an evaluator identify a deployment property of a controlled system whose behavior changes with evaluation conditions and history? Test T15–T17 and T21–T24. Hypothesize that a randomized neutral cue and verified reset semantics enable identification with the declared confidence in a bounded family, while fixed cues or unverified history force unresolved cases. General strategic audits and stronger complexity guarantees remain open.

## B. Scope

Use `EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X09/` and offline benign instrumented decision agents in an inert task. Manipulate an explicit local evaluation label, task context or reset state. No assessment of real monitoring evasion, unauthorized tools, exploitable behavior or paid APIs. Pin X04 and X08 methods or their accepted exact commits.

## C. Mathematical specification

Freeze the bounded policy/environment family, legal cues, independent target label, horizon, deployment threshold, and accessibility assumptions. Distinguish independent reset episodes and stateful history. Conditional response kernels govern history-dependent regimes. Certify only nonempty target-homogeneous confidence sets with simultaneous or stopping-valid coverage. Report expected and worst-case acquisition costs separately. For resettable tractable pairs check applicability of T22. Fixed-cue equivalent laws imply the T21 minimax error obstruction.

## D. Methodology

Cross cues with contexts and a reset/history factor. Include useful and irrelevant cues, common observability, no-reset ablation, false-certification control and misspecification test. Use parent custody for held-out condition assignments or seeds. Freeze mix, thresholds, confidence, sample calculation and budget or stopping rule. Commit audit evidence and forecast before opening. Compare conventional fixed-cue evaluation, exhaustive, random and greedy acquisition, and exact bounds where available. Analyze false certification, accuracy, abstention, acquisition cost and uncertainty.

## E. Implementation

Provide executed controlled policy harness, query/audit engine, independent exact interactive law check, seeded development and held-out procedures, analysis, tests, environment and raw per-condition results. Verify chronology and distinguish exact analysis replay from statistical measurement.

## F. Acceptance

Require independently checked laws, informative and noninformative controls, justified finite-sample coverage within declared assumptions and a valid misspecification result. Compare sealed predictions with held-out measurements and preregistered criteria. Record failed and inconclusive cases as outcomes.
