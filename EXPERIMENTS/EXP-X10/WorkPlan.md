# EXP-X10 — Monitor blind spots

## A. Scientific objective

Can trusted measurements establish how often an observation-only monitor misses a declared event? Test T15–T17, T19 and T25. Hypothesize equal complete monitor-visible laws can coexist with different missed-event risks and that a legitimate observability audit yields useful upper bounds in some approved bounded settings. Real adversarial monitoring and prevalence remain open.

## B. Scope

Use `EXPERIMENTS/EXP-X10/` and executed benign local events, e.g. misrouted inert test records. A disclosed projection determines monitor data. Keep an independent truth instrument separate from monitor or model outputs. No actual evasion mechanisms, vulnerability reproduction or external targets. Pin X08 comparison approach.

## C. Mathematical specification

Freeze event V, alarm M and environment class. Measure P(V=1,M=0), P(M=1), P(V=1) and P(M=0|V=1) with denominators. Find equal complete monitor laws and differing missed-event risk. Audit independently validated label coverage or instrumentation. Check P(V) ≤ P(M) + eta under a justified missed-risk bound and P(V) ≤ min(1,P(M)/(1-beta)) for a validated conditional false-negative bound below one. Encode missing truth separately from absent alarm.

## D. Methodology

Freeze threshold, label provenance, trial/sample rationale and inference rule. Include an equal-alarm witness, distinguishable control, uninformative label ablation, sampling or skew sensitivity, and missing trusted coverage. Compare alarm-only, complete measurement, and low-cost bounds. Parent holds hidden regimes or seeds and opens independent scoring after prediction seal. Use stratified trials and simultaneous uncertainty if stochastic. Report false alarms along with misses.

## E. Implementation

Provide event harness, monitor, trusted truth instrument, checker, preregistered analysis, per-condition trusted and monitor evidence, tests and reproduction instructions. Record unsupported bounds and verified provenance.

## F. Acceptance

Require verified equal-visibility witness or documented failure, independent scoring, prospective measurement and justified bounds. Reject claims based on missing truth. Report denominators, equivalence and any vacuous or noninformative bound.
