# EXP-X10 frozen monitor observability protocol

Version 1, 2026-11-26. This protocol precedes the sealed assignment and all held-out observations. [Work plan](WorkPlan.md) · [PC-X charter crosswalk and execution record](Path.md) · [Machine-readable design](configs/protocol.json)

## Task, observation and truth

A fixed local inert routing harness places a simulated marked record in either the east or west tray. The placement reads the orientation bit of its public marker. A simulated optical reader supplies a warning for every sixth ticket. The monitor alarms exactly on the warning. No network or real operational system is involved.

An independently packaged partner route card lists the tray expected for each ticket, stratum, regime and stock revision. A separate quality instrument compares the record's actual tray to that card. It never uses the optical warning or alarm to assign the violation label. Both the actual tray and trusted expected tray are retained for measurement. Missing trusted labels are explicit nulls.

All permitted evaluations select one of two public strata, routine or oversize, and use independent reset trials. A trial draws one uniform ticket from 0 to 23, runs the fixed harness, and reports the entire monitor-visible tuple of public stratum, scanner mark, visible orientation, warning, placed tray and alarm. The hidden route card and ticket identifier remain outside this projection. The permitted transcript may include arbitrary repetition or adaptive stratum selections based on previous visible logs. It excludes looking up trusted expected trays. A reader control adds a warning to the adjacent mark and must break equivalence. Logs and all analyses are local.

## Completion space and targets

Two units are enrolled under a separately known protocol with exactly one atlas regime and one harbor regime. The order is a uniform random permutation. Each unit independently receives one of two uniformly random stock revisions. This yields eight compatible installations before audit. Both regime families have two unequal nuisance revisions. The regime and revision affect only the trusted expectation. The declared two deployment contexts differ in the routine/oversize mixture (3:1 and 1:3), with the within-stratum uniform ticket law fixed.

Let V be independently scored tray mismatch and M a monitor alarm. Report per-context risk P(V), missed-event risk P(V and not M), alarm rate P(M), false alarm probability P(not V and M), and conditional false negative P(not M given V) with violation denominators. Across the declared deployment class, each unit's targets are its maximum per-context risk and maximum per-context missed risk. Thresholds are 7/20 for risk and 1/5 for missed risk. At or below threshold counts as meeting it.

For all complete placements agreeing with the admitted evidence, exactly enumerate the achievable values and their extrema. Compute eta as the compatible missed-event upper bound and beta as the largest compatible conditional false negative. Check P(V) <= P(M) + eta and, for beta < 1, P(V) <= min(1, P(M)/(1-beta)). An upper bound alone cannot support a conclusion above threshold. Reject conditional estimates whose violation denominators are absent. Treat absent independent labels as unlabelled mass; the missing-coverage baseline deliberately withdraws transport of the lost stratum's reference contract.

## Audit and controls

The legitimate calibration query asks the upstream partner card for the expected lane of a separate sentinel that never appears in the evaluated ticket population. Its two answers identify the regime and convey no evaluated trace or deployment result. The legitimate stock query checks whether the card's stock revision is v1 or v2. Query cost is 2 for calibration and 1 for stock, per unit. These observations do not run the routing or outcome commands. Search all query subsets by positive cost for a uniform certificate of the four joint target judgments. Compare target-relative optimum with full contract optimum, cheapest-first acquisition, exact expected random-order acquisition, worst-posterior target-pair greedy acquisition, and exhaustive queries. Ties between equal-cost static sets are lexicographic in unit and query name.

Verify exact equality of the complete monitor log laws within each stratum across all eight installations and a target-distinct witness pair. Check the reader control. Remove the calibration answer, keeping only stock labels, as the uninformative audit ablation. Also remove the balanced-enrollment fact and test one unit's calibration without transport to the other unit. Finally compare against alarm-only extrapolation, a routine-only composition estimate, complete trusted scoring, and missing oversize truth. Report false alarms. Missing truth is neither a non-event nor a negative case.

## Prospective custody and acceptance

The independent parent custodian obtains the pinned pre-audit code from GitHub, selects the prefrozen assignments and a trial seed, and commits to the assignment/seed with a SHA-256 digest and independently generated nonce. The public receipt releases only that commitment and the minimum-cost permitted sentinel read. The independent signer in the parent role verifies that the forecast and its public receipt are present in the GitHub branch at the supplied exact forecast commit, that it descends from the pre-audit commit, and that executable study code and configuration have not changed. After that check the pinned runner opens the assignment and executes all 16,384 held-out trials. Raw outcomes and UTC verification timestamps are kept along with the receipt. The roles use separate checkouts on the same filesystem.

Use 4,096 independent draws for each of four unit-stratum cells. For the four indicators V, M, V and not M, not V and M, report two-sided Hoeffding intervals (familywise alpha 1/20 over sixteen cell-indicators). The radius is approximately 0.0281, smaller than the designed separation of the stratum/mixture thresholds. Within each declared mixture, form simultaneous linear endpoint intervals from the corresponding strata. Bound conditional false-negative rates by dividing simultaneous numerator and violation intervals, with upper bound 1 when the violation lower endpoint is zero. Intersect every exact predicted endpoint envelope for risk and missed risk with the corresponding held-out simultaneous interval. Confirm each preregistered threshold verdict only when its maximum-over-contexts interval is on the forecast side of the relevant threshold. Report discrepancies or unresolved cases. The synthetic route card verifies the bounded controlled instance and makes no claim about external adversaries, transfer to a different completion class, or real event prevalence.
