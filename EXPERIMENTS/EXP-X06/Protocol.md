- In the admission-at-publish completion, receipt admits no ticket and the renderer initially separates all histories. `publish` atomically admits the ticket and retains the renderer.

- All operations cost one for `publish` or the configured stratum-specific cost for `attest`. Support is deterministic for the fixed request world and exhaustively instantiated for both worlds. Both operations terminate at `settled`. The source exposes only the complete operations.
