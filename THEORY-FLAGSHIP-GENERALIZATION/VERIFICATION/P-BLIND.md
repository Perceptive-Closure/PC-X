## G01.2 · Mesh correction and strict examples

The stated boundaries can be witnessed by the following rational data.

- **Irrational common controller.** Use two legal literals and let \(x\in[0,1]\) be the probability of the first. For a continuous world parameter \(t\in[1/4,1]\), require \(f(t)\le x\le g(t)\) at every world, where \(f(t)=t/(1+2t^2)\) and \(g(t)=(1+2t^2)/(8t)\). - **No finite exact subsystem.** Let \(x,t\in[0,1]\) and require \(t(x-t)\le0\) for every world \(t\). All coefficients are continuous polynomials. The full feasible set is \(\{0\}\). - **Unattained world extremum.** On the compact set \([0,1]\), use score \(t\) when \(t<1\) and zero at one. The supremum is one and no world attains it.

For each \(s\in[0,1]\), the linear functional \((a,b)\mapsto b-2sa\) has its unique minimum over the convex hull on the point \((s,s^2)\), since its value on the generating curve is \((t-s)^2-s^2\).
