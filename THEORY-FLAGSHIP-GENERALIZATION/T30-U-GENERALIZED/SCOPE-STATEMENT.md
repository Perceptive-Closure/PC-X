# Amended neutral U scope for independent verification

27 November 2026. This addendum narrows the original [neutral statement seal](STATEMENTS.md) before release of the constructor proof. It is part of the exact candidate to reconstruct. All other conditions and conclusions are as stated there.

1. In U.G1 a delivered letter includes all actual newly visible data used by the public state update. Once that letter is fixed, the next public state has the common update \(s'=\sigma(t,s,a,o)\), independent of the tag, private state and parameters. In particular, a revealed next visible state whose law depends on the tag is included in the delivered letter and in the relevant tag's probability factor. The next hidden state retains its individual unifilar update. Count the resulting actual delivered-letter and factor alphabets in all bounds.
2. In U.G3 each upfront source record supplies fixed mechanism or revision facts only, never an installed value of \(\theta\). The guard and full-inspection receipts report only the declared revision facts about \(B\) and the applicable surcharge set, not its realized member. The full inspection and ordinary record report the family specification for the ordinary route and reveal no installed target information. For each fixed \(B,m,k,\beta\), the law of the complete authorized upfront records agrees at \(\theta=1/4\) and \(\theta=3/4\), including the optional calibration prefix when present. Comparators start with exactly these same source facts and share the same timing and charges.
3. U.G3 catalog disclosure cells retaining a bad revision retain a true-positive bad completion. The impossibility claim applies to such a cell, or to any other compatible class retaining a true-positive bad completion under the same finish and obligations. If an independently specified class contains no positives, the positive service floor alone imposes no issuance on it. The catalog minimum here concerns a realized good revision.

4. The close-pair conclusion in U.G4 includes the initial delivered record in the comparison. It has total variation at most the same \(\kappa\) for the selected pair, or is identical in both worlds. At most \(H\) further rows may be exposed by the finite hard-horizon policy. Across a coupled common prefix, a stopped record is padded with a deterministic identical no-op.
5. The optional calibration claim in U.G3 concerns a sharper margin issued only if the claimed actual bound follows uniformly over the intersection of the time-valid confidence set and the exact guard envelope. The conditional calibration model must hold under the actual admitted monitoring choices and on the specified source scope. The coverage proof does not justify arbitrary data-dependent reported values.

6. U.G1 prescribes ratios only at positive-flow states. Zero-flow states are unreachable under that realized policy. A total callable prescription there is provided only where an authorized default exists or the implementation contract independently requires and supplies one. A zero-inflow state with no authorized command or stop is admissible as an unused formal node.
