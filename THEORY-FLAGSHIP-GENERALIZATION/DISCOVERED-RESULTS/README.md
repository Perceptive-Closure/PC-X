- Two shared conditional draws give a nonpolyhedral complete-law envelope, exact disagreement risk \(1/2\), and one under an invalid independently switchable model. Weak compact constraints can have a finite refutation when infeasible while **no** finite subset preserves the optimum for a different feasible instance. [Continuous examples](../T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md)
- Independent verification can improve a conditional risk envelope without reducing compatible worlds. The guarded source fixture leaves positive unmatched mass while its signed risk \(3/19\) is sharper than a bound combining separate extremes \(3/17\). [Transport proofs](../T30-P-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md)
- A single pair of composite mixtures can obstruct every eligible entire design even where every simple opposing pair is distinguished. Another exact profile needs private design randomization to attain \(2/5\). [A proof](../T30-A-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md)
- Exact two-probe assurance \(7/12\), conditional risk \(5/16\), positive-witness utility \(25/32\), and count compression on the same continuous and strategic service. [U proof](../T30-U-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md)
- Rich repeatable separated bits bound full identity by a priced polynomial schedule on the same menu. Any general claim of stronger exponential separation must cope with such richer actual observations. [U.G4](../T30-U-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md)
