# Computational and acquisition boundaries

| Problem | Established guarantee | What is charged or remains unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact linked semialgebraic synthesis, G01.1 | Decision by real algebraic elimination, exact algebraic realizer, exact violating parameter and emptiness certificates. Infeasible compact realization needs at most \(d_X+1\) actual represented blocker worlds. | Fully expanded legal tree, rational bit sizes, payoff graph, all real quantifiers, coefficient expansion or expression gates, exact roots/certificates. General CAD can be doubly exponential in variables. Fixed-policy quadratic-box thresholds are already coNP-hard. |
| Mesh certificates, G01.2 and U.G2 | Relaxed and tightened programs with justified exact guard and modulus. U gives \(L\le C^*\le U\) under respective feasibility and a margin-based interval. | A compact continuum may require infinitely many distinct constraints to represent the exact value using only world samples. A mesh size need not be polynomial in dimension. Closure alone does not produce a checked net or a useful slack witness. |
| Conditional common flow, G02.1 | Linear evaluation in displayed matched edges at a fixed world with complete conditional evidence, excluding arithmetic and source checks. | Matched-product expansion, finding valid subrows, disjoint verified fragment assembly, event provenance and all unmatched legality. No general automatic source validation. |
| Transport fact selection, G02.2 | Finite exact realized, uniform fixed and prospective worst-supported costs within a finite report menu and admitted envelope proof family. At most \(2^m\) distinct repair patterns. | Charge finite tuples, admissibility regions, nonnegative cost data, algebraic policy and blocker solves, report enumeration and all read stages. Hitting-set optimization is NP-hard, and prospective state count can be \(\prod_j(1+f_j)\). |
| Complete strategic transcripts, A.G1 | Rational LP polynomial in explicit finite world/tree data for local supported menus. Finite equality witnesses from compact class images, strict finite actual obstructions below minimax without compactness. | A full strategy list or even the full legal tree can be exponential in succinct input. In continuous classes a hull membership result is not automatically a tractable convex oracle. |
| Safeguard selection, A.G2 | Finite blocker hitting and exact realized price under a catalog of truthful nondisturbing source facts. NP-hard even for two-sign size-two block witnesses. | Proof of each fact, authorized source access, budget effects, full reference completions, realized versus prospective valuation. |
| Matched acquisition, A.G3 and U.G3 | Independent finite lower and upper laws on each actual menu. A gives \(5/4\) expected and 3 hard. U gives \(7/12\) expected and 1 hard. | Distinct menus and joint constraints. Prices exclude only the separately identified charged source/operational stages. These do not prove universal efficient optimal acquisition or a runtime speedup. |
| Prior-free parametric flow, U.G1 | At most \((H+1)|S|\prod_m |Z_m|\binom{H+r_m}{r_m}\) vertices, with at most \(|A||O|\) counted edges per vertex. Degree at most \(HD\) before score multiplication. U.G3 has \(t+1\) sufficient records at \(t\) and \(O(N^2)\) prelude states. | Charges for exact support compilation, rational coefficient bits, sufficient state labels, dimensions, number of tags and factors, numeric versus binary input horizon, verified callability and solver representation. The general exponent grows with the structure. |
| Longer horizons and richer menus, U.G4 | Tail-based error \(D b_H\), cost \(K\sum_{n\ge H}b_n\), or bounded discount \(\lambda^H\) times a known multiplier. Identity via lawful \(\sum_i c_i\lceil 2\gamma_i^{-2}\log(m/\delta)\rceil\). | Verified same controller cutoff with actual legal fallbacks, uniform tails where claimed, authorized repeated conditions, and a complete finite identifying array. Unrestricted infinite synthesis and general succinct complexity are open here. |

These are worst-case mathematical size, sample or cost statements. The baseline X16 runtime comparison remains mixed, with its explicit solver faster on six of eight profiles.
