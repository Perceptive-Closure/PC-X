| P continuous synthesis and algebraic certificates | Parametric transition reachability, semi-infinite finite-dimensional optimization, exact real algebraic decision, Helly and finite-dimensional realization. A more general result in the literature could imply parts of this construction. |
| Governed safeguarding and oversight | Reactive action shields and Bayesian decision-specific test acquisition establish prior safety and information results under their own conditions. The strongest candidate contribution is the compatibility of constructive evidence and a lawful common policy under correlated continuous and strategic uncertainty, with exact finite obstructions and lower/upper cost validation. Neither priority over all prior work nor an externally reviewed theorem is inferred.
