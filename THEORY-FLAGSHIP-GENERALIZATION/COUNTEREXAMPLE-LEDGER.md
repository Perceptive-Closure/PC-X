# Counterexamples and acceptance boundaries

These cases target specific weaker readings. Symbolic, rational and structural checks are mathematical artifacts. Historical adverse observations retain their separate experimental meaning.

| Challenged inference | Exact failure or restriction | Verified case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rectangularize linked parameters or test only ends | With two shared Bernoulli parameter draws, worst disagreement is \(1/2\), the two endpoints both give zero, and a switched-row model gives one. The convex hull of complete laws is nonpolyhedral. | [P examples](T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) |
| Finite blockers, attained extrema, rational probabilities without their hypotheses | With \(x>0\), \(\theta x\le\theta^2\) admits all finite subsystems but fails together. With \(x\ge0\), the exact maximum is zero and every finite subsystem has positive maximum. Discontinuous \(f(\theta)=\theta\) below 1, \(f(1)=0\) yields unattained supremum. A separate rational model forces \(x=1/\sqrt2\). | [P examples](T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) |
| Smooth uncertainty implies exact attainable discretization | Strict target thresholds require label strata. A feasible hard equality at one \(x\) can be erased by positive tightening. An actual mesh alone cannot prove absence of intervening violations. | [P examples](T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) · [U.G2](T30-U-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md#2-certified-deployment-value-interval-ug2) |
| Geometric outer cover countermodel equals actual obstruction | Add a spurious world with no callable useful literal to one actual safe world. The cover loses feasibility while the actual installation retains it. | [P examples](T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) |
| Equal support/marginals certify conditional transport | Fair bits can be equal almost surely in one world and opposite almost surely in another. Equal positive support with probabilities \(1/10\) and \(9/10\) likewise need not match risks. | [P examples](T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) |
| Omit unmatched context, real event decoding or out-of-scope policy | Uncovered \(1/5\) could be harmful. Matched visible fields can carry different raw violation meanings. Checking A with equal source rows says nothing about B sporting a different actual effect. | [P examples](T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) |
| Stack verified fragments without a joint evidence condition | Two independently valid checks may certify the same \(3/4\) ticket fragment. Summing them spuriously gives \(3/2\). Both marginals of the sum require domination. | [P examples](T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) |
| Evidence read continues the original physical state automatically | A measurement can erase a hidden bad state and then correctly report zero. It describes the written source. The unmeasured source remains harmful. | [P examples](T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) |
| Different actual literal invocations are interchangeable, or unlikely freezes can be ignored | An action may lack a common callable implementation. A positive raw successor moving a frozen resource bars the whole action even if its mass is \(1/100\). Complete nominal and unmatched continuations need exact guards. | [Common contract](COMMON-CONTRACT.md) · [P examples](T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) |
| Pairwise feasibility or a per-world controller proves common execution | Higher-order policy families can be feasible pair by pair and jointly empty. Whole linked legal observation sets determine the same action rule. | [Accepted T29/T30.CG](BASELINE-MANIFEST.md) · [G01](T30-P-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md) |
| Add in-scope risks only at activation | A diagnostic or other information-producing step can already violate. Coverage must include earlier true events. Resetting or shredding pre-issue effects invalidates the model. | [Common contract](COMMON-CONTRACT.md) · [A cases](T30-A-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) |
| Ignore closed composite hulls or privately split experiments when conditions fail | Opposite simple law families can differ everywhere and still share one vector of every experiment law. Leaking the chosen private design before the source is installed can restore chance error in the randomized example. A prior with equal sign weight need not assign equal weight to individual atoms. | [A cases](T30-A-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) · [A scope](T30-A-GENERALIZED/SCOPE-CLARIFICATIONS.md) |
| Equal evaluation behavior or output-only scope handles changes after verdict | An admitted responder can keep private state, see changed authority, and alter physical proposals. Unregistered memory, disclosure or anticipation of a private random draw changes the operative conditional law and may invalidate the claim. | [A cases](T30-A-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) · [U.G3](T30-U-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md#3-one-joint-source-and-optimal-lawful-use-ug3) |
| Calibrated marginal samples, optional stopping and conditional issuance are interchangeable | Repeating one hidden fair bit gives all unconditional means \(1/2\) while averages stay \(1/2\) away from the model mean. Repeated uncorrected looks are not time-valid. Issuing only on all of a bad event of mass \(\eta\) can make conditional error one. | [P dependent/calibration examples](T30-P-GENERALIZED/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md) |
| A finite uniform deadline needs only pointwise distinct targets | Uniformly close opposite complete laws at a fixed horizon give worst error at least \(1/2\) in supremum. If the startup record reveals the sign, a purported row-only obstruction misses that disclosure. | [U.G4](T30-U-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md#4-controlled-extensions-and-obstructions-ug4) · [Corrected scope](T30-U-GENERALIZED/SCOPE-STATEMENT.md) |
| Group by support or an incomplete observation letter | A tag-sensitive next visible state omitted from the counted letter loses distinguishing information and may double total mass. Equal support can mask different likelihoods, authorization, event flags, carried proposals and actual work. Distinct edges to the same counted state retain their multiplicity. | [U.G1](T30-U-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md#1-pointwise-joint-flow-and-implementation-ug1) |
| Source revision evidence may leak sign or positive coverage is unnecessary | A lawful ordinary receipt can reveal installed \(\theta\) without revealing the guard, defeating a paid-price bound. A bad cell known to contain only negatives can refuse without testing a positive service obligation. The amended class rules both out where the lower bounds apply. | [U neutral correction](T30-U-GENERALIZED/SCOPE-STATEMENT.md) |
| Project worst surcharge after arbitrary calibration | Issue on the first Bernoulli(\(\beta\)) bit being one. Its selected constraint can decrease over the surcharge interval as issue probability changes. The endpoint grid proof is restricted to a \(\beta\)-invariant prefatory law. | [Grid scope](T30-U-GENERALIZED/GRID-SCOPE-ADDENDUM.md) |
| Lawful approximation gives a lower bound on the unrestricted optimum | Deleting a cheap feasible action from the comparison polytope and retaining an expensive one can overstate the physical optimum. An upper controller needs a lift; the lower needs exhaustiveness. | [U.G2](T30-U-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md#2-certified-deployment-value-interval-ug2) |
| Small policy graph implies globally tractable preprocessing | The legal tree, report Cartesian product, exact support and continuous coefficient decision need independent size bounds; exact fixed-policy threshold is already coNP-hard. Polynomial size in numeric \(H\) is not polynomial size in its bit length. | [Complexity](COMPLEXITY-BOUNDARIES.md) |
| Almost-sure termination or discount gives hard safety | Deterministic \(\tau=k\) on arbitrary installations has no uniform tail. \(\Pr(\tau>n)=1/(n+1)\) gives infinite expected time. A catastrophe just beyond the cutoff can remain certain even with tiny discounted contribution. | [U.G4](T30-U-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md#4-controlled-extensions-and-obstructions-ug4) |
| A general target-acquisition separation follows from the point menu | Repeatable truthful separating identity probes can identify every genuine bit at polynomial total price under the same error and law. T07.K is scoped to a different point-query interface. | [U.G4](T30-U-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md#4-controlled-extensions-and-obstructions-ug4) · [Accepted T07.K](BASELINE-MANIFEST.md) |
| Pair an actual optional monitor to a nominal zero-surcharge monitor | Two actual Bernoulli(\(1/32\)) calibration reads produce at least one one with probability \(63/1024>1/32\), versus zero nominal. Pair the actual prefix or account for that discrepancy separately. | [Transport correction](T30-U-GENERALIZED/TRANSPORT-SCOPE-ADDENDUM.md) |
| Infer topological path safety from zero forbidden probability | A continuous uniform draw can include a forbidden singleton in its topological support while its event probability is zero. Use positive atoms or a separate exact path rule. | [Acceptance scope](ACCEPTANCE-SCOPE.md) |
| Treat a filtered report cell as a replacement for the original hard guarantee | A gives useful service at one world, is unavailable at an original world, and remains forbidden under the original guard even when the truthful report isolates the first. A relative blocker includes the fixed guard rows. | [P post-review addendum](T30-P-GENERALIZED/POST-REVIEW-ADDENDUM.md) |
