Each modification is relative to the exact accepted flagship. See the [accepted baseline and scope](BASELINE-MANIFEST.md), [P proofs](T30-P-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md), [A proofs](T30-A-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md), and [U proofs](T30-U-GENERALIZED/PROOFS.md).

| P1 known fixed conditional laws with a polytopic initial mixture of finitely many components | Replaced as a representation restriction | G01.1 permits rational-semialgebraic initial laws, conditional transition and observation kernels, scores and support guards. Two shared Bernoulli draws give a curved complete-law envelope unavailable from a finite polytopic mixture. |

A surcharge-reading prefatory record can change issuance, invalidating aggregate monotonicity. The general U.G2 interval still takes the whole indexed family. |

The quantitative scoped finite theorems retain actual source membership, exposure, independently available evidence, the charged controller class and all physical constraints. General simultaneous identification from arbitrary live behavior and general learning of the physical risk law remain unresolved or fail on the cited countermodels.
