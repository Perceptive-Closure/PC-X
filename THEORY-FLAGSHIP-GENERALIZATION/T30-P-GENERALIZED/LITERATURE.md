# G01/G02 · Primary literature and advancement

## G01. Nonlinear linked kernels and finite blocker groups

Hahn, Hermanns and Zhang compute rational reachability probability functions in parametric Markov models. The finite path multiplication and probability representation in G01 follow the established parametric verification principle. Winkler, Junges, Pérez and Katoen study selecting parameters for polynomial transition laws and the complexity of reachability, including variants complete for the existential theory of the reals. Their quantifiers and property classes vary by result. G01 requires one observation-measurable policy satisfying several constraints for every compatible world. Its Simple Max Cut reduction certifies a restricted coNP lower bound on one universal fixed-policy problem. General parametric reachability hardness is already established. [Hahn et al., original paper](https://depend.cs.uni-saarland.de/tools/bibitem/HahnHZ10.pdf) · [Winkler et al., original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.01503) · [Garey et al., Simple Max Cut](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975%2876%2990059-1)

Koller, Megiddo and von Stengel, §2, give the realization flow and chance-weighted payoff representation for extensive forms with perfect recall. Basu, Pollack and Roy give effective real quantifier elimination. Dubois and Stengle's algebraic emptiness results supply the negative-one identity after the sign constraints are converted to equations. None is new in G01. The constructive specialization obtains exact algebraic common executable policies, grouped world witnesses and checkable proof identities for linked nonlinear source probabilities, including independent hard authority. The example of a nonpolyhedral law hull verifies a strict enlargement over the accepted polytopic mixture method. [Sequence form](https://ai.stanford.edu/~koller/Papers/Koller%2Bal:GEB96.pdf) · [Basu et al., original QE paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/235809.235813) · [Dubois, original paper](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/116/7126-11512_2007_Article_BF02589551.pdf) · [Stengle, original paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01362149)

Shapiro's discussion of finite-support multipliers and discretization in semi-infinite convex optimization gives the broad context for these results. Compactness and finite-dimensional convexity yield finite infeasible groups, and a mesh needs a verified error or regularity margin. G01 explicitly separates failure of all worlds at a fixed cap from the finite-world exact optimality statement, which the constructive example disproves. Davenport and Heintz show general double-exponential lower bounds for elimination output and CAD. This does not make that bound an instance lower bound for the specific PC-X formula. The contribution is a source-grounded mathematical specialization of established methods, with a proved larger family than the prior PC-X result. [Shapiro](https://doi.org/10.1080/02331930902730070) · [Davenport and Heintz](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0747-7171%2888%2980004-X)

## G02. Partial controlled transport, evidence choice and calibration

Haesaert, Soudjani and Abate establish approximate probabilistic simulation and policy refinement using lifted transition couplings, an interface, and bounds on output discrepancy. Bian and Abate give a finite trace-distance product bound from approximate probabilistic bisimulation. The G02 subcoupling and residual mass inequality are specializations of those comparison ideas and accepted T18. G02 additionally uses single-policy or uniformly checked law-preserving source literals and observations, keeps the physical hard guard through uncovered continuations, and separately checks genuine terminal score intervals that can differ on a matched record. Applying the resulting absolute score intervals to the signed selective objectives uses probability accounting rather than a new TV inequality. [Haesaert et al.](https://doi.org/10.1137/16M1079397) · [Bian and Abate](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.04547)

Chandrasekaran, Karp, Moreno-Centeno and Vempala give an exact implicit hitting-set template from an unhit-pattern oracle. G02's advance beyond accepted P2 is its admissible proof-refinement pattern. A fact can increase verified common mass or tighten terminal scope without removing any compatible world, so merely distinguishing labels or eliminating blockers is insufficient. The proof orders independently witnessed non-overlapping components and usefully signed interval refinements. Its optimum applies to the pre-specified admissible proof family with independent source reality. No priority is claimed for the outer hitting-set algorithm, and priority for the combined operational oracle remains unresolved. [Chandrasekaran et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.1472)

Howard, Ramdas, McAuliffe and Sekhon give general time-uniform Chernoff bounds via nonnegative supermartingales. The conditional Hoeffding and predictable-skipping argument used here is a simple instance of the established framework. G02 connects it to a checked continuous-world parameter, allows drift with separately justified fixed bias, and retains actual hard authority and physical selective utility on the appropriate scopes. Calibration certifies only constraints from validated score laws. This is an operational extension of the accepted P3 stream class, with no new concentration principle. [Howard et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.03204)
