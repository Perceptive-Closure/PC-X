## U.1 · Realization and physical transfer

Fix a world \(w\). For a legal observation/action prefix \(h\), propagate a hidden row vector using the specified actions as interventions. Denote its mass of hidden state \(z\) by \(\alpha_w(h,z)\), and its sum by \(\lambda_w(h)\). On the next action and observed letter,
\[
\alpha_w(hao,z')=\sum_z\alpha_w(h,z)K_t(w,z,s(h),a;z',o).
\]
The initial vector includes the allowed initial observations. The \(\lambda_w\) values are chance and responder likelihoods with no controller probabilities multiplied in. A responder program is evaluated at its declared information record before the next fresh draw. Preserving \(w\) and its state across this update preserves all declared cross-row correlations.

An independent seed is an equivalent description of any finite behavioral policy by selecting random decisions for its finite records in advance. For a possibly correlated seed let \(x(h)\) be the seed mass that follows the actions in \(h\) if presented with the observations there. This is a function of the recorded prefix and independent seed law. The conditional physical law for those actions is the same throughout the participating seeds. Consequently the actual mass of \((h,z)\) in world \(w\) is \(x(h)\alpha_w(h,z)\). This factorization applies to randomized tests that privately remember a seed when the seed changes the process only through the recorded actions or disclosures. If the responder can use the planner's selected code or an extra seed field as a cue, its actual delivery must instead be a declared action or observation in the model.

Write \(x(h,a)\) for the seed mass that also takes \(a\) there. The constraints are
\[
x(h)=1\text{ at each allowed initial record},\quad
\sum_a x(h,a)=x(h),\quad
x(hao)=x(h,a),\quad x(h,a)\ge0.
\]
The middle equality is per controller record. The next-observation equality replicates the controller factor across all possible letters. Chance probabilities are accounted for in \(\alpha_w\), so adding the two kinds of factors would count chance twice. Use only the literal actions lawful over the conditional support \(\bigcup_w\{z:\alpha_w(h,z)>0\}\). A possible hidden state with positive prefix probability cannot be ignored by choosing a private seed differently. It remains possible on each compatible seed with positive probability of that action prefix. A state with no permitted continuation supplies no outgoing flow unless it is a terminal allowed by the actual contract. Unreachable zero-factors do not require an artificial physical action.

Conversely, a feasible set of factors gives the lawful behavioral ratios \(x(h,a)/x(h)\) when \(x(h)>0\). Induction along each history gives exactly the same worldwise occupancies as in the algebra. At an impossible history its prescription is irrelevant. Each stage or terminal outcome indicator or reward has a rational contribution linear in these factors. For example the stage conditional transition contribution is \(x(h,a)\alpha_w(h,z)K_t(w,z,s(h),a;z',o)\). Affine constraints on these expectations therefore give an exact LP. For a declared selective event use the difference \(\Pr(V\cap I)-\varepsilon\Pr(I)\). It is a conditional bound only when the denominator is positive. A positive separate floor makes the denominator positive on every world where that claim is required. This does not impose a negative-world activation floor when the objective requires correct negative decisions.

The feasible action-factor polytope is bounded by one and closed. Its linear image is a polytope. A worst expected objective uses an epigraph coordinate bounded by the maximum declared finite path cost when feasible. Standard rational LP gives an attaining rational solution or a finite exact dual feasibility or infeasibility certificate. To impose a hard cap \(b\), use the indicator of exceeding \(b\) as another terminal coordinate and impose zero in every world. Since all actual distributions are finite, that removes every positive-support violating path. Test the sorted finite list of all lawful leaf cost sums, plus zero, for a sharp supported cap. This may require building the path-cost register or enumerating leaf prices. There is no claim that numerical expectations give a pathwise cap.

The source conditions give the actual abstract law by induction from the checked start. For an actual history mapping to \((h,z)\), the same action and independently checked row advance its projection. Sum over the concrete histories with that map, using actual event and work decoding before taking the statistics. Thus a lawful policy implementing the abstract prescription has the predicted whole-trajectory projection, including any risk from information-producing work when that risk is in scope. A reverse record-and-choice correspondence maps every admitted physical policy back to the represented class with the same metrics, giving exact physical optimality when world coverage is exact. A checked forward implementation and outer coverage prove a conservative guarantee. Their inverse failure need not be a physical impossibility.

With exact finite worlds the initial target image is empty, homogeneous or mixed. In the mixed case select one world of each label. An independent target-only feasibility test can remove the proposed utility constraints and explicitly retain all interface and error constraints of that comparator. Its infeasibility is a certificate for that interface and cap, and neither it nor a failure of joint realization makes a positive intrinsic label negative. An unresolved computation cannot supply such a certificate.

## U.2 · Preserving all fixed-world constraints on one state graph

Under the reference distribution \(p\), conditional on \(h\) at positive mass, the factor \(x(h)\) cancels in the normalization. Thus
\[
b_h(w,z)=\frac{p_w\alpha_w(h,z)}{\sum_{v,z'}p_v\alpha_v(h,z')}.
\]
It depends on the fully recorded actions and observations, rather than on how the action choices were randomized. Write \(q=(t,s,b_h)\). The possible worlds and current hidden states are exactly the positive coordinates. Time, the lawfully recorded state and the actual command all enter the row. The mixture chance law for \(o\) at \((q,a)\) is
\[
 P(o\mid q,a)=\sum_{w,z,z'} b_h(w,z)K_t(w,z,s,a;z',o).
\]
Bayes normalization of its summands determines the next \(b\), and the public update determines the next \(s\). With a fixed finite state and the required guard markings the lawful literal set has the same value for all histories in this \(q\). Thus the next-state law is common for all those histories. Payoffs can depend on actual \(w,z,z'\) and \(o\) as in the statement.

For an arbitrary full-history policy define reference-mixture occupancies \(m(q)\) and \(f(q,a)\) by its actual probabilities. The action sum is \(m(q)\). The inflow is the initial mass if any plus \(\sum_{q',a'} f(q',a')P(q\mid q',a')\) from the preceding layer. Conversely a nonnegative feasible flow implements \(\Pr(a\mid q)=f(q,a)/m(q)\) at occupied states. Forward induction on \(t\) reproduces exactly those occupancies. All source-lawful copies of the same \(q\) share their action menu. If a budget-violation flag is used instead of filtering choices, its zero-probability constraints are among the preserved coordinates.

Conditioned on \((q,a)\), the hidden \((w,z)\) law is \(b_h(w,z)\). To see this, sum over the histories assigned to \(q\). Given any such \(h\), the hidden distribution is the same, and the ensuing action uses only the record and independent choice. Hence for any stage metric \(F_w(t,z,s,a,z',o)\) its expected value in world \(w\) is obtained by summing
\[
\frac{f(q,a)}{p_w}
 \sum_{z,z',o} b_h(w,z) K_t(w,z,s,a;z',o) F_w(t,z,s,a,z',o)
\]
over its layers and adding the analogous \(m(q)b_h(w,z)/p_w\) terminal terms. This matches every fixed-world coordinate simultaneously. No unknown world's value is averaged away. The two directions prove equality of the feasible metric images. Policy projection preserves those metrics, not arbitrary unrecorded path functionals. An additional path statistic needs a state update or a separate declared coordinate with a correct finite augmentation.

For the unifilar subclass in the statement, each compatible world contributes one hidden-state entry and its unnormalized likelihood product for a given history. Each incompatible world contributes zero. With \(r_w\) positive row-likelihood values this product is specified by at most \(H\) factors, hence by a nonnegative vector of \(r_w\) counts of sum at most \(H\). There are \({H+r_w\choose r_w}\) count vectors by adjoining the unused count as a last entry. Multiplying each world's options and the public and clock possibilities gives the stated bound. Normalization and collisions can reduce that number. Start with the exact state and enumerate legal actions and supported next records from each newly found posterior. Use exact rational equality to merge them. This avoids unfolding the histories whenever the reachable set is small. The input and bit bounds are recorded separately. No rectangular independence assumption appears in the count or flow.

## U.3 · Combining distinct calibration and operation errors

Fix the actual world and let \(B=\{w\notin W(D)\}\) be the data-driven coverage failure. For any allowed \(D\notin B\) the conditional identification failure is at most \(\delta\) and conditional risk is at most \(\varepsilon\) under the chosen policy. The bad outcomes contribute at most one. Thus total unconditional false-decision probability is at most \(\Pr(B)+(1-\Pr(B))\delta\); the same calculation gives \(\Pr(B)+(1-\Pr(B))\varepsilon\) for risk. Apply the union bound on each covered outcome and use one on bad outcomes to obtain the asserted joint error bound. For an in-scope calibration action the whole-attempt loss must include that action. The proof does not transport a later guarantee backward onto an unmodeled hazardous acquisition step.

For selection, on covered outcomes apply the actual conditional inequality \(\Pr(V\cap I\mid D)\le\varepsilon\Pr(I\mid D)\). On \(B\) bound the joint mass by \(\Pr(I\mid D)\). Sum to get
\[
 \Pr(V\cap I)\le\varepsilon\Pr(I)
                  +(1-\varepsilon)\Pr(B\cap I).
\]
Use \(\Pr(B\cap I)\le\min\{\eta,\Pr(I)\}\) and divide when \(\Pr(I)>0\). This is purely a decomposition of probability and uses no conditional independence between the selected certificate and the coverage event. A true-positive utility lower bound can similarly be multiplied by \(1-\eta\) when the same nonnegative utility is otherwise taken as zero for a conservative lower bound. The separately recognized P confidence construction supplies one possible simultaneous-coverage premise within its trusted diagnostic scope. No such diagnostic theorem proves physical membership without an admissible calibration and source interface.

## U.4 · Sharp acquisition with actual linked work

An exact finite source realization has a read-only enrolled threshold table and two separate authorized draws. The dry invocation tests its fresh word against that table, authenticates the bit, and leaves the cell and the work artifact unchanged. The finishing invocation checks the literal route against the intake's installed route, appends the route and note to the durable product and flags the real live violation by its own fresh word. Giving different integer ticket names with the same uniform decoded table leaves the projected law. The responder can affect the entire prelude and its note after the disclosed verdict while the protected threshold and draw channels are invariant. That invariance is an independently checkable source-dataflow restriction. It does not require an audit trace to predict arbitrary post-certificate behavior. Choosing the route preserves actual authority because the work receipt reveals the unique currently executable finishing literal. Without the receipt, the two possible routes have no common finishing literal at that record.

Fix the same \(k,m\) in opposite \(b\) worlds. Before the optional paid read the actual full law, including the controller's seed, work receipt and prior nuisance history, is the same. The probability \(a\) of making that paid read is consequently the same in the pair. Conditional on a read the differing bit distributions have total variation \(1/2\). Conditional on skip the remainder of the predecision record is the same. The full record TV is at most \(a/2\), by summing the history-conditioned TV or coupling the shared histories and bit. Processing the record cannot increase TV. A policy with both errors at most \(\delta\) must have positive declaration probabilities differing by at least \(1-2\delta\), so \(a\ge 2-4\delta\). The same argument at \(a\le 1\) gives infeasibility below \(1/4\). For \(\delta<1/2\) any feasible policy has positive paid-read mass and unit worst supported cost. This lower bound is against all admitted history-dependent tests and is valid for the joint optimization because dropping physical and utility constraints still yields it.

Call with probability \(a=2-4\delta\) independently of the previous record. After the call report positive when the dry bit is zero. Otherwise pick either verdict with equal probability on skipping. Under either type the uniform error is \(a/4+(1-a)/2=\delta\). In a positive world activation probability and useful completion expectation are \(1-\delta\), and whole-attempt physical risk is \((1-\delta)/4\). In a negative world activation probability is \(\delta\) and whole-attempt physical risk is \(3\delta/4\). Both are at most \(2/5\) in the stated range. The positive utility is a completed real task indicator. If usefulness additionally requires absence of the counted violation, the same controller gives expectation \(3(1-\delta)/4\) on positive worlds for that separate metric. Both work stages respect the source guard, the two work units, and the common move cap. Some numerical risk is unavoidable for positive coverage because every completed positive-world task has risk \(1/4\) at the final draw.

On a negative world with positive activation, the last independent draw gives conditional risk \(3/4>2/5\). Thus a guarantee on every activated world would force zero negative activation. On a matched opposite pair every finite full pre-verdict record under any policy has common support, because the same actions are taken at the same lawfully observed histories and both score outcomes have positive probability. This equivalence persists if a private seed is retained by integrating its common law, and also after the declaration. Any positive activation mass in the positive world therefore entails positive mass in the negative world. This contradicts the alternative uniform selective requirement. At no point does a model-average risk imply the per-world selective statement.

For the growth comparison in the prelude, let \(j\) count steps where the successive bit stays unchanged, with an initial previous bit zero. Their probability is \(2/3\) under \(m=0\) and \(1/3\) under \(m=1\). At length \(t\) the nuisance likelihood ratio for \(m=0\) versus \(m=1\) is \(2^{2j-t}\). The remembered bit is determined by the parity of \(t-j\). Each \(j=0,\ldots,t\) is reached. Thus \(2^t\) full records before the intake give exactly \(t+1\) posterior states with the relevant memory. Fixed-width subsequent routing, score, verdict and finishing stages multiply the count by a constant. This gives a quadratic accumulated prelude count and exponential explicit-history count in the same family with nonvacuous utility, positive risk and genuine response dependence. The claim concerns state enumeration and rational optimization size, rather than general wall-time dominance.

## Recoveries and compatibility

To recover the support-robust T30.CG operational objective, expand the exact finite accepting-graph installations into immutable tags. Assign every supported row outcome a strictly positive probability, for example uniform in the named row, retaining exact literals, authority, effect, receipt and work. Require zero identification error, zero safety error, useful terminal indicator one on each positive world, and the original supported price cap. All positive-support bad paths then violate an exact zero- or unit-probability condition. Each positive-probability action can be selected deterministically at its corresponding record without admitting a new supported path. The reachable observations therefore have the same supported semantics as the original. The associated cost optima agree. The number of turns allowed for a separately counted report can be padded, preserving the actual substantive move cap. This representation may enumerate every accepting path, and it imports no width bound from T30.CG or unnatural prior assigning zero to a real installation.

For the pure predeployment T30.C comparison a complete static panel can be one explicit action with the sum price and genuine vector of simultaneous readbacks. Remove the utility constraint and require exact separate class verdict. This recovers the same objective with a potentially much larger panel action set. It does not make early predeployment queries stage-local or inherit their validity when the act changes the installation. D-35 uses the same checked E/R/A no-touch exclusion on every raw positive successor. T18's exact conditional correspondence is the zero-lost-mass special case adopted in U.1. Limited stochastic loss envelopes require separately imposed valid inequalities, as in P.1. The finite strategic test/frontier of A with sufficient trusted likelihood and actual continuation guard is a special case of the finite U objective at a fixed cap; its almost-sure stopping theorem remains a different scope.

A literal use of P's external reporting and fresh protected attempt requires no audit carry into that attempt. It does not prove the warm start of a strategic mechanism. A uses a guarded conditional event bound that covers actual warm starts. On the common exact finite fragment, the reference/deployment row checks of P, the protected probe and genuine event link of A, and U's optimization apply jointly when independently checked. U.4 gives a concrete witness. In it the same source threshold controls the probe and final chance event, the responder may still update and act after the cue, and the two linked jobs preserve authority only through the genuine first receipt. The two narrower strands' different full mathematical domains are not presumed equal.
