# T30.P · Primary-source comparisons

Research date 27 November 2026. Mathematical originality is assessed separately from extension of the accepted PC-X proof registry.

## P1. Source comparison and constrained policy synthesis

Koller, Megiddo and von Stengel (1996), §2, use perfect recall to index a player's own action sequences, constrain their realization probabilities with one flow equality per information set and recover behavioral strategies by ratios at positive predecessor probabilities. Terminal payoffs are weighted by the chance probability of the relevant leaves. T30.P1's sequence flow and implementability argument are this known representation applied to the source-checked union of histories. Treating an independently verified forbidden operation as a nonnegative algebraic marker then implements a hard support condition. It adds a checkable physical premise and consequence to the usual given game tree. [Koller, Megiddo and von Stengel, original article](https://ai.stanford.edu/~koller/Papers/Koller%2Bal:GEB96.pdf)

Ben-Tal and Nemirovski (1999), §1, impose feasibility for all coefficient data in a represented uncertainty set and optimize the resulting robust counterpart. Polyhedral uncertainty in the linear coefficients gives the finite dual formulation through ordinary LP duality. This already supplies the computational idea used here when \(x\) and per-component loss vectors are available. T30.P1 separately proves when a source yields those loss vectors for every policy on the lawful records. Balke and Pearl (1997), §3, also optimize over a polytope of latent response-type probabilities, using constraints from instrumented data to get sharp causal bounds. Their optimization keeps the intervention and contrast fixed. The protected policy in T30.P1 is an additional decision, and the weights are tied across source-checked report, diagnostic and operational contexts. [Ben-Tal and Nemirovski, original article](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-6377(99)00016-4) · [Balke and Pearl, original article](https://ftp.cs.ucla.edu/pub/stat_ser/r199-jasa.pdf)

Kim, Lee, Kim and Poupart (2011), §§2–4, already show deterministic policies can be suboptimal in a chance-and-observation model with fixed transition and observation kernels and expected-cost constraints. Their randomized constrained dynamic program carries budget information. T30.P4 supplies a different numerical instance, where full lawful work and one genuine receipt are required and worst-case risk ranges over a fixed linked family. Randomized-control necessity as a general phenomenon is established earlier. [Kim, Lee, Kim and Poupart, original proceedings article](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2011/329)

Saghafian (2018) distinguishes uncertain transition models and chance conditional on a model, optimizing an ambiguity-sensitive expected utility under his APOMDP assumptions. Nakao, Jiang and Shen analyze an alternate robust POMDP with moment ambiguity in transition-observation distributions and feedback on realized transition-observation probabilities. Their belief recursion relies on the particular ambiguity and feedback model. T30.P instead learns constraints on the one fixed prior over persistent finite source components, with hidden transition memory and complete histories after obtaining lawful observations. Its polynomial LP is in the expanded tree and explicit polytope, while their dynamic results address different state and information structures. This is a restricted representation, with no comparative dominance. [Saghafian, original article](https://saghafian.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum11646/files/saghafian/files/apomdp-saghafian.pdf) · [Nakao, Jiang and Shen, primary article](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05988)

Bian and Abate (2017), Theorems 4–5, derive a sharp finite trace-distance bound from approximate probabilistic bisimulation. The established T18 argument already constructs a common submeasure for gated source events from verified local lower mass. P1 carries that argument through one governed controller realization and linked latent weights. The undercoverage formula follows from splitting common and residual mass. Pearl and Bareinboim (2014) characterize a different transport question from selection diagrams, available interventions and population invariances. P1's tied enrollment and local row comparisons are independently supplied source facts. They can potentially follow from richer proved models, and the present transport result makes no inference of causal invariance from observational similarity. [Bian and Abate, original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.04547) · [Pearl and Bareinboim, primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01603)

**Disposition.** Known constituent methods with an operational specialization. The additional guarantee over M03 is universal in governed protected policies with one linked source compilation and constructive executable controller output. Per-component row fidelity remains an essential source premise. The proof gives no new general robust-POMDP method or approximate probabilistic bisimulation bound.

## P2. Minimum evidence for common operational feasibility

Chandrasekaran, Karp, Moreno-Centeno and Vempala (2011), §2.1, show how to solve an implicit hitting set exactly by repeatedly solving a master problem over known patterns and obtaining an unhit pattern or acceptance from an oracle. This is the same master proof as T14 and M03 when the oracle returns an opposite completion. Hyafil and Rivest (1976) prove hardness of minimizing expected identification depth in a binary decision tree with a specified object class. T30.P2's prospective recursion has a different objective and terminal predicate, with its own worst-path hardness reduction. [Chandrasekaran et al., original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.1472) · [Hyafil and Rivest, original article](https://doi.org/10.1016/0020-0190(76)90095-8)

The additional capability is an exact oracle for minimum sufficient signed reports that must secure a *shared executable policy*, even when every available world individually shares the same intrinsic target and is individually useful. In that case there may be no opposing-target witness at all. The finite grouped obstruction and its dimension bound follow from classical separation and finite convexity. The proof extracts actual region members from a compact LP infeasibility certificate, so it can check groups without enumerating a linked region's vertices. Once these groups are supplied, the minimum cover itself is an instance of the known oracle optimization. The matched worst-path prospective runtime remains combinatorial. [Extraction and minimum cover](PROOFS.md#2-t30p2--joint-certificate-blockers-and-prospective-optimum)

**Disposition.** A substantive scoped advance over the prior PC-X opposite-completion oracle. Exact operational specialization of known robust optimization, convex feasibility and implicit hitting set. Whether an earlier physical audit model combines joint policy blockers, the same source-checked report interface and the compact realizing-law oracle is unresolved.

## P3. Statistical certification and its limits

Hoeffding (1963), Theorem 2, gives the bounded independent-sum tail inequality. Spending a summable fraction of the confidence failure probability across all stream-index pairs gives elementary stopping-valid confidence; tighter alternatives for more general nonparametric and martingale setups are already developed by Howard, Ramdas, McAuliffe and Sekhon (2021), especially the boundary and stitching constructions in §§2–3. P3 uses the simple union bound because it accommodates registered predictable selection with explicit reset premises. Its role is to ensure the same admitted weight vector remains feasible during policy selection. [Hoeffding, original paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830) · [Howard et al., original article](https://doi.org/10.1214/20-AOS1991)

Luedtke and Ahmed (2008) analyze when substitution of an empirical distribution and a changed nominal chance threshold yields bounds on the original optimal value or feasible candidate solutions. P3 does robust optimization over a simultaneous source-calibrated law set with a separate guard for unconditional authority. It retains the ordinary distinction between sampling confidence and physical chance risk. Its stopped-record \(\varepsilon,\delta\) accounting and coupling lower bound are standard probability methods. The joint moment margin is a sufficient operational condition for a feasible decision by a deadline, including availability of a *common* useful policy at unresolved laws. [Luedtke and Ahmed, original article](https://doi.org/10.1137/070702928) · [T30.P3 proof](PROOFS.md#3-t30p3--coverage-stopped-decisions-and-joint-physical-bounds)

**Disposition.** Operational specialization. The statistical method alone gives no new concentration bound, no general optimal stopping rule and no guarantee for unvalidated source law. The margin and guard requirements specify what can be certified in the same governed model.
