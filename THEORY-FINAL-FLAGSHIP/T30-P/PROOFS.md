## 1. T30.P1 · Transport, hard legality and chance constraints

### Equality of source laws

A controller sees only its declared subrecord.

First condition on the controller seed. The seed fixes the decision at each possible lawful record, as a function of that record and the seed. The initial decoded path probabilities agree by the verified initial law. At a positive-mass decoded prefix, the actual concrete prefix lies above its decoded state. If the controller selects a literal, every concrete refinement uses that same literal with its verified resource effects. At every such refinement the conditional probability of each decoded continuation is the same declared row mass. A mixture over these concrete refinements therefore has that row mass. Multiplication by the existing prefix probability proves prefix equality at the next stage. A zero prefix contributes zero. Absorbing leaves use the common padding rule. This proves equality of the decoded path laws with the seed fixed. Integrating the independent seed and then the one component draw proves the claimed equality for the fresh attempt. Thus conditioning on a reached audit record is permitted at each fixed actual \(w\). The terminal claims follow by pushing the equal laws through their genuine event, achieved work and charge maps.

The local row checks require complete execution support. For a primitive vector with conditional components, multiply along each positive conditional prefix, then sum over the vectors decoding to each target cell. The same structural check applies to every literal, so the proof is uniform over the controllers included in the skeleton. The protected controller seed only influences the source via represented invocations and effects.

### Exact sequence representation

For the computation use the marked tree, stopping at its first flagged operation. This tree never needs a probability law for the consequences of making a forbidden physical call. At such a checkpoint its formal edge to the marker has conditional mass one. For a lawful call that can breach an actual hard bound, first use its genuine chance row and then mark the offending successors. In particular the freeze test applies before sampling a row, and a single changing positive successor removes the entire source-atomic occurrence.

A randomized controller can be represented by independent seed values that specify pure decisions for every possible controller record. For each sequence let \(x_\sigma\) be the probability that this seed prescribes the indicated choices at that sequence's records, whether or not a nature draw reaches them. The subset of seeds matching a predecessor sequence partitions according to the next choice at each of its decision records. This gives the flow equations, including \(x_\varnothing=1\). Conditional on a seed matching the sequence of a leaf \(t\), the concrete nature-path factor \(k_i(t)\) is unchanged. Only the declared actions and effects influence the source. Hence that leaf has mass \(k_i(t)x_{\sigma(t)}\) in component \(i\).

Conversely, for any feasible realization flow \(x\), at a record \(h\) with \(x_{\sigma(h)}>0\) give each decision \(a\) probability \(x_{\sigma(h)a}/x_{\sigma(h)}\). These numbers sum to one. On a sequence whose last weight is positive all predecessor weights are positive, and the product of these ratios telescopes to its last weight. If its last weight is zero, the first zero extension has choice probability zero from its positive predecessor. For a zero predecessor record assign an arbitrary formal decision if needed. Such records have no supported execution as a result of their predecessor sequence. The law therefore has the specified leaf masses. The same argument applies to records whose nature probability is zero in all worlds of the guard. The actual controller only needs decisions on reached records. Outcomes can be sampled with independent private draws implementing the rational ratios.

For a fixed \(p\), every term in \(B_p(x)\) is nonnegative. If a controller at a reached record gives a positive probability to an invocation forbidden at a compatible concrete checkpoint, the compatible checkpoint has a positive nature factor. An analogous argument applies to a supported hard breach. Conversely a marked leaf of positive mass provides exactly one such failure. It follows that imposing \(B_p(x)=0\) for every world in the guard is equivalent to common callable choices and satisfaction of every declared hard cap on every supported path. Positive-support conventions and finite depth are used here. The marker cannot authorize an impossible invocation. The payoff equations for a guarded controller now sum exactly its physical terminal outcomes, without any marked branch. ### Compact robust linear program

For each desired inequality and each nonempty guard or calibrated region \(P=\{p:Ap\le a\}\), put the relevant component coefficients in a vector \(Lx\). Use \(Lx=B(x),V(x),-G(x),J(x)\) and right-hand side \(c=0,\varepsilon,-u,b\) respectively. The marker means are nonnegative, so their upper bound zero also imposes equality. The simplex conditions and any equality in a region may be included as pairs of weak inequalities. The scalar condition at this region is
\[
\max_{Ap\le a} (Lx)^\top p\le c.
\]
Its left side is attained and finite because \(P\) is nonempty and compact. Finite rational linear-programming duality, treating \(p\) as unrestricted with restrictions in \(A\), gives the equivalent constraint
\[
\exists y\ge0:\quad A^\top y=Lx,\qquad a^\top y\le c.
\]
Use a separate \(y\) for each inequality-region pair, with one shared \(x\). The constraints are linear in the joint variables. Each controller remains fixed across all parameter worlds. An attained conditional row or maximizing world can be different for different inequalities because every original guarantee is universal over the whole fixed world set. These optimizations cannot change the chosen component law within a path.

This LP has rational data. If feasible, a rational point and its dual certificates for the individual worst-case rows can be recovered by exact rational LP. If infeasible, a rational Farkas certificate exists. Bounded linear worst-case objective caps may be included as scalar variables. The projections of all these robust constraints onto the compact \(X\) are closed, since each is an intersection of closed halfspaces. A bounded objective epigraph cap then gives attainment. Rational LP gives rational optimal decisions and values. Each nonempty region has attained rational maxima and minima of \(\rho^\top p\). Uniform positive means the maximum over the union is \(\le\tau\). Uniform negative means its minimum is \(>\tau\). Otherwise rational extreme witnesses exhibit each sign. The weak and strict sides of the threshold are separated exactly, including equality.

### Common path submeasure

Its conditional mixture preserves the domination. A null lower prefix gives only a zero lower bound. Taking the mixture with the same fixed \(p\) retains domination. ## 2. T30.P2 · Joint certificate blockers and prospective optimum

### Extracting worlds from a compact infeasibility certificate

On a nonempty exact report cell, label extrema either supply an opposing rational world for the requested label or establish homogeneity. For a positive homogeneous cell with no protected policy, continue with the T30.P1 infeasibility certificate. Write \(Hx\le h\) for the realization polytope, encode all equalities in it as pairs, and index each robust row-region pair by \(\ell\). Its region is \(A_\ell p\le a_\ell\). Let \(L_\ell x\) be the vector of its component coefficients and \(c_\ell\) its upper bound. In the robust LP use
\[
L_\ell x-A_\ell^\top y_\ell=0,\qquad
a_\ell^\top y_\ell\le c_\ell,\qquad -y_\ell\le0.
\]
Apply the rational Farkas alternative to this whole system. Denote the multipliers for these three row types by \(z_\ell\) (free), \(\alpha_\ell\ge0\), and \(\beta_\ell\ge0\), and for \(Hx\le h\) by \(\lambda\ge0\). Its cancellation and strict contradiction give
\[
\lambda^\top H+\sum_\ell z_\ell^\top L_\ell=0,\qquad
A_\ell z_\ell=\alpha_\ell a_\ell-\beta_\ell,\qquad
\lambda^\top h+\sum_\ell\alpha_\ell c_\ell<0.
\]
If \(\alpha_\ell>0\), the rational weight \(p_\ell=z_\ell/\alpha_\ell\) lies in the original corresponding region because \(A_\ell p_\ell\le a_\ell\). Pair it with that region's report tuple. If \(\alpha_\ell=0\), then \(A_\ell z_\ell\le0\). A nonzero such vector would generate an unbounded ray from any point in the nonempty region, contrary to compactness. Thus \(z_\ell=0\) for zero multiplier rows. The displayed multipliers now give a contradiction using only \(Hx\le h\) and the actual robust inequality at each of the extracted worlds. This is a rational grouped infeasibility proof with no vertex search. If there are no extracted worlds, \(X\) itself is empty and any rational surviving world suffices as a blocker.

For nonempty \(X\), define for each extracted world the convex set of its feasible \(x\)'s inside \(X\), retaining all the constraints of that world. Delete redundant worlds until their intersection is minimally empty, using ordinary rational LP with these finite rows. If the minimal family had \(s>a_X+1\) worlds, choose \(x_j\) satisfying every set except \(j\). These \(s\) points in the affine hull of \(X\) have a nontrivial affine dependence. Separate its positive and negative coefficients and normalize each group to sum to one. The two convex combinations then give the same point \(z\). For every \(j\), at least one group omits \(j\). All points in that group lie in the \(j\)-th feasible set, so \(z\) lies in it by convexity. It would lie in every set, a contradiction. The resulting witness therefore has at most \(a_X+1\) worlds. Recompute an ordinary rational infeasibility certificate on them for independent checking. All worlds remain in their original report regions. They can be joint obstructions even when each world's own constraints are feasible.

This proves the exact hitting characterization.

Start with an empty family of blocker patterns. Solve the finite weighted cover for its least cost subset, resolve the corresponding cell, and return a sufficient subset if found. Otherwise a resulting nonempty pattern is disjoint from the subset and therefore genuinely absent from the present family. An empty pattern proves the full menu insufficient. At most \(2^m\) distinct patterns can be added. Every truly sufficient subset hits the accumulated family, so the cover optimum is a lower bound, and a returned sufficient subset attains it. A checker needs the source admissibility, the blocker worlds and their certificates, their report differences, the exact weighted cover optimum and the final decision and controller. The bound of \(a_X+1\) concerns worlds per blocker, not how many patterns are needed.

### Optimal prospective plan and refutation

At a valid leaf, a lawful correct verdict with its attached controller costs no further audit charge. At a nonempty nonterminal cell, success requires an unread command as the first action. The actual reply can be any reply whose retained cell is nonempty. Its state keeps only full tuples compatible with the complete observed path. The worst supported cost of that first choice is therefore its charge plus the largest least feasible continuation cost. Minimizing across possible first actions proves the recursion by induction on remaining reads. A cap is checked along each path, using the residual paid budget if needed. With fixed additive costs the consumed read set determines the spent amount. Record the minimizing read and actual controller witnesses when finite. When no allowed continuation attains a cap, each possible first read has either an exceeded charge or a response refuting its remaining budget. This is a decreasing-depth refutation with source-valid realizers for the responses.

A randomized exact audit succeeds only if each of its positive-probability first commands has successful continuation on every supported reply and respects the cap on every supported path. Thus one can select one supported first command and apply the same reasoning recursively, obtaining a deterministic exact audit with no larger worst supported cost. ## 3. T30.P3 · Coverage, stopped decisions and joint physical bounds

Fix an actual admitted pair \(w\) throughout the calculation. The menu's stipulated conditional Bernoulli laws allow one to construct a probabilistically equivalent execution by sampling independent arrays \(U_{j,n}\) of independent uniform variables and returning \(1_{\{U_{j,n}\le m_j(p)\}}\) at the \(n\)-th call of diagnostic \(j\). The legal rule only sees the selected prefix of these arrays. Equality of actual execution and this construction follows inductively by the stipulated conditional law at every selection and the same deterministic exact replies. The construction also represents arbitrary adapted stopping. Hence selection of unusually large estimates cannot change which confidence intervals need simultaneous validity.

For a registered stream and index \(n\ge1\), Hoeffding's two-sided inequality at the lower allowed radius gives error at most
\[
2\exp(-2n r(n)^2)=\frac{\delta}{k n(n+1)}.
\]
Clipping and outward rounding keep the true mean whenever this constraint succeeds. Sum over all \(k\) streams and \(n\ge1\) using \(\sum_{n\ge1} 1/[n(n+1)]=1\). With probability at least \(1-\delta\) every interval ever used contains the actual mean. Exact reads retain the true world with certainty by their checked semantics. To calculate a radius with rational output, obtain a certified rational enclosure of the real square root of width at most \(1/n\) and use the upper endpoint. Series with interval remainder bounds or standard rigorous interval arithmetic supply such enclosures. The mathematical error guarantee uses an upper endpoint, rather than an approximate floating-point nearest value.

Let \(\mathscr A\) describe the full stopped audit record and its selected positive policy when present. Write \(E_{\rm bad}\) for the stopped-record event \(w\notin C\). It is measurable by \(\mathscr A\) at fixed \(w\), and \(\Pr(E_{\rm bad}\cap I)\le\min(\delta,q)\). On \(I\setminus E_{\rm bad}\), conditional on \(\mathscr A\), the fresh attempt has actual violation probability at most \(\varepsilon\) and actual expected work at least \(u\). \[
\begin{split}
