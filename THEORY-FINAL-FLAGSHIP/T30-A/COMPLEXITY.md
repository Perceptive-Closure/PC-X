## Reach and source checking

Input consists of a checked finite compilation or an independently supplied inductive certificate, the source event decoder, the admitted profiles and the true information and call interfaces. Charge source inspection, random input verification, reach compilation, monetary paid probes, work, moves, runtime and memory separately. A history derivation can require exponentially many nodes in a succinct representation with finite horizon. For an indefinitely repeatable model use a closed finite guard invariant and independently check the continuing authorization. A fixed time or work cap can invalidate this extension.

With an explicit typed transition table and declared reached states, checking initial coverage, closure, common literals and whole-occurrence freeze is linear in the supplied rows up to equality tests, grouping records, and rational arithmetic. Check the local dataflow and primitive law on each required row, the physical decoder on every supported output, and the extra failure support at each linked and reference cut. Counting explicit truth tables is linear in their total size. Small boolean relationships can use verified finite arithmetic or a source equivalence proof instead of enumerated words. This accounting does not charge only a successful filtered trace. The size of the exact compatibility description and the cost of proving actual source correspondence remain separate.

For an ordinary-record matching certificate, store related concrete states or finite histories, matching initial law and rational coupling rows conditioned on each common positive-mass receipt. A supplied certificate is verified by checking marginals, observable equality, exact mass and relation support. Searching a finite pair table can use transportation feasibility for each designated node pair, command and receipt, with edges allowed only for equal ordinary observations and admissible successor pairs. Delete a node pair whenever such a condition fails. Iterating deletion terminates after at most the number of candidate pairs. The remaining relation is the greatest relation passing these local conditions. Check the initial coupling separately. This is a certificate search for the sufficient ordinary-record match condition. Nonexistence of its certificate does not establish total-variation separation of full laws. The search works on a pair of actual fixed programs or their checked finite machine representations. Enumerating candidate worlds or checking a succinct program class can have further cost.

## Finite acquisition

For A.2 a classifier may identify the finite \(p_\theta\) fiber using any verified informative repeatable probe \(i\). A sample cap \(n=\lceil2\log(2/\delta)/(r_i^2\Delta^2)\rceil\) suffices for the nearest-mean rule, with the disclosed rounding upwards. The confidence statement refers to the specified guarded laws. It gives no probability for omitted worlds or unchecked guard semantics.

For A.3 at hard horizon \(H\), every encountered paid total is a sum of \(H\) or fewer catalog prices. Let \(D_H\) be the number of distinct residual budgets generated by feasible nonnegative counts up to \(H\), starting from the specified cap. The memoized recurrence has at most \((H+1)(2H+1)D_H\) entries and examines \(|Q|\) probe candidates per entry. This is an arithmetic count. Scale rational costs to integer units of a common denominator \(d\). Then \(D_H\le\lfloor db\rfloor+1\), a pseudopolynomial money bound, and also \(D_H\le\binom{H+|Q|}{|Q|}\). An explicit horizon encoded in binary may be large even without many prices.

One can calculate in unnormalized pair likelihood mass instead of accumulating arbitrary conditional fractions. Form a common integer denominator \(D\) of the per-type protected transition rows, and write the score posterior at each node by the two integer powers of the numerator and denominator of \(\lambda\). For remaining horizon \(h\) each residual pair Bayes error value has an exact rational representation with denominator dividing a fixed initial posterior denominator times \(D^h\). This follows from representing the value by the Bayes failure of an attaining deterministic binary leaf rule, with ties allowed to be resolved by a deterministic choice before symmetrization. Padding unused slots by chance-one outcomes gives the denominator bound. A fair tie choice does not change the minimum Bayes value. The bit lengths are therefore polynomial in \(H\) and the encoded menu and protected input lengths. Expressing all reachable \((b,h,s)\) as an explicit policy table takes the stated recurrence size. The cost query “minimum cap at \(H\)” can enumerate all attainable total catalog charges no greater than \(H\max_i c_i\), evaluating a candidate cap by the recurrence and keeping the least feasible one. Ordinary moves cannot improve the value, so their possible charges do not create a smaller optimal cap. If the all-affordable cap fails there is a finite-horizon infeasibility certificate.

In the equal-price formula, binomial sums can be evaluated using successive rational probabilities or an exact finite convolution over revealed bits. No strategic tree or policy enumeration is required after the checked reduction.

## Expected cost

Use rational comparisons to select the least cost per reveal and bracket \(\delta\) between adjacent \(e_j\)'s. The index can be found by doubling and then binary searching powers of \(\lambda\). Exact power evaluation and comparison cost depends on the bit length of the produced integers. The exact output itself can require numbers of bit length \(O((m+1)\operatorname{bits}(\lambda))\); do not infer a uniform polynomial bound in the length of a succinct mean and confidence input when the sources are arbitrarily close. For instance \(q=1/2+2^{-n}\) held at fixed confidence has \(m\) of order \(2^n\). A symbolic boundary implementation can keep the private lottery parameters in their exact factored form and retain a score counter. This is a distinct model from a fixed horizon.

On an actually extended source with no bar to continuing sampling the rule attains the stated minimum expected charge. Above any finite cap there can be positive supported continuation probability, for example when a selected erasure probe repeatedly erases. It is possible to truncate at a finite cap with an explicit slack by forcing a decision at the cap. A crude valid tail bound for the chosen boundary and selected probe follows from the block argument in the proof. Truncated testing is a different confidence and budget calculation; the expected optimum is not a feasible witness at the unmodified confidence with any finite cap.

## What is optimized

The exact costs are paid optional information acquisition after fixed verification of the source class. The target can be certified with no knowledge of the responder's individual program in the guarded positive types. The results do not optimize a joint menu of probabilities, source inspections, and semantic verification. In particular they supply no cost dominance over a trusted direct law measurement newly admitted to the interface. Such a measurement can alter the matched law premise and the attainable optimum. The certificate is also limited to the registered useful work and guarded activation, irrespective of facts known about unrelated actions.
