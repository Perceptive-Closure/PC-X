[Bareinboim and Pearl, A General Algorithm for Deciding Transportability of Experimental Results](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.7485)

[Alshiekh et al., Safe Reinforcement Learning via Shielding](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.08611) · [Jansen et al., Safe Reinforcement Learning via Probabilistic Shields](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06096)

## Statistical decisions and acquisition

Wald and Wolfowitz prove that an SPRT for two simple hypotheses with independent observations minimizes expected sample count in each hypothesis among tests meeting its achieved error probabilities. At \(\delta=e_j\), the revealed-bit component of A.3 is a classical two-boundary SPRT. Its reflection symmetry and exact integer no-overshoot calculation explain the grid. For intermediate \(\delta\), the lower bound in this work uses the adjacent lattice secant and applies to the matched subtype of the entire strategic interface, including adaptive mixed purchase costs and arbitrary nuisance-dependent stopping. The interpolation is a specialization of the convex statistical decision structure. No originality claim is made for the SPRT, Bayesian posterior or optional information argument. The reviewed classical source establishes the optimum at its achieved error pair. It does not state the guarded adaptive menu reduction or the exact all-confidence frontier at this interface. Other sequential-analysis literature may already give the underlying two-point lattice formula. [Wald and Wolfowitz, Optimum Character of the Sequential Probability Ratio Test](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177730197) · [Author article reproduction](https://www.barkerstats.com/PDFs/COVID/%24AllfilesMax/perm_Wald_SPRT_Optimality.pdf)

The equal-price clause of A.3 is a direct application of independent erasure as a garbling, with the adaptive simulation checked in the actual interface. The binomial error is standard. [Blackwell, Comparison of Experiments, Theorems 1–2](https://digicoll.lib.berkeley.edu/record/112749) · [Blackwell, Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032)

Nitinawarat, Atia and Veeravalli study controlled sensing with fixed sample and sequential risks. Nitinawarat and Veeravalli give a broader controlled Markov setting, where the strategy asymptotically optimizes information per nonuniform sensing cost, and supply rules satisfying specified decision risks. A.3 additionally supplies a nonasymptotic exact optimum for every class confidence in the narrow symmetric lattice under the specified known interface, and an exact separate hard-budget frontier. It makes no claim to strengthen the general controlled Markov theorem. [Nitinawarat, Atia and Veeravalli, Controlled Sensing for Multihypothesis Testing](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.0858) · [Nitinawarat and Veeravalli, Controlled Sensing for Sequential Multihypothesis Testing with Controlled Markovian Observations and Non-Uniform Control Cost, Section 5](https://arxiv.org/html/1310.1844#S5) · [Deshmukh, Veeravalli and Bhashyam, Sequential Controlled Sensing for Composite Multihypothesis Testing](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.12697)

## Assessment

A.1–A.4 are a constructive source-grounded specialization. Each tool used in the proof has close precedents. The particular capability is to derive both an actual prescribed deployment label and a bound for provisioned linked useful work, with genuine responder drift allowed, then find the exact paid audit optimum on the specific information quotient and give a physically realized obstruction when the source coalition lacks the needed distinction. The acquisition and operational reading cannot be inferred from classical decision rules without checking that quotient, event decoding, callable actions and physical carry. No claim of precedence is made for stronger distinctions in a broader model.
