## Checking the source premises

A reachability certificate lists actual initial support, checked literal action tables and compatible physical states at each full lawful controller decision record. Start at each admitted installed type and responder or use an independently justified overapproximation of their guard behavior. Retain unexported fields if they can affect subsequent observation, event or authorization. For each retained physical prefix check the actual callable source invocations, delivered responder view, order of proposal and chance, and every positive-support raw successor. Compute the public child sets using the output actually delivered. Check extensional effects on physical resources, evidence, representation and authority before applying the freeze. A raw successor that violates the freeze disqualifies the entire occurrence. For a retained literal every actual branch at that controller record must permit it. Different literals with a common abstract label do not pass this check.

The checker verifies that the code reads the same immutable binding, identifies the original random input primitives that supply the draws at each guard cut, and follows the pure dataflow to the actual receipt and actual finalize outputs. A closed invariant can replace enumeration if it contains the initial support and is closed under every retained real row. An outer certificate yields a sound upper envelope. Its extreme rows require realized linked programs and starts to witness physical attainability. A support list cannot prove probabilities or independence. In an installation using the source pattern the uniformity and freshness of the primitive input words need separately authenticated source or calibration facts. The actual interpretation of finalize outcomes and physical completion needs an independent event decoder. A self-scored evaluator flag cannot supply that premise.

The auditor is given the authenticated receipt only at its declared location. The responder acts on its own delivered signals and may remember them. Commands can change physical or private state and the check follows those effects through activation. Information needed to pick a literal deployment route must be available in the actual lawful record. This requirement applies independently of the answer to the target classification problem. ## A.1 Guard transport and activation

Fix a world and a covered controller. Condition on the full pre-draw cut, including the concrete \(x\), responder private view and selected proposal \(a\). By the declared guarded random input law there are \(ML\) equiprobable pairs of words. Exactly \(|F_\theta^{-1}(1)|L\) satisfy the first disjunct of the checked event formula. The complementary source words contribute \((M-|F_\theta^{-1}(1)|)|D_{x,a}|\) disjoint violating pairs. The conditional violation probability is therefore
\[
\frac{|F_\theta^{-1}(1)|L + (M-|F_\theta^{-1}(1)|)|D_{x,a}|}{ML}
= p_\theta+(1-p_\theta)|D_{x,a}|/L.
\]
It lies between \(\ell_\theta\) and \(u_\theta\) at each such cut. These inequalities survive averaging over the actual conditional cut law at any reached activation record and over the cut law under the declared direct protocol. Each averaging uses the actual fixed world and preserves its correlations. No common evaluation and deployment proposal law is needed. A program that realizes the extremizing fraction almost surely in the specified protocol supplies the corresponding endpoint. Mere occurrence of such a cut is insufficient, because the admitted program might be required to reach other cuts with positive probability.

The upper bound at the reference start proves \(T(w)=+\) for \(u_\theta\le\varepsilon\). The strict lower bound proves \(T(w)=-\) for \(\ell_\theta>\varepsilon\). The same upper bound carries to all linked activation histories of a positive world, including those where the actual responder uses the verdict and its carried state. An actual positive output is exactly the decision to issue and activate. Thus on the positive world a wrong-or-abstain error is the complement of \(I\), which implies \(\Pr(I)\ge1-\delta\). Averaging over activation histories gives \(\Pr(V_D=1,I)\le\varepsilon\Pr(I)\). The expectation of \(G_D\) is exactly \(\Pr(I)-\Pr(V_D=1,I)\), hence at least \((1-\varepsilon)(1-\delta)\). On a negative world a positive output is itself an error, so \(\Pr(V_D=1,I)\le\Pr(I)\le\delta\). The audit and lawful nonactivation actions have zero actual violation risk, giving the whole-attempt bound \(\max\{\varepsilon,\delta\}\). On a negative world this joint guarantee places no small bound on the risk conditional on false issuance. All conditional comparisons require positive conditioning mass.

## A.2 Receipt fibers and the pair certificate

Write \(L_z(y\mid i)\) for the protected receipt law in a matched world \(z\) at a selected literal \(i\), with the dummy chance-one receipt on ordinary moves. Consider any full lawful audit record \(H\) with positive probability in both worlds. Induct on its length to couple the conditional hidden states using the source pair relation. The initial coupling can be conditioned on equal initialization records. The controller chooses the same action at the same record when its private seed is fixed. Because \(L_z(y\mid i)\) is independent of the concrete hidden prefix given the action, conditioning on a common-probability receipt multiplies the probabilities of all the compatible hidden states in each world by a constant in that world. Apply the certificate's conditional coupling at each related pair. Its remainder records are equal and its next states are related. On conditioning on the equal remainder the relation still supports a coupling of the two conditional states. Zero-mass records require no such conditioning.

Consequently a one-step extension of the full record has probability factor \(L_z(y\mid i)\) times a common conditional remainder factor, times the common controller factor if one is present. Equivalently hold the entire auditor seed fixed, use the same state-coupling argument, and integrate against the same independent seed law. These equalities apply with the seed included in the full record. Decisions, clocks and the fact of stopping introduce no extra hypothesis-dependent factor. On an almost surely finite audit the stopped-record conclusion follows by partitioning on the finite stopping time. Future deployment results are outside that audit record.

For opposite labels in one fiber the protected factors are equal. Their complete record laws agree for every design under one fixed pair of linked programs. If the common probabilities of \(+,-,\bot\) are \(a,b,c\), the sum of opposite errors is \((b+c)+(a+c)=1+c\). One error is at least \(1/2\). Equality of individual marginal fields would not establish the pair certificate. Opposite witnesses from a sound outer class can block that robust certificate, but physical impossibility requires realization.

Conversely, a single homogeneous fiber needs no samples. If there are several fibers and their labels are known, choose a probe \(i\) with \(r_i>0\). Repeat it a verified finite count \(n\), recording \(X_t=1\) for receipt one and zero for the other receipts. Given the full actual past the conditional mean remains \(r_i p_\theta\) and the conditional Bernoulli law is fixed. Hence the \(X_t\)'s on this fixed schedule have the product Bernoulli law even if other outputs and memories adapt. Let \(\Delta\) be the smallest positive gap between distinct \(p_\theta\)'s in the retained catalog. Output the label of a nearest \(r_i p_\theta\) to the sample proportion, with any tie rule. A wrong label requires an absolute deviation of at least \(r_i\Delta/2\). The probability is at most \(2\exp(-n r_i^2\Delta^2/2)\) by the bounded Bernoulli exponential inequality. Choosing \(n\) for the stated \(\delta\) gives a finite hard cap \(n c_i\). Each of these \(n\) commands and the actual linked activation must remain lawful under the chosen cap. If the direct endpoints of a crossing interval are realized by two matched worlds, their labels differ, which proves the stated special obstruction. An interval crossing without such witnesses leaves ambiguity.

## A.3 Finite hard budgets

For the lower bound put an analytical prior of \(1/2\) on each of the two fixed realizable matched worlds. This prior is a proof device. The protected receipt updates negative-to-positive odds by \(\lambda,\lambda^{-1},1\) for \(1,0,*\), respectively, on their common support. The ordinary-record factors cancel. Thus on the entire decision record the negative posterior is \(\pi_s\). The best terminal Bayes test incurs error \(e_{|s|}\), and abstention incurs one. A protected reveal gives a one conditionally with probability \(\bar p_s\). This law given the whole decision record depends only on \(s\) and the probe. An ordinary move leaves \(s\) unchanged. The lower-bound analysis may additionally retain realized paid costs if necessary, since the pair certificate matches these costs. The upper construction only uses known fixed protected prices.

Induct on the remaining optional moves. At \(h=0\) the terminal error is at least \(e_{|s|}\). The sealed \(B\) is nonincreasing in \(b\) and \(h\): increasing either retains existing choices, and the continuation values are nonincreasing by induction. At a stopping decision the conditional error is at least the stop term. On an affordable selected probe the induction lower bound is its exact continuation average in the recurrence. On an ordinary move its charged cost \(C\ge0\) and one move are used with unchanged \(s\), so the continuation is bounded below by \(B(b-C,h-1,s)\ge B(b,h,s)\). These bounds hold before averaging over policy randomization. Every robust policy obeying the caps also obeys them under the fixed matched pair. Its worst class error is at least its Bayes error, hence at least \(B(b,h,0)\).

For attainment, take an action minimizing the recurrence at each reached \((b,h,s)\) and stop with a most probable label, using a fair independent choice at a tie. The source check provides each selected literal. The same recurrence for this policy is exact under the equal-prior average in every admitted pair of types because it uses only the protected receipts. Also \(\pi_{-s}=1-\pi_s\) and \(\bar p_{-s}=1-\bar p_s\). Induction gives the same value for \(s\) and \(-s\) and identical minimizing actions. Use a common tie choice among those actions at mirror states. The two type-wise receipt processes, stop and decision are mirrors under that policy, so their errors are equal. This gives the minimax value, even though additional records outside the matched pair can be useful on some individual worlds.

When prices are equal, no design uses more than \(n=\min\{h,\lfloor b/c\rfloor\}\) paid probes. If \(r_*>0\), a best probe observation can be turned into an observation of probe \(i\) by retaining its revealed bit independently with probability \(r_i/r_*\) and erasing it otherwise. An auditor with a whole batch of \(n\) best observations can simulate every adaptive design's protected observations serially using this operation, and use the matched conditional remainder and controller kernels as shared-law auxiliary randomness. Its fixed batch contains at least as much Bayes information as the simulated output. At the actual price and horizon the batch is lawful. The reveal count \(J\) is independent of the hypothesis and distributed as \(\operatorname{Bin}(n,r_*)\). In the negative world the number of ones among those \(J\) revealed bits has law \(\operatorname{Bin}(J,q)\). The mirrored majority errors with a fair tie equal the stated expression. With \(r_*=0\) the matched pair again gives the fair-guess optimum.

## A.3 Expected cost under almost sure stopping

Use the same analytical prior. Put \(\kappa=1\) for the negative world and \(\kappa=-1\) for the positive world. For each optional move the increment \(X_t\) is \(+1,-1,0\) for a revealed one, revealed zero, or erasure or ordinary move, and is padded by zero after termination. Let \(N=\sum_t|X_t|\), \(S=\sum_tX_t\) and let \(C\) be actual paid charges. Given the actual world, past and chosen probe \(i\), the conditional means of \(|X_t|\) and \(\kappa X_t\) are \(r_i\) and \(\mu r_i\). On ordinary moves and padding both are zero. Put \(\gamma=\min_{i:r_i>0} c_i/r_i>0\). Comparing conditional expected charges term by term and using monotone convergence gives \(\mathbb E C\ge\gamma\mathbb E N\). For lower-bounding finite cost policies we have \(\sum_t\mathbb E |X_t|<\infty\), which justifies the signed summation \(\mathbb E(\kappa S)=\mu\mathbb E N\). This avoids an optional stopping claim for the possibly nonintegrable number of free ordinary moves.

The stopped-record posterior is \(\pi_S\) by partition into finite stopping times. Therefore
\[
\mu\mathbb E N=\mathbb E[S(2\pi_S-1)]
=\mathbb E[|S|(1-2e_{|S|})].
\]
The posterior Bayes error has expectation \(\mathbb E e_{|S|}\le\delta\) for any policy meeting the class-wise failure bounds. Let
\[
f(e)=(1-2e)\log((1-e)/e)/\log\lambda,\qquad 0<e\le1/2.
\]
Then \(f(e_j)=j(1-2e_j)\). It is strictly decreasing on \((0,1/2)\) and its second derivative there is \(1/[e^2(1-e)^2\log\lambda]>0\). Let \(A\) be the affine line through \((e_m,f(e_m))\) and \((e_{m+1},f(e_{m+1}))\) for the adjacent bracket of \(\delta\). The line has negative slope and is below \(f\) at all the grid points \(e_j\). This follows from convexity outside the interval between the two endpoints, equality at the endpoints and the absence of any grid point strictly between them. Hence
\[
\max_z\mathbb E_z C\ \ge\ \mathbb E C
\ \ge\ \frac{\gamma}{\mu}\mathbb E f(e_{|S|})
\ \ge\ \frac{\gamma}{\mu}A(\mathbb E e_{|S|})
\ \ge\ \frac{\gamma}{\mu}A(\delta).
\]
This is the sealed interpolation bound. Applying Jensen only to the smooth function would give a strictly weaker bound between adjacent points.

For attainment use a probe minimizing \(c_i/r_i\). A fixed boundary \(j>0\) is hit in finite mean optional moves. From any continuing position a block of \(2j\) consecutive revealed ones would hit a boundary. It has conditional probability at least \([r_i(1-q)]^{2j}>0\) when the draws are continued. This bounds the chance of continuing through successive blocks by a geometric tail. At stop \(|S|=j\), so the Bayes decision gives average error \(e_j\), and by mirror symmetry it has that error in both types. Costs also have the same expectations in both types. Since \(c_i=\gamma r_i\), the charge comparison is equality for this policy, giving cost \(\gamma j(1-2e_j)/\mu\) under each type. Boundary zero is the fair guess. The private lottery between adjacent boundaries attains the bound and the exact target error. The chosen probe and test ignore every nuisance field. Their protected sampling law is therefore the same for any admissible responder within the same type. A.1 supplies the corresponding linked operational guarantee.

At \(\delta=0\), the complete record laws of the pair at every finite time are mutually absolutely continuous: all revealed symbols have positive probability in both worlds, erasure support is shared, and the other factors match. Partitioning by finite stopping times preserves that property. Thus a zero-error decision cannot stop almost surely with opposite decisions in the two worlds. The argument includes hidden auditor randomization. A fair guess suffices at \(\delta\ge1/2\).
