# Primary mathematical baselines

27 November 2026. This intake records comparison tools and their assumptions. Specific originality dispositions depend on the final accepted statements. The mathematical baselines remain applicable regardless of the experimental outcomes.

## Probability, transport and semantic anchors

- Smallwood and Sondik's finite-horizon POMDP starts with a fixed controlled Markov and observation law. The posterior on hidden state supplies the standard law for control and supports finite-horizon value recursion. It can include a static unknown world as part of the hidden state. It supplies neither physical model membership nor independent justification of the hidden event and authority maps. [Smallwood and Sondik, 1973](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.21.5.1071)
- Iyengar's robust Bellman construction requires a rectangular law class for its rowwise optimization. A physical installation can constrain different visits to share one parameter and can prohibit particular combinations of admissible-looking rows. Applying rectangular recursion to the hull changes the comparator. Raskin and Sankur's multiple-environment model instead seeks one controller across a fixed unknown environment. This is a close prior model for a fixed-world operational problem. A quantitative theorem must explain what is added by the independently checked event, acquisition and callable source interface. [Iyengar, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.1040.0129) · [Raskin and Sankur, 2014](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSTTCS.2014.531)
- Pearl and Bareinboim use selection diagrams and do-calculus to decide transport of causal effects under explicit mechanisms and measured populations. Haesaert and Soudjani use approximate probabilistic simulations to lift controllers and quantify event error under related stochastic models. These are different, substantial prior transport frameworks. PC-X's proposed finite proofs must specify how actual row certificates and missing knowledge restrict the new joint policy guarantees. Shared labels or an observed trace alone supply no bridge. [Pearl and Bareinboim, 2014](https://doi.org/10.1214/14-STS486) · [Haesaert and Soudjani, 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.11445)
- Howard, Ramdas, McAuliffe and Sekhon derive time-uniform confidence sequences from supermartingale bounds. A finite or stopping-valid confidence construction is standard inference under its conditional law assumptions. Source validity, unconditional false issuance, selective reliability, and future deployment risk are separate issues. [Howard et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1214/20-AOS1991)

## Strategic information and joint optimization

- Koller, Megiddo and von Stengel give linear-size realization variables in the explicit perfect-recall game tree. The prior result explains sequence-form algebra and the improvement over normal-form enumeration. It supplies no deployed-risk label independently of the modeled game. M04 already applies this method to the PC-X audit interface. [Koller, Megiddo and von Stengel, 1996](https://ai.stanford.edu/~koller/Papers/Koller%2Bal%3AGEB96.pdf)
- Blackwell's comparison concerns simulation or decision risks under a common set of alternatives. A comparison based on adding an authorized richer read changes the observable experiment. It can defeat a lower bound on a more restricted menu. Evaluation-only equivalence needs the whole retained observable record at the declared authority and possible action histories. [Blackwell, 1951](https://digicoll.lib.berkeley.edu/record/112749)
- Chernoff studies sequential allocation for unknown hypotheses and costly observation choices under specified observation models. Kaufmann, Cappé and Garivier's change-of-measure bound relates adaptive expected sample counts to law divergence and binary decision error. To carry the same reasoning over to stateful strategies one needs a complete conditional law along history, and an admissible opposite pair or mixtures that actually induce those laws. A lower bound derived from separate row maxima can fail under linked responder histories. [Chernoff, 1959](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177706205) · [Kaufmann, Cappé and Garivier, 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.4443)
- Constrained partially observable control already studies maximizing reward under expected constraints. Occupancy or realization formulations with rational declared inputs are standard. A chance-risk flag can be put in the state to express a terminal expectation. A new common-model result must connect its optimism or infeasibility to independently checkable source licensing, law coherence, identification, fixed/conditional coverage, and an actual informative work protocol. [Isom, Meyn and Braatz, 2008](https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/2008/AAAI08-046.pdf)
- Formal shielding provides a prior enforced interface for permitted decisions or replacement of unsafe actions. A source guard must still be checked against every literal supported effect. An enforced restriction on responder actions can justify an operational condition without inferring agreement between evaluation and deployment behavior. [Alshiekh et al., 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.08611)
- General planning under partial observation has known hardness, so a complexity bound in a finite expanded tree or enumerated information-state set does not by itself improve succinct worst-case complexity. [Papadimitriou and Tsitsiklis, 1987](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.12.3.441)
