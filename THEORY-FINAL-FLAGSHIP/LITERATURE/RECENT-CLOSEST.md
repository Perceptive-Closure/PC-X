# Closest finite robust planning and testing sources

Theorem 1 and Definition 4 of Bovy, Probine, Suilen, Topcu and Jansen put a fixed environmental tag into a POMDP and treat a set of initial beliefs as adversarially selected. The tags remain fixed and a common policy maximizes one worst-case expected reward. Their explicit reduction to a one-sided POSG and their finite-horizon and approximate solution methods are direct prior theory for part of the new fixed-world model. A posterior involving the hidden environment is therefore established technique. A claim expressed through several per-world affine constraints, typed pre-activation test errors, source-atomic lawful loops and genuine event readout needs the exact stated extension and comparison. [Bovy et al., 2025, Theorem 1 and §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.23744v1) · [Proceedings record](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/e568da4c4cce48b5de30e81473ef889c-Abstract-Conference.html)

Isom, Meyn and Braatz encode constraints on expected secondary costs in POMDPs and explicitly study a time-to-detection objective with bounded false alarm. Their update operates on objective and constraint values attached to policy vectors. This is a close mathematical predecessor for joint event and effort optimization, even without a source-semantic interpretation. The ordinary finite belief-state and occupancy approach already explains linear optimization on the expanded sufficient-state space. A polynomial bound in a source-defined subclass requires a separately checked argument about its reachable rational beliefs. [Isom, Meyn and Braatz, 2008, pp. 291–296](https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/2008/AAAI08-046.pdf)

Reiter's diagnosis theorem relates minimal diagnoses to hitting all conflict sets. An infeasibility group of completions can be treated as a conflict set, so conflict extraction and the Boolean hitting step have close established analogues. The new PC-X question is which conflicts are physically realized and which legitimate observed read rules remove enough whole-world policy constraints. Removing an opposite-label world alone can leave a common useful policy infeasible. [Reiter, 1987](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(87)90062-2)

The classical sequential probability ratio test gives exact optimality under its specified simple hypotheses and boundary errors. Additional work is required for irregular atoms, randomization between discrete boundary levels, optional probes with unequal price and erasure, and an operational proof that retained messages are no more informative on a least-favorable strategic pair. Physical deployment transport uses a separate guarded-event argument. [Wald and Wolfowitz, 1948](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177730197)

The false-issuance-versus-conditional-reliability distinction is an instance of selection changing the inferential target. Conditional post-selection inference constructions use additional models or conditions. An unconditional bound from simultaneous truth inclusion leaves room for all failure mass to concentrate on issuance. [Fithian, Sun and Taylor, 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.2597)
