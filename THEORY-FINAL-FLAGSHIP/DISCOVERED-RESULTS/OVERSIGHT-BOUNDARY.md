## Upper bound for a richer admitted menu

A legitimate exact non-disturbing identity menu executable in one sequence gives full identity at its total price. There is an analogous fixed-cap statistical limit directly from the conditional-interval model in M04.3. Let \(m\) trusted binary probes be jointly legal for \(n\) uses each, with price \(c_i\) per use and with actual conditional means always in verified intervals \(J_{wi}\) for fixed operational identity \(w\), regardless of its admitted retained memory. Suppose every distinct pair of identities has some \(i\) with \(\operatorname{dist}(J_{wi},J_{w'i})\ge \gamma>0\). Allowed fields and possible effects here are the trusted sequential experiment itself. For \(\delta>0\), choose
\[
n=\left\lceil 8\gamma^{-2}\log(2m/\delta)\right\rceil
\]
and read each declared probe \(n\) times. Conditional Hoeffding, applied to the bounded martingale differences about their predictable means, puts its sample average within \(\gamma/4\) of its realized average mean except with probability \(2\exp(-n\gamma^2/8)\). The realized average mean lies in \(J_{wi}\). Simultaneously for all probes, except on probability \(\delta\), the actual identity survives the distance-\(\gamma/4\) test against all the intervals. Two distinct identities cannot both survive because their separating intervals have distance at least \(\gamma\), while passing the same probe would give distance at most \(\gamma/2\). Thus the rule returns the full identity with failure probability, including abstention, at most \(\delta\) and hard paid cap \(n\sum_i c_i\). No union over the number of possible identities is required. [Accepted conditional interval audit](https://github.com/Perceptive-Closure/PC-X/blob/07e91bec6f906eb460f025255e20e8e470adf3f2/THEORY-GENERALIZATION/WORKSTREAMS/M04/PROOF.md)

With inverse gap, menu length, total price and \(\log(1/\delta)\) polynomial in representation length, this rules out an exponential minimum **acquisition price** for identity on that menu. It gives no polynomial-time identity decoder if the catalog itself is succinct. Repetitions and every phase must remain lawful, and setup, source verification and useful work remain separate costs. No claim holds if the apparent gap disappears under composite strategic responses or the evidence does not identify operationally distinct completions.

For example, adding legitimate authoritative reads of each hidden-key bit and the gateway to T07.K would make noiseless identity cost at most \(n+1\) at unit price. If such fields have the same fixed known bit-flip law as the point interface, each bit can be resolved with a bounded sample allocation and the above bound. That addition changes the legitimate observation interface. If the source only authorizes point reads, the existing lower bound retains its scope. An expensive trusted native call might also dominate a proposed new strategy when it can establish stronger physical guarantees; its information, objective, price and authority require an explicit matched comparison.
