Version 1. The supported optimization core is finite and rational. Stronger structural or scalar bounds must state their extra restrictions. This file specifies interfaces and quantifier order for the research strands. Individual theorems must state which source and calibration hypotheses they discharge.

## Source objects, evidence and membership

An installed world is a fixed pair \(w=(C,\nu)\) in a nonempty compatible class \(W(e)\). \(C\) gives the source program, certificate representation, evaluation/environment mechanics, initial physical law and source occurrence semantics. \(\nu\) is one admissible nonanticipating responder program for that \(C\), including any linked rules after certification. These programs may react to a phase cue or disclosure and maintain private memory. Correlations and law constraints are global restrictions on \(w\), including initial installed graph paths. Deployment uncertainty may be an explicitly listed rational world set or a separately justified linked representation. Each candidate is an entire law-producing object, not a separately chosen row at each reach.

Evidence \(E\) consists of admitted fact observations with timing and provenance. Representation \(R\) is the extensional certificate relation or actual source projection of interest. Authority \(A\) is the physical set of legitimate literal occurrences, with all extensional effects on \(E,R,A\) checked before applying any proposed freeze. An occurrence is removed when any of its positive-support successors violates the declared freeze, even if that branch is not selected later. Scorer-visible fields have no special authority over event truth. Distinguish exact realized compatibility, concrete model witnesses, a checked outer cover, statistical confidence about calibrated rows, and unknown membership. Emptiness implies an inconsistency with the declared evidence model. An outer countermodel can refute a robust certificate in that cover, while physical impossibility needs realizability or an exact representation.

## Time and information

The finite horizon is expanded into a typed history tree with clock, physical/resource state, phase, current meter, legitimate command, private views, genuine job events, and terminal labels. Include hidden carry and violation-so-far or another exact event automaton when necessary. Policy decisions include legal queries, operational commands, a verdict \(d\in\{+,-,\bot\}\), and a legal phase transition or stop. Treat \(d\) as a physical occurrence whose publication timing and information recipients are declared. An audit can be genuinely external or a useful work phase. A query that disturbs the installation must be modeled as a physical transition and charged under the relevant objectives.

Let \(h\) be the controller's complete lawful decision record, including its own past actions and every retained observation it is allowed to use. It can include a legitimately private observed challenge. The responder receives a separate record \(j\), including only the actual delivered signals and its own actions. Chance uses conditional row \(K_w\) after the legitimate moves and extensional effects. All positive successors are explicitly represented. A callable command at \(h\) is the same executable source invocation on every concrete branch consistent with \(h\) and the candidate scope, with all applicable authorizations, hard budgets and freeze constraints. Different literals with a common abstract name do not implement an action. An authorized failed or denied call is an action only when explicitly included, with its actual effect. The default record excludes hidden state, future chance and the other role's private random draws.

A randomized controller uses an independent seed or fresh draws conditional on its information. \(w\) is fixed before those private draws. Known audit design and already disclosed draws can affect \(\nu\) through \(j\). The same chosen \(\nu\) persists into deployment when required by \(C\); genuinely unlinked continuations must also be included if \(C\) allows them. If a theorem ranges over private mixtures of pure responders, the initial law, target label and mixture semantics must be explicit. Convexifying observations cannot authorize switching worlds or reassigning a fixed target.

## Actual targets and governed guarantees

Predeclare an intrinsic \(Y(w)\in\{+,-\}\) that is invariant across the later audit and choices of the certificate rule. This can be the declared fixed-policy score under a prescribed deployment protocol, or a known-world existence test for a specified start, authority, horizons and deployment responders. A target quantified over continuations must use the same allowed continuation class in all counterfactuals. An audit policy does not redefine \(Y\). For a purported known-mode service property, distinguish \(\forall w\,\exists\pi_w\) from \(\exists\pi\,\forall w\) under one common current information interface. A positive label cell may have no joint useful/safe policy.

\(V\) is a genuine actual violation indicator. \(G\in[0,1]\) is actual useful reward or completion. A scope flag designates whether audit/work violations count in \(V\); all such violations count when included, even if the later deployment is safe. Use explicit \(I\) for the event of positive issuance or activation and reserve \(\delta\) for identification/calibration error and \(\varepsilon\) for physical risk. A theorem must declare the precise quantities e.g. whole-attempt \(\Pr_w^\pi(V)\), selective \(\Pr_w^\pi(V\cap I)\le\varepsilon\Pr_w^\pi(I)\), unconditional or selective utility, \(\Pr_w^\pi(d\ne Y(w))\) with abstention charged as stated, or false positive issuance. If conditional interpretation is desired, enforce a positive issuance floor on its declared worlds. Multiplying by \(\Pr(I)\) is only a linear inequality at zero mass. Explicit useful-positive coverage prohibits a permanently refusing witness.

Quantify \(\exists\) one lawful policy before \(\forall w\), then integrate actual fresh experiment/operational randomness and private controller draws under that fixed world. Worst expected paid cost and worst supported path budget are different objectives. Store separate anchor verification, source compilation, statistical sampling, external and in-task payment, work, clock, runtime and memory quantities. Independent statistical calibration may use a stated fixed batch or stopping-valid bound. A confidence event about the actual model cannot supply a probability of unverified semantic misspecification. Distinguish wrong-or-abstain from false issuance and absolute deployment bounds from bounds conditional on selection.
