# Counterexample and failure ledger

The rightmost column identifies the result or missing premise exposed by each witness. A witness in an exact finite model has the stated mathematical effect. A candidate in a sound outer cover needs realizability before it is a physical impossibility.

| Failure mode | Witness or sharp consequence | Scope addressed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gap and threshold | Same-report negative laws converge in all diagnostic means to a positive threshold law. Every finite prefix is arbitrarily close. Uniform forced accuracy below \(1/2\) error is impossible even if only the positive limit is required to stop almost surely. | P3 finite confidence and stopping |
| Repeated fixed-time tests and hidden multiplicity | A declared null whose successive independent false-rejection events each have chance \(\delta\) has a false discovery by \(n\) tests with chance \(1-(1-\delta)^n\). Stopping on the first such result invalidates the single-test rate. | P3 requires simultaneous or stopping-valid calibration |
| Conditioning on issuance | Put all bad calibration mass inside a rare issuance event with equal probability. Conditional failure is one despite a small unconditional false-issuance budget. | P3/U.3 exposure term |
| Confusing error units | At one lawful label-correct positive source, every useful run still has unavoidable chance of a real violation. At a poor calibration, an otherwise safe policy could be falsely classified without any physical violation. | P4/U.4 and U.3 combine different events |
| Exactness and attainment | An infinite menu offers only resolving commands of prices \(1+1/n\), with one command required. Every policy's price, or finite expectation under any mixture, is strictly above 1, but the infimum is 1. | Finite rational attainment of P1/A.3 finite/U.1 cannot be asserted for an infinite lawful menu |
| Opposite full laws | Remove the protected dry score or replace the physical threshold only in live deployment. All legal pre-verdict records can match while live risk is \(1/4\) versus \(3/4\). | A.2/U.4 transport and information boundary |
| Intersecting composite laws | In a one-bit complete-record experiment the positive alternatives are laws concentrated at 0 and at 1. A negative is the fair bit. No simple opposite pair agrees, but positive mixtures contain the negative law. Any forced test has worst error at least \(1/2\). | Accepted M04.2 remains distinct from A matched simple pairs |
| Pairwise distinct but shrinking | Two opposing conditional means can be different at every \(n\), yet approach one another. Pairwise separation without a uniform margin gives no uniform finite accuracy cap. | P3/A.2 finite class and explicit observability conditions |
| Stateful strategy after certification | A higher permitted post-certification proposal can change risk even when all protected score laws are identical. A single probe-threshold type can have true direct risks \(1/4\) and \(5/8\). | A.4 restricted deployment proposal and source event premises |
| Illegally revealed randomness | If the responder sees its own invocation's fresh \(Z\) before choosing a set of size one, it can select \(\{Z\}\). The incidence fraction \(1/L\) then fails as a hazard probability. | A.1 before-draw order |
| Inconsistent row splicing | The only installed hazard vectors are \((q,0)\) and \((0,q)\). A product relaxation adds \((q,q)\) and predicts risk \(2q-q^2>q\). | P1/U.1 preserve fixed global worlds |
| Same target, different useful policies | Three intrinsically positive types have commands with bad risk \(9/10\) for the matching type and \(1/10\) otherwise. Every pair permits useful risk \(\le17/50\), while the triple would require three choice probabilities each \(\le3/10\), which is impossible. | P2 higher-order blocking groups and U.1 verdict versus realizability |
| Randomization can be necessary | In P4 the actual first-job record branches with \(1/3,2/3\). The opposite risk order for the two second-stage routes needs an aggregate half choice. Deterministic receipt policies miss it and the best robust deterministic risk is \(7/30>1/5\). | P1/U.1 general chance policies keep legal randomization |
| Unsafe or disturbed information acquisition | The first work event in P4 already carries true violation probability \(1/20\). Dropping it changes the whole-attempt risk. A changed calibration source can leave scores identical with wrong physical bounds. | P3/U.3 require correct counted risk and transport |
| Omitted raw support | A legal recorded row may have a positive-probability hazardous successor sharing its visible letter, or touching \(R\) under the same fast literal. Omitting it changes both the numeric bound and possibly whether the entire atom is callable. | P1/U.1 complete raw support and X16 LONG test |
| Matched abstract names with no common command | Route L and route R require disjoint literal invocations until usefully measured. Renaming them cannot create a shared action. | U.2 guard state and X16 split-call control |
| Free initial revelation | If the true type is made visible at initialization, the routed example can achieve zero paid classification cost. The original lower bound applies to the original complete retained record only. | A.2/U.4 scope and X16 initial guard |
| Missing useful receipt or hidden carried state | Suppressing the intake receipt removes the shared finish, even though each known route works. A remembered authorization token or carried tariff omitted from the state also invalidates compact recurrence. | U.2/U.4 and corresponding X16 controls |
| Canceled finalization | Actual reached cut risk \(3/4\) at probability \(1/2\) with otherwise no violation gives direct protocol risk \(3/8\). Prematurely canceled positive declarations also leave useful completion short. | A.1 exact one-finalizer and full-event correction |
| Permanently refusing | Its low actual risk is compatible with zero completed useful tasks. The declared positive utility and decision guarantees exclude it. | P4/A.1/U.4 non-vacuity |
| Inadequate belief | Distinct numerical observation probabilities can leave identical support and identical world-conditional physical point states. Any resulting merged verdict loses minimax accuracy \(1/4\) and falls to at least \(1/2\) on the one-bit pair. | U.2 requires joint likelihood, not support alone |
| Cost/domain substitution | U.4 at error \(1/3\) costs \(2/3\) in worst expectation and 1 on the worst supported path. X16 online work acquisition and pure predeployment certification obey distinct timers. | Keep objectives and matched alternatives separate |
| Hidden representation cost | A polynomial-time exact LP after unfolding hidden programs and histories may consume exponential input. U.2's polynomial graph needs fixed latent and likelihood complexity. | No generic polynomial claim |
