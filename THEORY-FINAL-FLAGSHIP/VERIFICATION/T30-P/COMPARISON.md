## Further statistical obstruction from hostile review

Affirmed under the matched pre-verdict interface. Suppose p_0 is an admitted positive threshold world and p_n are admitted negative worlds with its exact full report tuple and diagnostic mean vectors converging to the mean vector of p_0. Assume uniform wrong-or-abstain error at most δ<1/2 and almost-sure stopping under p_0. Let A_H be positive issuance by a deterministic audit cap H. One may use a cap on total moves and thereby use at most H diagnostic draws. Under p_0 the events A_H increase to eventual positive issuance, whose probability is at least 1−δ. Pick finite H with P_{p_0}(A_H)>δ. The complete finite-record TV estimate applies to that truncated process and implies P_{p_n}(A_H)→P_{p_0}(A_H) as n→∞. For all sufficiently large n that probability exceeds δ, contradicting the negative world's entire-run wrong-issuance bound. No uniform bound on stopping time or expected sample count is required for this argument. It uses a positive limit law included in the actual class; the same reasoning works for an admitted strictly positive limit label with the same moment accumulation.

For example, a one-parameter Bernoulli diagnostic of mean p with exact report constant, intrinsic threshold 1/2, p_0=1/2 and p_n=1/2+1/n for n≥3 has this obstruction. A weaker reporting rule may abstain without counting it as failure, in which case the conclusion about uniform correct issuance cannot be inferred. An outer-cover limit without a physical realization supplies only a represented impossibility. This is an additional boundary rather than a replacement for the sealed finite-cap result. P1–P4 are supported with the stated exact source and sampling scope.
