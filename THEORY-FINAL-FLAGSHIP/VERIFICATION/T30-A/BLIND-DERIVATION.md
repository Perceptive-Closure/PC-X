## A.1. Actual linked deployment risk

Fix a world, type and genuinely reached pre-draw cut, including warm-start state and the responder's proposal. The protected word U is fresh uniform conditional on that entire cut. The events Fθ(U)=1 and {Fθ(U)=0, Z∈D_{x,a}} are disjoint. Conditional independence gives respective masses pθ and (1−pθ)|D_{x,a}|/L. This calculation conditions after the responder sees the issuance and activation cues and selects a. It permits lawfully carried state and arbitrary correlations among I, state and proposal. Averaging over the cuts reached from a positive-mass controller activation record gives [ℓθ,uθ]. The reference protocol is another such mixture from the registered start and its respective actual cues. A realizable program attaining an extremizing fraction on almost every reached cut supplies the corresponding endpoint in that protocol. An isolated extremal cut does not prove this.

With each type wholly on one side of ε, the intrinsic reference target is determined by its interval. For the positive side E[1_I V_D]≤uθ P(I)≤εP(I). Wrong or absent declarations have probability ≤δ, so P(I)≥1−δ. Since physical completion equals 1−V_D on activation and both quantities vanish otherwise, E G_D=P(I)−P(V_D∩I)≥(1−ε)(1−δ). For the negative side positive issuance is a wrong declaration, which bounds P(I) and P(V_D∩I) by δ. Audit safety and complete coverage of the deployment violation by V_D give max(ε,δ) for the whole attempt. Equality at ε is positive. The reference and linked responder distributions can differ throughout.

## A.2. Receipt fibers and full-record impossibility

Choose a probe i with r_i>0 and set r=r_i. Conditional on every eligible prefix and the selection of i, the authenticated receipt has probabilities rpθ, r(1−pθ), 1−r. They are fixed even conditional on the full pre-draw state and then by averaging on the audit record. Repeating the same legitimate probe n times produces independent Bernoulli indicators of receipt 1 with mean rpθ, by the product rule. If the candidate labels are all equal, report their constant value. Otherwise let Δ>0 be the smallest separation between distinct finite catalog means. Classify by the closest catalog mean's source-checked label. A wrong declaration implies an estimation error of at least Δ/2. Hoeffding gives a uniform bound 2 exp(−nΔ²/2), and the verified repeatable interface must actually support the chosen finite schedule and activation. If no probe reveals, the homogeneous single fiber has a constant decision.

For a matched opposite pair in a heterogeneous fiber, couple their initial states as given and share the auditor's randomization. At a shared record the controller invokes the same lawful command or stops. The same fiber gives identical protected-receipt marginals for every probe and the ordinary dummy. Sample the same receipt, use the specified remainder coupling, and retain an equal new complete record and the paired relation. Induct through the audit, with padding for earlier stops. Equality of the full stopped audit laws includes the declaration, even with randomization. Two opposite labels tested on a common law have correct-declaration probabilities that sum to at most one, so one has failure at least 1/2. A crossing interval could reflect unreachable extremizing proposals, an outer-cover mode, or cuts that cannot coexist in one admissible completion. ## A.3. Likelihood process before activation

For the lower bounds, use the stated realizable ordinary-matched pair with equal prior. Let H=1 mean the negative type p=q, and H=−1 the positive type p=1−q. The ratio of negative to positive complete record laws at a reached finite prefix is λ^s. To see this independently of the auxiliary records, construct two auxiliary paired processes. Each initializes its hidden pair from the supplied coupling, uses the same audit seed law, and follows the same conditional pair remainder kernel after the selected receipt. The first process samples the receipt from the negative-world row, the second from the positive-world row. Their corresponding physical marginals are the exact native executions because each paired remainder kernel has the required native conditional projection. No switch of responder program is made; the pair includes its actual state/history. For a positive paired history the only factors in the likelihood ratio that vary between the two processes are λ for each 1, λ⁻¹ for each 0, and 1 for erasure or dummy. The product λ^s depends on the audited record alone. Integrating over coupled hidden histories and auditor randomization therefore preserves this density on the complete record. The same checked identity holds on the retained joint record with the seed, without requiring atom probabilities for it, and on any finite stopped record.

## A.3. Finite hard cap

## A.3. Expected cost

In the separate repeatable class let τ be a.s. finite, N its count of revealed receipts, and S its terminal excess of 1 over 0. Put k=min_{i:r_i>0} c_i/r_i. At each selected probe conditional expected reveals are r_i, and cost c_i≥kr_i; ordinary costs are nonnegative. Summing gives E paid cost≥k E N, where the expectation is for the equal-prior matched pair. Infinite cost is irrelevant to a lower bound. If the cost is finite, E N and E|S| are finite.

In a world of sign H, each signed receipt increment has expectation H μ times the reveal probability conditional on the selection and full pre-draw record; ordinary moves and erasures contribute zero. The decision to continue precedes the next draw. Sum the conditional expectations, justified by the expected sum of absolute increments E N, to get E[H S]=μ E N. The stopped likelihood identity follows by partition on the finite stop time. Thus E[H | stopped record]=2π_S−1 and

    μ E N = E[ |S| (1−2e_|S|) ].

Any feasible forced or abstention-counting decision has average class failure at least E e_|S|, so E e_|S|≤δ. To minimize the right side use the geometry of the attainable posterior errors. Let a=log λ>0, d=x(1−x), L=log((1−x)/x), and f(x)=(1−2x)L/a on 0<x≤1/2. Then f(e_j)=j(1−2e_j), a f'=−2L−(1−2x)/d<0 in the interior, and a f''=4/d+(1−2x)²/d²=1/d²>0. The line through (e_{m+1},f(e_{m+1})) and (e_m,f(e_m)) has negative slope and lies below f at all lattice error values. Indeed nothing from the lattice lies strictly inside that interval, and convexity bounds the line on either extension. Taking expectations and using E e_|S|≤δ gives a lower bound of the value of that line at δ. This is precisely the numerator interpolating neighboring boundaries in the statement. Worst expected cost bounds the pair's average from above, so the same lower bound applies to the desired objective after multiplying by k/μ.

Achieve it by using a probe that realizes k and randomly choosing the two specified boundaries at initialization. Boundary zero is a fair guess. At any j>0 the rule stops as soon as |s|=j. A fresh block of 2j consecutive probes has a uniformly positive conditional probability of all revealed 1s, which would force absorption from any remaining state. This proves a geometric tail and finite expected paid cost. Posterior error is exactly e_j at every stopping history; reflection gives equal class errors and expected counts. The drift identity then gives paid cost exactly k j(1−2e_j)/μ, since only the single selected probe is used. Mixing adjacent boundaries yields the stated optimum and equality of error δ in both types, with the same receipt-law guarantee in all admitted worlds. A fair declaration costs zero when δ≥1/2. If δ=0, at each a.s. finite stopping record the likelihood odds are finite and positive and e_|S|>0, so zero error is unattainable even with infinite expected cost. A lawful unbounded sampling reserve and positive-work type-specific activation remain part of the underlying model.
