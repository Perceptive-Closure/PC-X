## Guarded source class

Use finite rational source tables or an independently checked finite expansion. A fixed installed world \(w=(C,\nu)\) has a hidden source type \(\theta=\theta(C)\) in a finite catalog \(\Theta\). Its source program, physical installation and law, and one fixed admissible responder rule \(\nu\) persist from audit through activation. The respondent has its actual private view, own past actions, memory and every delivered signal, including evaluation and activation cues and the public positive verdict. It can react to all of them. The controller has its lawful record, including an authenticated receipt when one is delivered to it privately. Responder choices on any invocation precede its trusted draws. Neither participant can use the other's undisclosed draws. A world is fixed before any private controller randomization. The allowed worlds can be correlated subtypes of the catalog with history-dependent effects. A catalog of allowable worlds or a proved outer cover has to include the actual installation. A physical impossibility witness needs realizable worlds.

The following are checks on actual reachable source occurrences. These can be verified on closed sets of source states that retain the guard-relevant effects of private history, using concrete support traversal or an independently justified inductive invariant. Guard checks cover the stipulated reference start as well as a start carried from each allowable audit prefix. The closure check follows every positive-support successor of every declared action and responder move. It checks common executable literals, physical work, clock and cost accounting, reserve of lawful activation, and all extensional effects on the relevant evidence, representation and authority under the declared freeze. Any mandatory preamble, if present, is fixed, accounted for separately and finishes before the counted optional moves. Every catalog probe remains common callable at every such decision record. The declared activation protocol invokes common callable commands determined by the actual lawful record. When there is more than one hidden physical route, the actual lawful record must suffice to choose a common literal at every decision. We consider policies for which a positive verdict immediately triggers this protocol and a negative verdict or abstention finishes without deployment. The verdict is issued before the responder chooses its deployment proposal. Terminal audit error counts a wrong label or abstention. Audit moves, including any useful work, are safe for the declared actual violation predicate.

The guard has the following checked primitives.

1. A protected binding identifies an unmodified source register with value \(\theta\) and the same finite table \(F_\theta:[M]\to\{0,1\}\) in the probe and deployment instructions. The binding, full table semantics and invariant are checked even at reachable warm starts. Put \(p_\theta=|F_\theta^{-1}(1)|/M\). At each selected protected invocation the guard obtains a fresh uniform word \(U\in[M]\), conditionally on the entire source history and proposal through the pre-draw cut. Unprivileged responder actions, the selected public phase and permitted carry can affect the rest of the invocation. They do not modify the bound type, the word law or the prescribed dataflow. The controller does not get the type by reading the binding proof.
2. The protected probe catalog is finite, \(Q\). Probe \(i\) charges a fixed paid amount \(c_i>0\), consumes one of the optional audit moves and may perform actual useful work. An independent fresh reveal bit of known mean \(r_i\in[0,1]\) is drawn under the guard at the cut. The actual instrument's authenticated receipt \(Y_i\) is \(F_\theta(U)\) when it reveals and the erasure symbol \(*\) otherwise. The protected receipt is the controller's actual observation. Optional ordinary audit moves have nonnegative paid costs and give no receipt. They do not change the fixed menu, its prices or available activation. Other lawful observations are allowed and may be strategic. For an upper bound these observations need not be informative.
3. The responder can select a permitted deployment proposal \(a\) after seeing the verdict, its private carried view and any other declared inputs, and before fresh guard draws. The finalizer draws \(U\) and an independent fresh uniform word \(Z\in[L]\) at that cut. There is a verified map from its supported *physical* finalize outcomes to actual violation and completion. The actual violation bit of this work is
   \[
   V_D=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
   F_\theta(U)=1\ \text{or}\ [F_\theta(U)=0\text{ and }Z\in D_{x,a}],
   \]
   where \(x\) is the concrete pre-draw physical state and \(D_{x,a}\subseteq[L]\) is a checked table entry. The job supplies actual completion reward \(G_D=1-V_D\) when invoked. No deployment gives \(V_D=G_D=0\). In particular, the reported evaluation score is unnecessary for this event check. Fixed hard operational constraints are verified independently of the count of optional probes, up to their declared cap when one is imposed. The extrema \(\rho_\theta^-,\rho_\theta^+\) are lower and upper bounds obtained by examining the fractions \(|D_{x,a}|/L\) for all declared reachable reference and linked deployment cuts for that type, with nonempty proposal support. Using the corresponding minimum and maximum gives exact local extrema on that inspected set.

Fix a rational \(0\le\varepsilon<1\). The intrinsic target \(T(w)\) is \(+\) iff the *actual violation probability* of the prescribed direct guarded deployment protocol in \(w\) is at most \(\varepsilon\), measured from its registered initial law, including its own prescribed preamble and delivered activation cue. This reference protocol is fixed independently of the audit and does not use the audit terminal declaration. Its responder is the same fixed program with the reference protocol's actual initial view. The audited operational start instead uses the actual linked responder and physical state carried by the audit, with physical issuance and activation. Let \(I\) denote positive issuance and activation in that attempt.

## A.1 · Guard transport and activation

In every admitted world, on every reached linked deployment pre-draw cut, conditional violation probability is \(p_\theta+(1-p_\theta)|D_{x,a}|/L\). Conditional on any positive-mass activation history at the controller, and in the direct guarded deployment protocol, violation probability is in
\[
[\ell_\theta,u_\theta]=
[p_\theta+(1-p_\theta)\rho_\theta^-,\ p_\theta+(1-p_\theta)\rho_\theta^+].
\]
This conclusion is uniform over all admissible linked responder histories and all lawful controller policies covered by the reach check. For a fixed type suppose realizable linked programs achieve each extremizing fraction almost surely in the respective protocol of interest. Then the corresponding endpoint is sharp in that protocol over these programs. Extremizing proposals merely observed at some cuts need not give an attained global endpoint.

If every type obeys \(u_\theta\le\varepsilon\) or \(\ell_\theta>\varepsilon\), the intrinsic target is determined by those alternatives for every admissible responder. Once a lawful test has uniform wrong-or-abstain probability at most \(\delta\), its audit and guarded deployment satisfy
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
T(w)=+: &\Pr_w(V_D\cap I)\le\varepsilon\Pr_w(I),\
             \Pr_w(I)\ge1-\delta,\
             \mathbb E_wG_D\ge(1-\varepsilon)(1-\delta);\\
T(w)=-: &\Pr_w(I)\le\delta,\
             \Pr_w(V_D\cap I)\le\delta .
\end{array}
\]
The unconditional whole-attempt violation probability is at most \(\max\{\varepsilon,\delta\}\). On a world of either target, there is no violation during the declared audit. Endpoint equality is on the \(+\) side. In particular the bounds on the cuts allow useful positive service while the exact responder and the likelihood of its ordinary evaluation scores remain unidentified.

## A.2 · Receipt fibers and a full-law obstruction

A pair of realizable worlds is *matched on ordinary records* if the following finite pair certificate exists. It includes a coupling of their full initial states with equal lawful controller initialization records, and a relation on paired pre-activation states containing its support. At every such pair and each lawful common literal, the trusted receipt in world \(w\) has probabilities \(r_i p_\theta,r_i(1-p_\theta),1-r_i\) for \(1,0,*\) on a probe, with a single dummy receipt on an ordinary move. After each receipt with positive probability in both worlds, there is a coupling of the two actual conditional remainder-and-next-state rows. This coupling has equal other lawful observations, clocks and paid costs and maps to the pair relation. The conditional row includes the recorded instrument, physical effects relevant to the audit, and each world's fixed \(\nu\) at its actual view. All record fields available to the controller, including later release of prior measurements, must meet the check. Receipt equality is required of the coupling only after conditioning on its shared receipt value. The receipt marginal is permitted to differ between the two worlds. Finite states may encode sufficient private memories; if they do not, perform the check on actual histories. The controller is allowed free private randomization. Pair checks are applicable only where all lawful designs use the stated audit interface and a finite proof covers all their reached pre-activation states.

Define a receipt fiber by \(p_\theta\) if a probe with \(r_i>0\) exists, and put all worlds in one fiber if none does. Suppose the targets associated with each fiber are prescribed from the actually admitted worlds. For the positive implication suppose these targets are accessible from a finite source calculation, for example by A.1 on each type. For that implication the designer may select the finite horizon, and the source checks must allow repeatability up to the selected finite sample cap without violating other hard constraints. For the converse suppose each fiber containing both targets includes two such realizable worlds of opposite target matched on ordinary records. Then, for any fixed \(0<\delta<1/2\), some lawful finite hard-cap audit attains uniform wrong-or-abstain probability at most \(\delta\) if and only if every fiber is homogeneous. Two opposite-target worlds in one fiber with the stated pair certificate have equal full audit record laws for every lawful design, including randomized adaptive designs. A finite compatible source calculation that finds such a pair distinguishes a genuine obstruction from a failed conservative bound. An interval with \(\ell_\theta\le\varepsilon<u_\theta\) by itself supplies no physical counterexample. If both endpoints are realized for the direct reference by opposite-target worlds matched on ordinary records, it supplies such an obstruction within that type.

## A.3 · Exact costs in the two-type symmetric class

Assume both types are realized and satisfy A.1 with type \(+\) having \(p_\theta=1-q\) and type \(-\) having \(p_\theta=q\), for a rational \(1/2<q<1\). Assume a realizable pair of opposite targets matched on ordinary records as in A.2. The initial protected record is empty, and each catalog move has fixed price and availability as stated. Audit designs may use the whole lawful record, private randomization, auxiliary observations and optional ordinary moves. Cost is cumulative paid optional audit cost. Mandatory preamble and deployment work, setup, time and verification costs are separate. For the finite hard-budget result each optional audit move consumes one move. Put \(\lambda=q/(1-q)\), \(\mu=2q-1\), \(e_j=(1+\lambda^j)^{-1}\) for integers \(j\ge0\), and \(s=\#\{1\}-\#\{0\}\) in authenticated receipts, ignoring erasures.

**Hard cap.** At an optional paid cap \(b\ge0\) and move cap \(h\ge0\), the exact minimax wrong-or-abstain probability is \(B(b,h,0)\). Define \(B(b,0,s)=e_{|s|}\) and, for \(h>0\), set
\[
\begin{split}
 B(b,h,s)=\min\Bigl\{e_{|s|},\
 \min_{i:\ c_i\le b}\big[ &(1-r_i)B(b-c_i,h-1,s)\\
   +r_i\big(\bar p_s B(b-c_i,h-1,s+1)
             +(1-\bar p_s) B(b-c_i,h-1,s-1)\big)\big]\Bigr\},
\end{split}
\]
where \(\pi_s=\lambda^s/(1+\lambda^s)\), \(\bar p_s=\pi_s q+(1-\pi_s)(1-q)\), and an empty inner minimum is \(+\infty\). The value is attained by a lawful test using authenticated receipts and a fair choice of verdict at a tie when it stops. With rational input the recurrence and choice comparisons are exact. At a given \(h,b\), the two-way error requirement \(\delta\) is feasible exactly when \(B(b,h,0)\le\delta\). Enumerating attainable charges up to \(h\) computes the exact minimum hard cap at that horizon, with infinity when none meets it. If all probes have the same price \(c>0\) and \(r_*=\max_i r_i\), set \(n=\min\{h,\lfloor b/c\rfloor\}\). The optimum also equals
\[
\mathbb E_{J\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,r_*)}
\left[\Pr(\operatorname{Bin}(J,q)<J/2)+\tfrac12\Pr(\operatorname{Bin}(J,q)=J/2)\right].
\]
The inner expression takes value \(1/2\) at \(J=0\). With no informative probe the optimum is \(1/2\).

**Expected cost.** In a separate model extend the checked class and the matched pair consistently to unbounded optional moves, with the same clocks, prices, phase linkage and no finite move, paid or work cap that bars continued sampling. Require authorization at every reached optional time and almost sure stopping in every admitted world. At least one probe has \(r_i>0\). For \(0<\delta<1/2\), the exact minimum worst expected paid cost is
\[
 \min_{i:r_i>0}\frac{c_i}{r_i}\ \cdot\
 \frac{t\,m(1-2e_m)+(1-t)(m+1)(1-2e_{m+1})}{\mu},
 \qquad
 t=\frac{\delta-e_{m+1}}{e_m-e_{m+1}},
 \quad e_{m+1}\le\delta\le e_m.
\]
Here \(m\ge0\), with either adjacent representation allowed at a shared endpoint. The test selects boundary \(m\) with probability \(t\), otherwise \(m+1\), and always uses a probe minimizing \(c_i/r_i\). With chosen boundary \(j>0\), it stops when \(|s|=j\) and reports \(-\) for positive \(s\), \(+\) for negative \(s\). Boundary \(0\) is the fair-guess rule. The cost and error of this test are the same in both types and its error is exactly \(\delta\). Each activated deployment uses the protocol of A.1. Availability and safety in this extension are assumptions on the continued actual source. The optimum is an expected-cost assertion for that extension. A pathwise hard-budget claim uses the separate finite theorem. For \(\delta\ge1/2\), zero optional cost suffices. At \(\delta=0\), almost surely finite stopping cannot give a correct decision in both types.

## A.4 · Finite fixture and controls

Consider \(M=L=4\), two sealed types with \(|F_+^{-1}(1)|=1\) and \(|F_-^{-1}(1)|=3\), and proposals \(a=0,1\) with \(|D_{x,a}|=a\) on all reachable cuts. The reference and linked guarded jobs are authorized on a common literal for the declared class. Permit the responder to retain bits from evaluator-visible traffic, to make its ordinary reports using its private state and evaluation cue, and to change its final proposal upon the specific positive issuance and deployment cue. Audit producers can emit the same nuisance records in both types using identical audit-phase logic while still choosing different moves in deployment. The protected gate has probe choices \((c_i,r_i)=(1,1/2),(3,1)\), with audit moves authorized independently of type and safe. Each probe completes an independently useful item with an authenticated receipt. Use \(\varepsilon=1/2\). This fixture admits frozen linked worlds with exactly the same ordinary pre-activation record laws for every design that suppresses the protected receipts. Both target signs occur. Across either probe's authenticated record, opposite laws overlap for every finite number of optional moves. With receipts kept, A.3 applies to the protected interface and A.1 bounds actual linked deployment violation in the positive type by \(7/16\) and guarantees \(\mathbb E_wG_D\ge (9/16)(1-\delta)\) there. In its repeatable expected-cost extension the minimum worst expected paid cost at \(\delta=1/10\) is \(32/5\); in the finite class the minimum hard paid budget at \(h=2,\ \delta=1/10\) is infeasible.

There is also a type-fixed opposite-target completion pair if the model permits a wider deployment proposal with \(|D_{x,a}|=2\) in type \(+\), fixes one continuation to always choose \(a=0\) and the other to always choose this wider proposal in reference deployment, and matches their initial and audit semantics including the protected receipts. In that modified class the intrinsic risks are \(1/4\) and \(5/8\) respectively for those worlds, and no authorized audit attains uniform failure below \(1/2\). This pair changes the actual continuation class and preserves the probe mechanics.
