## Primary literature

A.3 embeds that test after stripping erasures with exact guarded signal mechanics. Its proof uses a different conditional drift calculation to account for adaptive paid choices and auxiliary controlled records and gives exact interpolation at arbitrary admissible δ. The underlying symmetric two-boundary rule follows the classical method. [Wald and Wolfowitz original paper](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177730197)

[Blackwell original paper](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032)
