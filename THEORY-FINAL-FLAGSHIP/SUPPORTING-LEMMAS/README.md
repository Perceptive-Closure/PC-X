# Supporting results and proof dependencies

- Exact source-state projection preserves each compared controller's conditional row and whole trajectory. Lower row minorants instead preserve just the dominated path mass and its complementary lost-mass bound. [P1 proof](../T30-P/PROOFS.md)
- Rational robust feasibility dualization extracts a bounded-size conflicting complete-world group. Higher-order conflict is necessary for useful joint thresholds even when labels and pairwise policies agree. [P2 proof](../T30-P/PROOFS.md)
- A protected finite cut gives a sharp conditional risk expression even when the actual responder notices certification and changes its lawful proposal. A matched pair couples complete nuisance records while retaining the correct protected receipt likelihood. [A.1–A.2 proof](../T30-A/PROOFS.md)
- Bayes error and expected cost reduce to the protected likelihood ratio on the matched pair. Adjacent discrete boundary mixtures solve the stated symmetric expected-cost problem. [A.3 proof](../T30-A/PROOFS.md)
- A common policy action factor cancels from an exact posterior. Normalized occupation flows preserve every native world metric in the declared vector by weighting that world separately. [U.1–U.2 proof](../T30-U/PROOFS.md)
- Coverage errors may concentrate on reported activation. The exact unconditional decomposition adds the exposed issuance mass, with no independence claim. [U.3 proof](../T30-U/PROOFS.md)
